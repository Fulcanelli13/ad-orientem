### Source and copy decisions that matter

1. **Saint John (03)**: the Oloron [diocesan notice](https://diocese64.org/evenement/feu-de-la-saint-jean-a-oloron-sainte-marie/) is explicitly **20 June 2026**, with Mass, meal, a bonfire, song and prayer. This is a community gathering associated with **St John the Baptist**, *not* St John the Evangelist's December wine blessing, and neither a recurring date nor a Roman fire-blessing rubric.
2. **Eggs (04)**: [UNESCO decision 19.COM 7.b.14](https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/19.COM/7.b.14) documents wax-resist Ukrainian pysanka across religious communities, and [Radom museum](https://www.muzeum-radom.pl/terminy/plastyka-obrzedowa/361) documents Polish pisanki. Polish and Ukrainian remain two culturally described **subtypes of one draft decorating-action identity**. Some attributed protective uses belong to documented folklore and are not asserted as Catholic doctrine. Neither craft is the liturgical Easter-food blessing.
3. **Herbergsuche (05)**: the [2023 Großpetersdorf parish note](https://www.pfarre-grosspetersdorf.at/pfarre/1131/aktuelles/article/12277.html) actually describes the daily transfer of an image/statue among participating households, plus short Advent prayer at handover. This is **not** proof of a theatrical tradition or a universally scheduled nine-day novena.
4. **Guignolée (07)**: the [Québec heritage register](https://www.patrimoine-culturel.gouv.qc.ca/detail.do?id=234696&methode=consulter&type=bien) and [St Vincent de Paul 2026 organizer programme](https://ssvp-mtl.org/fr/guignolee) corroborate the historical Catholic charitable collection and later mixed civic participation. The current organizer has service-point-specific dates in 2026; Calendar must **not** copy them into a perpetual national schedule.
5. **Polish carollers and Hungarian plays (08, 09)**: [Polish educational source](https://zpe.gov.pl/a/polskie-obyczaje-bozonarodzeniowe-koleda-pastoralka-kolednicy-i-koledowanie/DWQhHa9xN) describes seasonal household visitors and the Herody performers; [Hungarian Catholic Lexicon](https://lexikon.katolikus.hu/B/betlehemez%C3%A9s.html) describes Nativity drama, including a separately documented [puppet variant](https://lexikon.katolikus.hu/B/b%C3%A1bt%C3%A1ncoltat%C3%B3%20betlehemes%20j%C3%A1t%C3%A9k.html). Shared Christmas drama and visiting are an indexing family, not a new generic parent or mandatory merger.
6. **Maltese L-Irxoxt (13)**: [Malta Public Service](https://publicservice.gov.mt/en/news/easter-in-gozo-a-celebration-of-faith-culture-and-community) confirms local Easter Resurrection processions, while [April 2026 reporting](https://timesofmalta.com/article/easter-sunday-mass-celebrated-traditional-processions-draws-crowds.1126486) confirms *running* in Senglea. The latter is a place-specific subtype, not a universal Easter requirement.
7. **Pentecost greenery (14)**: [Polish State Forests' account](https://www.lasy.gov.pl/pl/edukacja/blogi/blog-lesniczego/zielone-swiatki-2013-nie-zapominajmy-o-nich) attests domestic/gate greenery. The earlier **1930 archival photo-caption claim** was not reverified; remove that precise assertion from the candidate's publishable historical paragraph. This remains distinct from Corpus Christi route-flower decorations or Assumption priestly herb blessings.
8. **Inchinata (15)**: [Tivoli Diocese 2026](https://diocesitivoliepalestrina.it/festa-dellinchinata-2026/) describes an image meeting/bow on 14 August and renewal on 15 August. Legendary ancient origin is not historical proof. The event is Tivoli-specific and not an additional Mass rubric.
9. **Faradda (16)**: [Sassari municipality 2026](https://www.comune.sassari.it/it/novita/notizia/Candelieri-2026-segui-tutti-gli-aggiornamenti/) corroborates the guild procession with large decorated **wooden votive structures**, not small liturgical candles. The 2026 dates do not automatically establish later-year schedules.
10. **Frauendreißiger (17)**: [Munich archdiocese](https://web.erzbistum-muenchen.de/ueber-das-erzbistum/pressebereich/pressemitteilungen/2026-pm/gottesdienste-und-kraeutersegnungen-zu-mariae-himmelfahrt) confirms the Marian shrine period; [Maria Thalkirchen shrine](https://www.mariathalkirchen.de/index.php?id=127) itself says Pius VI granted an indulgence in **1780**. That attribution is a *source report*, not an independently examined original papal grant with verified full conditions or relevance to the 1962 calendar. **No indulgence claim is activated**.
