## What actually changed

**All 22 existing answered BAQ records and all 8 substantive sedevacantism (SDV) dossiers** have been indexed at the individual paragraph/source-reference level, without replacing their current full-length answers, traditional commentary or bilingual content.

- **238 source-bearing paragraph blocks** indexed with exact structural paths and source links: 103 BAQ and 135 SDV. All IDs resolve to entries in the original source registries. An external URL proves *registration*, not the accuracy of every proposition.
- **Seven unattributed BAQ opponents** (BAQ-06, 07, 17, 18, 19, 24 and 26) are now explicitly *editorially framed questions*, not quotations from a Muslim author, Protestant, millenarian, Catholic critic or technology theorist who is not actually named in the cited material. Their contextual biblical source links do **not** imply an external proponent made the objection.
- **BAQ-20** now uses [St Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* V.33](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103533.htm), for his historical expectation concerning the renewed creation, with [St Augustine, *City of God* XX.7](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/120120.htm) giving the alternative ecclesial reading. Historical early chiliastic opinion is not collapsed into a caricature of carnal excess or presented as an irreformable dogma. New bilingual objection and answer retain the real positions.
- **SDV-06** now cites the actual first-person French text of [Guérard des Lauriers explaining his thesis](https://www.sodalitium.eu/interview-de-monseigneur-guerard-lauriers/), rather than relying solely on a generic institutional article index and later summaries. The interview is a *reprint*; it is not asserted to be a collation of the original 1979 *Cahiers de Cassiciacum* edition.
- **SDV-01** Bellarmine II.30 and IV.6–7 pointers now go to the [complete translated Book II.30](https://novusordowatch.org/de-romano-pontifice-book2-chapter30/) and [translated Book IV.6–7](https://novusordowatch.org/de-romano-pontifice-book4-chapters6-7/). These editions are hosted by a proponent and have not been independently collated against Bellarmine's Latin; the conditional thesis and the alleged modern application are distinct.
