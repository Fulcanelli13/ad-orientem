## Verifiable and unverified boundaries

- All 18 research source claims were narrowly rechecked by the upstream source review, but **none** has independent full-article certification or source-independent witness certification. The frozen source URLs and historic scope are preserved under 34 link records; references identify where to examine evidence, not automatic permission to publish.
- The six proposed canonical IDs are owned once by PR #1759 and all their signoffs remain false. **No identity promotion is authorized by this crosswalk.**
- The other 11 bilingual draft profiles are preserved in their entirety but have **no reserved permanent canonical IDs** pending distinct-action versus same-practice/regional-variant adjudication. A seasonal heritage custom is not automatically a Roman Catholic rite.
- Four local-event cases (`03`, `13`, `15`, `16`) require current parish/diocesan/municipal programme evidence before any scheduled event. A feast's liturgical date is not evidence a procession will occur.
- R18-05, 07 and 17 also involve localized relays, charity drives or shrine practices; preserve source-only context until organizer/locality verification.
- Every calendar anchor is research context, not an independent date selection or override of the existing 1962 Calendar resolver.
- Historical source evidence does not grant an indulgence. No work is associated with any indulgence, partial/plenary status, confession/Communion/prayer conditions or temporal requirement without a separately verified grant and precise conditions.
