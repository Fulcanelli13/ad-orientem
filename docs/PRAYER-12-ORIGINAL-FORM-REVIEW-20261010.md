## Significant source discrepancies requiring care

- **Memorare to St Joseph:** [Diocese of Sacramento](https://www.scd.org/stjoseph/prayers) prints *my great protector*, whereas the app says *my sweet protector*. The source is relevant but **not exact**.
- **Sacred Heart aspiration:** the [USCCB's Day 7 Sacred Heart novena](https://www.usccb.org/novena-sacred-heart-jesus) prints the invocation **without** the app's added *Most*. Do not attribute the expanded wording verbatim to this citation.
- **Sub tuum:** the [Holy See's official 2018 communiqué](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2018/09/29/180929d.html) prints the original Latin phrase *in necessitatibus*, whereas the app has *in necessitatibus nostris*. These are distinct historical/printed Latin readings, not a punctuation-only mismatch. The English translations also diverge.
- **Come Holy Spirit:** the [USCCB's prayer](https://www.usccb.org/prayers/prayer-holy-spirit) is the same invocation and collect structure, but the English collect is modernised compared to the traditional app version.
- **Prayer for the Pope:** [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/prayers/prayer-for-the-pope.html) gives a related but different papal prayer. It is not the printed source of the app's composite `Tu es Petrus`-containing prayer.
- **Spiritual Communion:** [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/prayers/the-spiritual-communion.html) juxtaposes a prayer of St Alphonsus and a *different* prayer by Cardinal Merry del Val. The app corresponds to the first only.
- **Eternal Rest for one deceased:** [Catholic Truth Society](https://www.ctsbooks.org/prayers-for-the-dead/) prints separate male/female alternatives and responses, while the app combines pronouns. The difference is an editorial adaptation, not a separate doctrinal text.
- **Christ the King consecration:** [Itheca](https://app.itheca.org/biblioteca/Oraciones/DeumUnuTri.html) supplies the full Latin act; the canonical EN/FR are translations that must not be represented as quotations from that Latin-only webpage.

## Positive original-source witnesses

- [USCCB Morning Offering](https://www.usccb.org/prayers/morning-offering): the entire English prayer and 1844 Gautrelet attribution are present, apart from normalised punctuation and verse layout.
- [USCCB Divine Praises](https://www.usccb.org/prayers/divine-praises): all fourteen English invocations agree in order, apart from ordinary capitalization, while the official page does not supply French and Latin.
- [Holy See St Michael original](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2018/09/29/180929d.html): complete Latin original and English translation appear together; the app's English uses *thrust* instead of the official translation's *cast*.
- [Leo XIII *Quamquam pluries*, appended Prayer to Saint Joseph](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_l-xiii_enc_15081889_quamquam-pluries.html): the full traditional English text is supplied after the encyclical, independently of later novena formulations.

## Certification and publishing boundary

These **12 source-form reviews cover the English relation and, where printed, Latin**. French translations remain uncollated against an independent original French Catholic prayer book. The work does not certify the whole 36-language-cell corpus, resolve English translation rights, or authorise changing canonical prayers. All twelve records remain under independent original-edition/rights approval. No new source capsule is created, and no source link in the live reader has been changed merely for being a useful *comparator*.
