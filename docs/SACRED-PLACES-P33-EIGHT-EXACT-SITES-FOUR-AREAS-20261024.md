### Dedication and physical unit corrections
- Zarvanytsia is the Ukrainian Greek Catholic **Mother of God** basilica, not the inaccurately labeled Assumption basilica; it is Eastern Catholic, not Orthodox.
- Wanjin Taiwan civil Ministry source is tagged correctly as SECONDARY typed source, not fabricated Catholic diocesan evidence; OSM physical church is Catholic.
- The original Bodajk Marian church was raised to minor basilica rank in 2023 per its own diocese.
- Regla and Santo Cerro are distinct church buildings from associated towns/other parish chapels; the Galle diocesan source names the Matara Shrine.
- Exact current access and schedule at Atalaya are not asserted; no 1962 Mass Times, relic, images, biographies, or outbound public links required.
