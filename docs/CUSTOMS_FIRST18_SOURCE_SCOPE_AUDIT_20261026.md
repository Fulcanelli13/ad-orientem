## Changes made to copy (not publication)

The St Patrick cross article's unsupported 1681 precision was corrected consistently to **the 1680s**, in both EN and FR descriptions/histories in the working master, 18-profile review snapshot, 30-case review JSON and readable editorial packet. Galway City Museum attributes the account to Thomas Dineley in the 1680s; previous precision must not be propagated. This correction does not prove uninterrupted contemporary household practice.

## What should actually be released next

Group publication decisions in **one cohesive 18-case editorial pass**, but reject any individual case whose locality, priest/lay rite boundary, historical origin, or distinctness remains disputed. Local Catholic parish and diocesan testimony can support a named regional custom; civic heritage observations need clearly labeled cultural context. A positive claim-scope source check is **not** by itself a final identity approval. Approved contextual suggestions should be added only to the existing Ad Orientem Calendar after a real human signoff and browser/date-integrity checks.
