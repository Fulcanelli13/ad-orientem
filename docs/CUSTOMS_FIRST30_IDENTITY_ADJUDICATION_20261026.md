## Action and date reasoning for all 30 records

### 1. St Patrick's ribbon cross

**Proposed disposition:** Potential distinct custom

**Action identity:** St Patrick ribbon crosses are household badge-making, not Saint Patrick's liturgical feast itself; keep historic 1681 and museum 1930s decline bounded.

**Calendar/locality boundary:** 17 March contextual only; no presumed dispensation or parish event

**Outstanding gate:** Identity/modern adoption versus documented historic wearing

### 2. The Irish Christmas candle

**Proposed disposition:** Potential distinct custom

**Action identity:** Irish Christmas window or table candle has direct Dúchas family testimony and is not the church's liturgical candle rite.

**Calendar/locality boundary:** 24–25 December optional household suggestion; family duration varies

**Outstanding gate:** Verify Irish-region witness and avoid deceased-spirit folklore as doctrine

### 3. St John Baptist's community bonfire
