# Catholic Sexual Ethics — bulk high-stakes source review (9 October 2026)

This review follows the merged conservative-reader and six-debate source audit (PR #497). It does **not** replace the canonical 150-question / 55-debate / 440-stage research source of truth.

## Editorial changes applied to English and French

- **CSE101:** *Donum Vitae* II.B.5 condemns even hypothetical homologous IVF with no embryo destruction, on the independent ground of replacing rather than assisting the conjugal act. The child conceived by IVF remains a person entitled to unconditional dignity, love and care. [Original](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19870222_respect-for-human-life_en.html).
- **CSE104:** The best contrary institutional case is not that exploitation never happens, but the [ASRM Ethics Committee's 2023 position](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/ethics-opinions/consideration-of-the-gestational-carrier-an-ethics-committee-opinion-2023/) that gestational-carrier arrangements can be ethical with informed consent, independent legal counsel and effective safeguarding. *[Donum Vitae* II.A.3](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19870222_respect-for-human-life_en.html) gives a distinct objection regarding the planned division of gestation and parenthood, even without payment. [*Dignitas Infinita* §§48–50](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240402_dignitas-infinita_en.html) is a later application.
- **CSE105/CSE109:** *Dignitas Personae* §§18 and 22–23 respectively distinguish embryo cryopreservation from implantation-related care and prenatal diagnostic testing for treatment from destructive selection. [Original](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20081208_dignitas-personae_en.html).
- **CSE110:** *Dignitas Personae* §19 rules out destructive research and rejects transfer to infertile couples as fertility treatment. The proposed **prenatal adoption** solely to save an embryo is recognized as praiseworthy in intention, but with unresolved grave difficulties; a blanket endorsement **or** a blanket condemnation would exceed the passage. Answer revised in EN/FR.
- **CSE112:** [Vandervoort et al., 2024 (PMID 38724699)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38724699/) reports 8 articles and 1 book with data from 136 male and 250 female survivors. Its actual finding is that physiological arousal **can occur** during non-consensual activity; it does **not** estimate frequency or establish a universal mechanism. The bodily-response inference is not a confession of consent.
- **CSE117:** [RAINN, Sexual Assault](https://rainn.org/get-the-facts-about-sexual-assault-rape/) treats sexual activity with persons incapacitated through intoxication, sleep or unconsciousness as non-consensual. It is not an advocate of the opposite proposition and does not supply a universal legal threshold for mild impairment.
- **CSE123–124:** [CDF Declaration on Procured Abortion §§11–14 and footnote 19 (1974)](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19741118_declaration-abortion_en.html) insists on respect from the beginning of human life while acknowledging historic disagreements concerning timing of ensoulment. Claims about psychological harms of pregnancy after rape need separate clinical sources; a general ACOG abortion page does **not** prove a particular philosophical position about the embryo or represent a cited opponent saying the exact objection.
- **CSE125:** [CDF 2009 Clarification](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20090711_aborto-procurato_en.html) distinguishes direct abortion from an operation addressing maternal disease with collateral, unintended fetal death. Clinical method choice in ectopic pregnancy and Catholic evaluations of salpingectomy, salpingostomy and methotrexate must remain clearly separate; the NCBC is **not the Magisterium**. The ACOG ectopic page did not provide accessible full clinical passage text in this review: no false verification of all techniques or outcomes has been recorded.
