# Angelus cinematic art board — review candidates

Status: editorial shortlist, no artwork currently selected, downloaded, or licensed for production.
Use one coherent master Annunciation painting for the first three movements wherever possible. The final Oremus should be quiet, not an added fourth Hail Mary.

## Camera travel
Leonardo da Vinci — Annunciation (Uffizi), c.1472. Ideal: panoramic pan from Gabriel across the garden to Mary. https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/annunciation
Botticelli — Annunciation fresco from San Martino (Uffizi), 1481. Ideal: extended architecture and portico perspective. https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/annunciation-635552ac-2628-47d6-a494-ccdf07f6154a

## Parallax depth
Fra Angelico — Annunciation (Museo del Prado), c.1426. Colonnade, vaults, garden and sacred figures form compositional planes. https://www.museodelprado.es/en/whats-on/multimedia/the-annunciation-fra-angelico/a4d5e558-86de-e4c7-6792-c480406dfd74
Lorenzo di Credi — Annunciation (Uffizi). Multiple background layers and depth. https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/annunciation-lorenzo-di-credi

## Incarnation illumination
Fra Angelico — Prado Annunciation, ray and dove: strongest theological specificity and continuity.
Henry Ossawa Tanner — The Annunciation (Philadelphia Museum of Art), 1898. Gabriel as radiant shaft of light. https://www.philamuseum.org/objects/104384

## Fiat composition/match cut
Botticelli — Cestello Annunciation (Uffizi), c.1489–90, specifically Mary's responding hands and the original Latin Fiat inscription. https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/annunciation-d9604d56-89bb-414c-ba3c-fd71f4d28eec
Fra Angelico — Prado Annunciation, match-pan within the same painting toward Mary rather than a style-breaking fade.

## Contemplative stillness, Oremus
Sassoferrato — The Virgin in Prayer (National Gallery London), 1640–50. https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/sassoferrato-the-virgin-in-prayer
Fra Angelico — Prado Annunciation, settle on a stationary detail of Mary to preserve stylistic continuity.

## Preferred two directions
A: Fra Angelico Prado: 4 crops and focal compositions within same painting; sacred iconography and easy visual cohesion.
B: Leonardo Uffizi: panoramic camera journey; strong spatial progression, less explicit dove/ray symbolism.
C: Tanner PMA: powerful light storytelling, but a distinct artistic identity unsuitable for mixing into A or B.

## Art acceptance before public release
Verify museum metadata, official high-resolution reproduction permissions, derivative rights and credit. Review safe mobile cropping and text contrast. Only move backgrounds and compositional planes; never puppet sacred figures. Reduced-motion users must see clean static scenes. Regina Caeli needs its own Resurrection art family.
