# Ad Orientem · Prayer Corpus · Editorial Acquisition Master (Wave 04)

28 October 2026 · **Research decision proposal, not an approved text-import order**.

## Objective

Do NOT become a prayer-book encyclopaedia. Support useful traditional Catholic prayer in context, with **one-tap alternatives** (as in Morning/Evening Prayer). Do not impose an arbitrary cap of ten: the admission rule is marginal value, not count. No new top-level modules, no costs.

## Registry summary

- **97 source candidate groups** (not 97 prayers; some are multiple works, distinct recensions, prayers already owned, and non-prayers).
- **ADD**: 7
- **ADD_LATER**: 6
- **ALTERNATIVE**: 25
- **DEFER**: 29
- **EXCLUDE**: 4
- **RETAIN**: 26
- **No user editorial approval has been assumed**, beyond the user's principle of flexible alternatives and useful traditional prayers.
- 94 internal ownership records remain frozen, and prior ICKSP (225 source/index units) and Raccolta (161 index slots) are separate heterogeneous research ledgers. Never add them together as a count of unique prayers.
- Five source families: ICKSP/ICRSP; Raccolta; Sensus Fidelium; Lasance; Baltimore/St Pius X catechism.

## The actual first text-mining addition

- `src/pray/prayer-acquisition-historical-witnesses.v1.js` now contains FOUR complete English historical witness texts and TWO separately collated Latin witnesses from Lasance's 1908 *My Prayer-Book*: **Prayer before Meditation**, **Prayer after Meditation**, **Suscipe**, and **St Thomas Aquinas's Concede mihi** short prayer.
- They are immutable source-bound objects with real wording and text visibility checks, matched to the moment of prayer. Two provide a one-tap **before reading/study** choice.
