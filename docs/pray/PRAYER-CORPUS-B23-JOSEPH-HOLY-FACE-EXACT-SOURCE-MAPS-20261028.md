## Seven Sorrows / Seven Joys of St Joseph — actual source sequence

The *1910 Raccolta* no. 300 has seven separate source prayers, each immediately followed by **one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be**. It then ends with the specific *Jesus Himself was about thirty years old* antiphon, *Pray for us, holy Joseph* versicle, response and *O God, who in Thine ineffable providence* collect. None of these is optional to a presentation claiming the **complete historical source form**.

| No. | Joseph's sorrow | Corresponding joy | Witness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Doubt/anguish concerning Mary | Angel reveals the Incarnation | Matthew 1 |
| II | Poverty of Bethlehem | Angels' joy at the Nativity | Luke 2 |
| III | Circumcision and shedding of Christ's Blood | The Name of Jesus | Luke 2 |
| IV | Simeon's prophecy of suffering | Salvation and resurrection foretold | Luke 2 |
| V | Flight into Egypt | Living with the incarnate Son; traditional overthrow of idols | Matthew 2 plus extra-scriptural devotional material |
| VI | Fear of Archelaus upon returning from Egypt | Safe home at Nazareth | Matthew 2 |
| VII | Finding Jesus missing for three days | Recovery of the Child in the Temple | Luke 2 |

The old index ID **§115** applies to the 1857 edition. The complete 1910 witness is **§300**, while §301 is a separately numbered Novena. Do not treat a **seven consecutive Sundays** practice as one prayer/sorrow per week or a nine-day programme without explicit source confirmation. Do not automatically preserve superseded historical indulgence grants as present-day benefits.

## Holy Face — original Janvier 1887 nine-day journey

The 1887 manual's nine meditation subjects are, in order:

1. Bethlehem
2. Public ministry among the people of Judea
3. Transfiguration on Tabor
4. Garden of Olives
5. Court of Caiaphas / St Peter's denial
6. Pilate's Praetorium
7. Way to Calvary / Veronica (traditional)
8. Face upon the Cross
9. Resurrection

The source is **one opening invocation**, each day's **complete meditation**, a distinct **act**, a **virtue to practise**, a **spiritual bouquet**, shared **aspirations**, and the historical **closing collect**. The public presentations on Sensus Fidelium and Holy Face websites are witness candidates. The Sensus presentation additionally contains 2022 *League of St Martin* consecration and Holy Name material that is **not part of the 1887 nine-day text**; do not merge the appendices into the novena.
