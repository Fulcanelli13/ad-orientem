# Wave 02c — ICRSP France Office and Ritual index-only discovery

28 October 2026. This is an extension of the ICKSP/ICRSP research draft PR #1847.

The [ICRSP France liturgical directory](https://icrspfrance.fr/textes-liturgiques.php) visibly indexes **109** relevant source titles:

| Group | Index units |
|---|---:|
| Office psalm portions (some split) | 65 |
| Office sequence/service pages | 16 |
| Hymn entries | 10 |
| Great O Advent antiphons | 7 |
| Canticles | 3 |
| Office psalm groups | 3 |
| Prudentius poems | 2 |
| Roman Ritual pages | 3 |
| **Total** | **109** |

**Every one is an INDEX TITLE, not a separately read and print-collated article.** This includes historic prior versions (for instance early *Ad cenam Agni providi*), and an incomplete Office catalogue, not a full 1962 Divine Office.

Twelve title links correspond to existing frozen owners (Office hymns, canticles, and *De profundis*); these are candidate matches, not verified textual identities. The Vulgate Psalm entries 9, 143, and 144 are split into portions and must not be counted as newly authored prayers.

Cumulative external discovery **225 units** = 40 Wave 02a + 76 Wave 02b + 109 Wave 02c. The 94 internal owner records are deliberately excluded from this total. External units include duplicate witnesses, programmes, incomplete references and excerpts: they are **NOT 225 unique prayers**.

No articles imported, no printed edition certified, no changes to existing canonical prayer IDs or prayer UI. Next route: collect actual direct article URLs and inspect original-Latin/translation witness, while keeping Divine Office separately structured by hour/season, especially for 1962 rubrical conformity.
