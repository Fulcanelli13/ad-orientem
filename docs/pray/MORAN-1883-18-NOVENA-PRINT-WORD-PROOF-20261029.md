### Printed sequence and non-body texts

The Immaculate Conception novena's **preparatory prayer begins on p566 and continues onto p567**, before the individual daily texts. The printed source directs nine Hail Marys, a Glory Be and a choice of the Litany or *Tota pulchra* as distinct devotional elements, **not** as additional words inserted into each proper day-prayer body. Each day's daily prayer has a separate heading and date. Moran's printed note about indulgences relates to the *historical edition* and **must not** be transferred as a currently valid indulgence grant or set of conditions without independent current evidence.

St Joseph's printed programme begins on **p603**, instructing **three Our Fathers and three Hail Marys** on each day, alongside the selected day's body. These are likewise separate reusable elements, not words missing from the proper daily prayer. The ninth St Joseph prayer ends on p608, immediately before a distinct Corpus Christi novena begins.

The prior Wikisource DjVu copy omits Immaculate Conception printed pp566–567. The alternative full original [Internet Archive PDF](https://archive.org/download/TheCatholicPrayerBook1883/TheCatholicPrayerBook1883.pdf) visibly contains both. This corrects a *source-copy completeness* problem, not the reader's prayers.
