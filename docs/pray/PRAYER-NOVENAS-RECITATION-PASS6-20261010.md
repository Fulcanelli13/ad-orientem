# Prayer — novena source and recitation audit, pass 6 (10 October 2026)

## Scope and source discipline
This pass reconciles the **nine Holy Souls day prayers**, their **daily repeated verse**, and the **1883 Immaculate Conception closing alternatives** against the linked historical-text *transcriptions*, without assigning unearned printed-facsimile or French-edition certification. It extends the existing [16-record novena source-review matrix](../../data/pray/novena-source-review.v2.json) rather than creating another prayer database.

## Corrected functional omission: Holy Souls nine-day recitation
The [first-day transcription](https://www.ecatholic2000.com/life/prayer108.shtml) prints (a) the daily prayer, (b) one Our Father and Hail Mary, (c) **four verse lines beginning “On Thy spouses have compassion”**, followed by (d) seven “O most sweet Jesus” intercessions and a petition on behalf of the souls. The [second day](https://www.ecatholic2000.com/life/prayer109.shtml) and [ninth day](https://www.ecatholic2000.com/life/prayer116.shtml) explicitly invoke those daily repeating elements again. All nine linked pages were examined, and the distinct English daily prayers substantially correspond to the existing app bodies; some online pages have obvious typographic anomalies, such as “they powerful intercession” where the app reads “thy powerful intercession”. Such differences are recorded as transcription uncertainties, **not proofread original-edition corrections**.

The app already showed both common canonical prayers and the longer intercessions, but skipped the four-line verse on every day. This pass restores the original English verse and its **explicitly editorial French translation**, prepended to the existing shared closing prayer, at the existing Guided and Simple stages. It does **not** duplicate the Our Father or Hail Mary; those remain canonical references. The English original printing, the authenticity of the St Alphonsus attribution and a historic French printed edition remain uncollated.

The modern online witness was previously labelled `PUBLIC_DOMAIN_ORIGINAL · MULTI-WITNESS_DIGITAL_TRANSCRIPTION`, liable to be read as original-edition verification. The runtime record now accurately calls it a **modern digital transcription** and clearly states the French text's editorial nature.

## Corrected representation of Moran's Immaculate Conception novena
[Moran's 1883 novena](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Catholic_Prayer_Book_and_Manual_of_Meditations/Novena_in_Preparation_for_the_Feast_of_the_Immaculate_Conception) directs the **Litany of the Blessed Virgin OR the Tota pulchra hymn** after the day's prayer, not an entirely optional closing that may be skipped. The reader already provides the canonical Loreto Litany; no new duplicate version has been added. The closing rubric now says the app selects the Litany from the two historically permitted forms. The source disclosure identifies the alternative hymn and the 1883 historical collects, including a collect naming Leo, without mistaking that fixed historic name for the current pontiff.
