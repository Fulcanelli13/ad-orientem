## Pristine acceptance contract

Every promoted source must have the exact approved painting, authentic high-quality photograph, preserved full painted composition, natural colour/texture, no mounting hardware/print mat/exterior frame, no artificial upscaling, source-file copyright and photographer-credit compliance, and archived original/derivative hashes. Unknowns remain HOLD. Browser previews are not a publication gate.
