## Explicit non-conflation and source controls

- Golden Arrow (Holy Face/Holy Name) is a separate reparatory aspiration from Pius XI's `Iesu dulcissime`; Christ King dedication reuses its existing baptismal-vow canonical text rather than duplicating the Consecration of the Human Race.
- The 1909 St Joseph historical invocation sequence has **25 St Joseph invocations plus Holy Mary**; the 2021 form has **32 plus Holy Mary**. Seven 2021 additions are excluded from the historical default. Full printed-edition EN/FR lexical collation remains held.
- The Institute's recurring Immaculate Conception repeat-form observance is **30 November–8 December**; the historical Moran day-specific preparatory Novena remains separately labelled **29 November–7 December**. Institute Tuesday perpetual St Thérèse is a weekly source-linked form, not a nine-day Novena or the existing Twenty-Four Glory Bes.
- Eight newly accepted historical Office hymn recensions are distinct from the three original hymns, Eucharistic `Pange lingua gloriosi Corporis` owned elsewhere, and the separate Passion hymn. Original Office placement, language status and pre-1632 conditional exceptions remain marked. No modern ICKSP scores are redistributed.
- The bedside `Proficiscere` now follows post-1922 Marian/St Joseph and Virtutes Latin clauses from a 1937 Roman Ritual OCR witness. English/French are openly described as aligned editorial texts; **exact 1962 Roman printed collation remains pending**. Medical emergencies do not wait for prayer or a priest, and the app never simulates sacraments.
- The 1962 public Litany of Saints remains source-held as a whole; private historical litany witnesses must not be silently presented as equivalent to Rogation, Easter Vigil or Forty Hours texts.
