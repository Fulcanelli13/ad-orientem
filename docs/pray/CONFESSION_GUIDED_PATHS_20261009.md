# Confession: quick, returning and general preparation

The sacramental guide uses **three user-selected pathways**, not a time-derived theological requirement. None of them records sins or simulates the sacrament.

| Path | Audience | Examination |
|---|---|---|
| Quick / regular | Someone who comes to Confession regularly | The current compact Ten Commandments examination, one expandable list |
| Returning after a long absence | Someone who has not confessed for months or years | 13 cards: tell the priest you are returning, place the approximate interval, review the Ten Commandments one at a time, and conclude with contrition |
| Optional general Confession | Someone desiring a broader review of life or a significant past period, preferably with a confessor's guidance | 14 cards: agree the scope, review periods and duties, avoid compulsive re-confession of already absolved sins, follow the Ten Commandments, and conclude |

The user explicitly chooses the path. An absence of years **does not automatically require** a general Confession. General Confession is an **individual** confession over an extended period, not the distinct exceptional collective rite of **general absolution**. General review does not remove the duty to confess remembered grave sins not yet confessed by kind and number. For recurrences over a long interval, approximate frequency may be given honestly where exact counts cannot reasonably be recalled. Venial sins may also be confessed.

The interface has four shared macro stages: preparation/choice, examination, approach the priest (put away phone), and penance/thanksgiving. Only the examination stage expands to additional cards. The patient is never asked to write or select sins, mark transgressions, or upload a confession. Optional free-text time since Confession is ephemeral and cleared on a genuine route close.

**Grounding**

- [Code of Canon Law canons 959–960 and 987–989](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann959-997_en.html)
- [Catechism §§1454–1458](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_two/article_4/vii_the_acts_of_the_penitent.html)
- [Misericordia Dei, §3 (integral confession)](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/motu_proprio/documents/hf_jp-ii_motu-proprio_20020502_misericordia-dei.html)

This is a self-examination aid, **not** an individual priest's spiritual guidance. A confessor may determine that a shorter or broader review is appropriate, especially in cases of uncertainty, scrupulosity, or an exceptional sacramental history.
