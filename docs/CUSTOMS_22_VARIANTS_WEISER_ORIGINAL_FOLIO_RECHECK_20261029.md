## Corrections carried through all research projections

- **001 (p. 27):** Weiser describes prayer for the departed on Mondays, not an independently evidenced household ritual. EN/FR history narrowed accordingly.
- **003 (p. 55):** The actual regional privilege was associated specifically with the names **John or Joan** (Jean or Jeanne). Replaced the incorrect broad phrase 'a designated child' and revised bilingual historical context/practice. Fire safety remains.
- **013 (pp. 279–280):** Avoid attributing terms or a single date that the cited folio does not prove. Local patronal festivals, ecclesiastical dedication and civic celebration are distinguished.
- **021 (p. 312):** Weiser expressly describes certain communities in the Philippines; wording and geography narrowed to avoid country-wide implication.

The original source demonstrates descriptive claims only; it does **not** authorize a Roman Ritual blessing by laypeople or prove any automatic indulgence. Original Calendar, Mass, feasts, optional house customs and possible local processions remain distinct.
