# Customs R18 — original-witness recheck and first Calendar release order

**26 October 2026 · narrow source claims checked again: 18/18 · editorial signatures: 0 · Calendar releases: 0**

This is the final **research gate and priority ordering**, not a publication authorization. The 18 already fielded EN/FR drafts remain in [the bilingual candidate pack](../data/customs/master/customs-r18-bilingual-calendar-candidate-pack-20261026.v1.json), retaining 17 original master identities and one proposed Provençal companion.

## Release order

- **Tier A: six** date-stable household or historic-context customs. Best first human editorial signoff cohort because these are not dependent on live parish event schedules. They must remain voluntary or explicitly historical, geographically bounded, and source-cited.
- **Tier B: seven** region/faith/variant-sensitive or organizer-dependent subjects. Admit as contextual descriptions only when classification and geographic visibility are accepted; do not automatically issue participation instructions.
- **Tier C: four** locality-specific parish/civic procession or bonfire subjects. The historical custom can be described with a contextual feast link, but the **current event** cannot appear on the Calendar without a fresh dated local source.
- **Tier D: one** post-midnight Provençal Christmas *réveillon* passage, proposed as companion material under the existing Provençal *pain calendal* / *Gros Souper* owner. It should not generate an extra canonical identity or second date-based card.

## Original-source findings that materially affect approval

**Irish Christmas candle:** The Dúchas Schools' Collection record has two *different* provenance fields: it was collected at **Graine school in County Kilkenny**, and its named informant, **Mr M. Delahunty (age 68)**, supplied an address at **Graigaheesha, County Tipperary**. Both are stated in the archived record. Earlier notes leaving the informant county unknown were wrong and have been corrected. An informant's address does not establish the tradition's first origin or universal distribution. Source: https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbes/4758582/4756445/4934128

**St Patrick crosses:** The National Museum of Ireland dates Thomas Dineley's observation to **1681**, consistent with Galway City Museum's **1680s** decade formulation; the latter separately documents historic decline and describes optional modern reconstruction instructions. This may be shown as a documented traditional craft, not evidence of uninterrupted present-day practice or an automatic Lenten dispensation. Sources: https://source.museum.ie/en-IE/Collections-Research/Folklife-Collections/Folklife-Collections-List-(1)/Religion-and-Calendar-Customs/St-Patrick and https://galwaycitymuseum.ie/blog/1-reviving-an-old-tradition-st-patricks-crosses/

**Household candidates:** The Archdiocese of Vienna directly describes 4 December cherry/apple branches; Poland's Ministry of Culture describes hay below the 24 December Wigilia tablecloth; the Austrian parish of Großpetersdorf directly documents an Advent Marian-image relay between homes. These are descriptions of actions, not 1962 rubrical orders. Sources: https://www.erzdioezese-wien.at/heilige-barbara ; https://www.gov.pl/web/kultura/nowy-wpis-na-krajowej-liscie-niematerialnego-dziedzictwa-kulturowego ; https://www.pfarre-grosspetersdorf.at/pfarre/1131/aktuelles/article/12277.html

**Cross-confessional/Eastern-cultural limits:** UNESCO documents Ukrainian pysanka as a wax-resist craft transmitted across religious communities, while Radom Museum documents Polish pisanki techniques. Do not present the decorative craft as a Roman priestly blessing or imply both crafts are identical. Sources: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/pysanka-ukrainian-tradition-and-art-of-decorating-eggs-02134 ; https://www.muzeum-radom.pl/terminy/plastyka-obrzedowa/361

**Actual local events versus perpetual feast dates:** Oloron's source announced a bonfire on **20 June 2026** (St John Baptist's feast is 24 June); Tivoli diocese announced its Inchinata for **14–15 August 2026**; Sassari municipality documents its 2026 Faradda on **14 August**; Maltese reporting documents *l-Irxoxt* running in selected Easter Sunday processions. These may support local history and contextual associations, but **not an annually scheduled parish occurrence, a 1962 Mass gesture or ministerial ritual**. Sources: https://diocese64.org/evenement/feu-de-la-saint-jean-a-oloron-sainte-marie/ ; https://diocesitivoliepalestrina.it/festa-dellinchinata-2026/ ; https://www.comune.sassari.it/it/novita/notizia/Candelieri-2026-segui-tutti-gli-aggiornamenti/ ; https://timesofmalta.com/article/running-risen-unique-easter-tradition-draws-crowds-and-devotion.1108477

**Provençal meal separation:** French Ministry of Culture's original intangible heritage inventory, **printed page 4/PDF index 3**, describes the *Gros Souper* before midnight and the *réveillon* after midnight. This is a regional food custom, **not** universal 1962 fasting law or a separate liturgical act. Source: https://www.culture.gouv.fr/Media/Thematiques/Patrimoine-culturel-immateriel/Files/Fiches-inventaire-du-PCI/Les-fetes-de-Noel-en-Provence

## What is necessary to publish Tier A

The **six** Tier-A cards are now the sensible single acceptance batch, not a new broad research cycle. For every card an accountable owner must sign exact source-to-assertion scope, distinct owner, EN/FR articles, geographical visibility and 1962 day/season association, and confirm that the card is a voluntary or historical suggestion, not a priest's rite or obligation. Only then can new canonical IDs be allocated where appropriate. Implement from the latest `main` Calendar read model and run date/browser QA. Avoid creating another Calendar or Customs Atlas.

The other 12 continue in the same master review register but do **not** block accepting safe Tier-A subjects individually. Their separate constraints must not be waived just to increase published counts.

The existing [cohort 1 PR #1642](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/pull/1642) and [cohort 2 PR #1720](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/pull/1720) remain the owners of their earlier, different five release subjects. **None** of these R18 additions are currently published, and 35 is still the published master/runtime article baseline.
