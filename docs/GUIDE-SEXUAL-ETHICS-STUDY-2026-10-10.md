# Formation Phase 3 — optional source-first Sexual Ethics study (10 October 2026)

## Published basis
The public Catholic Sexual Ethics reader currently exposes 147 of the 150 editorial Q&A. The archived CSE055/CSE056/CSE058 remain excluded with their established redirects. Canonical ownership is 50 dossier units grouped in 7 families. Publication/review decisions and all original question, answer, debate and citation wording are unchanged.

## Two Guide functions
- The index contains a small, collapsible **Guide · how to study the questions**: read the original question/answer, examine objections and Catholic responses where present, and follow original links. This is study-method guidance, not another theological answer.
- Each of the 7 theme screens and 50 dossier screens offers an optional **Study in order** launcher that opens the existing reader at the first publicly accessible question of that scope. Previous/Next (or Finish) move through that existing canonical ordering.
- Each question in guided mode shows the current position and a reminder to consult its sources before advancing. Deep arguments, opposition attributions, paragraph citation links, apostolate handoffs and related questions remain the **same source-backed content** as in the reference reader.

## Rules and boundaries
- No new, duplicate, abbreviated or reinterpreted moral Q&A is authored.
- No archived/unpublished case is reintroduced; source/order helper filters solely against `CSE_PUBLIC_QUESTION_MAP`. Every public question appears exactly once across family paths.
- Guides are optional, session-only reading paths with no gamification, scores, persistent progress or implied doctrinal approval.
- Back/Finish returns to the correct owning family or dossier. Direct links and global search continue to open the established reference reader without forcing the study path.
