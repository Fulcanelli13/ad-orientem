## Real corrections

**CR-ORG-05 / CR-ORG-09 — historical author quoted on his own terms.** Guillaume Cuchet's university-hosted synopsis of his [2018 book](https://crhec.u-pec.fr/publications/comment-notre-monde-a-cesse-d-etre-chretien) specifically identifies Vatican II as a possible *trigger* in rapid French dechristianisation, within longer cultural decline. A [scholarly historical review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhef_0048-7988_2018_num_104_253_5072_t17_0432_0000_3) explores the sharp 1965/1968 timing and that proposed mechanism. Previous drafts were appropriately cautious but risked reducing the author's concrete claim to generic multi-causality. Keep the French case under ORG-05 and crosslink the international comparative causal question to ORG-09. No globally demonstrated causal model claimed.

**CR-LIT-04 — Protestant observers versus evidence of authorship.** A history of the British Joint Liturgical Group cannot establish the names, influence or drafting status of Protestants who might have attended the Roman *Consilium*. Attribution to specific Roman documents remains conditional on named meeting minutes/proposals. Paul VI's [*Missale Romanum*](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/apost_constitutions/documents/hf_p-vi_apc_19690403_missale-romanum.html) confirms final papal promulgation. [Historian Yves Chiron](https://churchlifejournal.nd.edu/articles/how-the-novus-ordo-mass-was-made/) examines 1967 proposals and the changing Roman institutions, without treating the wider British group as the Roman drafting commission.

**CR-LIT-04–07 — exact original opponent attribution.** [Cardinals Ottaviani and Bacci signed the letter of 25 September 1969](https://apr.sspx.org/en/letter-cardinal-ottaviani-pope-paul-vi-30144), transmitting a [*Short Critical Study* prepared by a distinct group](https://smac.edu/en/ottaviani-intervention-30257). The cardinal signatures are not proof they wrote every line of the Study. The documented 1969 text alleges less explicit sacrificial expression and other theological difficulties. The 1970 amendments to the General Instruction, discussed in [Chiron's history](https://churchlifejournal.nd.edu/articles/how-the-novus-ordo-mass-was-made/), need to be compared with the original 1969 version; they do not erase the historical objection or prove invalidity of the revised rite. CR-LIT-07's old answer was too methodological for a question about *which prayers changed*: a new sourced bilingual answer specifically contrasts the [1962 Offertory](https://www.liturgiapapal.org/index.php/recursos-lit%C3%BArgicos/misal/missale-1962/1712-ofertorio.html) with the revised preparation of the gifts, while noting preservation of the Roman Canon as Eucharistic Prayer I.

**CR-LIT-08 — replace abstraction with actual lectionary architecture.** [General Introduction to the Lectionary §§65–69](https://origin.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/general-introduction-to-the-lectionary-second-edition-28913) identifies a three-year Sunday cycle, seasonal arrangements and in Ordinary Time a one-year weekday Gospel plus two-year first reading cycle. This differs from the historic one-year Sunday Epistle and Gospel selection. These facts are directly stated in the new draft. A claim of deliberate omission of difficult verses still requires actual pericope indexing; increased potential exposure is not equal to what any congregation actually hears.

**CR-ECC-11 / CR-ECC-12 — separate doctrinal history from legal/teaching status.** Ecclesiology's relation among baptized faithful, bishops and Pope belongs to ECC-11; ECC-12 should focus on dated synodal enactments. In his [signed 24 November 2024 note, officially published 25 November](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2024/11/25/241125k.html), Francis said the 2024 Synod final document (approved 26 October) **forms part of the ordinary Magisterium of the Successor of Peter**, and simultaneously said it is **not strictly normative** and its application needs mediation. The old draft effectively treated it only as a further consultation and understated teaching status. The bilingual correction now states both, without equating ordinary Magisterium with a defined dogma or automatically effective universal legislation. [*Episcopalis communio* art.18](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/apost_constitutions/documents/papa-francesco_costituzione-ap_20180915_episcopalis-communio.html) is the juridical context.

## Preserved correct material

- **ORG-01–04,06–08:** preconciliar Pius X and Pius XII warnings, John XXIII's actual 1961 intent, the distinction between ressourcement and Modernism, and preparatory schema voting remain well specified. Karl Rahner's original 1962 manuscript is only catalogued in the recovered source: don't misquote unexamined content. SSPX 2012 criticism's four actual conciliar objections are represented in its own words.
- **LIT-01–03:** Pius XII-approved [*Maxima redemptionis* (16 November 1955)](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccdds/documents/rc_con_ccdds_doc_19551116_maxima-redemptionis_la.html) established Holy Week reform from 25 March 1956, after a 1951 Easter Vigil experiment; *Sacrosanctum Concilium* must be distinguished from 1964/1967 implementation and 1969 promulgation. The Council did not itself mandate universal Latin abandonment or priest-facing-people Mass.
- **LIT-09–12:** *Sacrosanctum Concilium* §§36 and 116 protects Latin and gives Gregorian chant a privileged place. The [25 September 2000 response on GIRM 299](https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/responses-to-questions-29995) explicitly rejects a universal prohibition of celebration facing the apse. [*Memoriale Domini*](https://origin.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/instruction-on-the-manner-of-distributing-holy-communion-28903) records 597 yes, 1,233 no, 315 yes with reservations, 20 invalid in the 1969 episcopal consultation on Communion in the hand. Historic veiling law, modern personal custom and permission for female altar servers are different juridical categories.
