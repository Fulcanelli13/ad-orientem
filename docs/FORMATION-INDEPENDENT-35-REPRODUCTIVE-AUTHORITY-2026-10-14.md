# Formation independent Q&A audit — Reproductive ethics, safeguarding, and authority

**14 October 2026 · Research branch only · Catechism excluded · Live app unchanged**

## Scope and checks

**35 existing owners:** 25 Sexual Ethics (**CSE101–125**), ten Church Crisis (**CR-AUT-01–10**). All 25 concise current English answers and linked references were examined; relevant fuller sections and available opposition were reviewed selectively. All ten Church Crisis drafts were read across their four existing English roles (**40 English sections**). Existing French fields were checked for presence but did not receive a separate native-language theological signoff.

Full-book acquisition is **not required**. Correctly attributed original passages or dependable quotations with precise work and locators substantiate bounded claims. Special care is required where a source is a **professional association's advocated position**, a **clinical study**, a **magisterial theological statement** or **canon law**; these have different evidentiary authority and purposes.

| Canonical question(s) | Finding | Precise original/attributed source |

| **CSE101** | Hypothetical IVF using only the spouses' gametes and destroying no embryos is **expressly** rejected in the original teaching. The drafted answer rightly distinguishes this from the dignity of children conceived by IVF. | [*Donum vitae* II.B.5](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19870222_respect-for-human-life_en.html) |
| **CSE104** | ASRM 2023 **actually endorses** gestational-carrier arrangements with independent counselling, consent, legal safeguards, and autonomy. The Catholic doctrinal objection in *Donum vitae* II.A.3 concerns the arrangement independently of showing that every carrier was coerced or paid. **Genuinely opposed arguments, both identified in the existing answer.** | [ASRM original 2023 Ethics Committee opinion](https://prod.asrm.org/practice-guidance/ethics-opinions/consideration-of-the-gestational-carrier-an-ethics-committee-opinion-2023/) |
| **CSE110** | *Dignitas personae* §19 identifies prenatal adoption to rescue frozen embryos as praiseworthy in intent but morally difficult and does not prescribe a universally approved solution. **Current answer is accurate; don't falsely present an unresolved case as settled.** | [*Dignitas personae* §19](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20081208_dignitas-personae_en.html) |
| **CSE112–113** | Earlier independent-review gap on bodily response in assault is now **resolved at the empirical-source level**. The 2024 original review screened **13,894 records, retained eight articles and one book**, and found that genital reactions can occur during nonconsensual sex; its data **cannot establish a reliable incidence rate**. These findings support existing precise wording. They never imply consent from involuntary response. | [Vandervoort et al., original 2024 scoping-review abstract, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38724699/); [Aquinas I-II q.6](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2006.htm) for separate voluntariness doctrine |
| **CSE114–120** | Coercion, incapacity, forced compliance, freezing, grooming, abuse of spiritual power and involuntary pleasure require distinct assessment. **CSE119 correctly explains that a survivor need not confess as personal sins actions perpetrated against them.** Canon 988 refers to sins committed by the penitent. Don't add speculative trauma prevalence or automatic assumptions about a victim's will. | [1983 Canon 988](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann959-997_en.html); [Aquinas I-II q.6](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2006.htm) |
| **CSE121–122** | Pius XI's historical criticisms concerned indiscriminate, potentially harmful sex instruction, **not** a universal prohibition of accurate age-appropriate parental education. Present concise answer avoids that anachronism. | [*Divini illius magistri*](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-xi_enc_31121929_divini-illius-magistri.html) |
| **CSE123–124** | The Church's abortion teaching is not based on a proof of pain, immediate viability or a dogmatically defined moment of ensoulment. Opposition centered on severe burden, trauma and autonomy is explicitly acknowledged without caricature. | [CDF *Declaration on Procured Abortion* (1974)](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19741118_declaration-abortion_en.html) |
| **CSE125** | The original **11 July 2009** CDF clarification distinguishes direct abortion from appropriately justified treatment of maternal disease whose *unintended* effect is fetal death. The original **23 June 2010** USCCB committee explanation is a second bounded witness. Ectopic pregnancy treatment is urgent medical care: [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/ectopic-pregnancy) identifies internal bleeding, severe pain, shoulder pain and weakness as possible emergency features. The existing answer correctly refuses to treat every specific procedure's Catholic moral classification as automatically decided by a medical-treatment list; emergency care must never be delayed by an unresolved ethical debate. | [CDF 2009 original](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/wss/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20090711_aborto-procurato_en.html) · [USCCB committee 2010 original](https://www.usccb.org/resources/direct-abortion-statement2010-06-23) |
| **CR-AUT-01–04** | Original [Aquinas II-II q.104 a.5](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3104.htm), [q.33 a.4](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3033.htm), [canon 212](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib2-cann208-223_en.html) and the 1998 CDF *Professio fidei* commentary underpin bounded obedience, correction and levels of assent. New **four-section bilingual draft**, sources linked. | [*Donum veritatis* §§23–31](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19900524_theologian-vocation_en.html) · [Canon 212](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib2-cann208-223_en.html) |
