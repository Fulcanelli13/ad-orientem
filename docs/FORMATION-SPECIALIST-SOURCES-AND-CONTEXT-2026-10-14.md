## Concrete context checks and cautions

- **CSE008/CSE010:** Existing distinction between the chosen object, intentions and consequences tracks *Veritatis splendor* §§78–82. John Stuart Mill's *Utilitarianism* supplies a genuine non-Catholic theoretical counterposition, but not an attributed position on these specific sexual practices.
- **CSE016/CSE061/CSE063:** Existing conscience, contraception and periodic-abstinence explanations track their original Catholic documents. Curran's 2018 retrospective supplies an authored critical position suitable for adversarial collation; it is not a substitute for a verbatim original 1968 dissent statement.
- **CSE101:** The current concise IVF answer's hypothetical case (homologous IVF with no embryo destruction) is explicitly treated by *Donum vitae* II.B.5; the later *Dignitas personae* is an additional source. Full debate, bilingual and ethical review still required.
- **CSE091/CSE096:** *Dignitas infinita* §§55–60 establishes the specific Church teaching discussed; an official moral text is not a source of independent clinical efficacy/safety evidence.
- **CSE112/CSE113:** The distinction between involuntary physiological response and consent is retained; the current cited 2024 empirical scoping review was **not independently verified here**. No prevalence inference may be drawn from this partial audit.
- **CR-DOC-04:** *Dignitatis humanae* and the preconciliar original *Quanta cura* are mapped against SSPX's own religious-liberty objections. Pairing original sources documents disagreement but does **not** settle continuity versus rupture.
- **CR-DOC-06:** *Mortalium animos*, *Lumen gentium*, *Unitatis redintegratio* and SSPX's 1974 original position are included for comparison; precise context and historical semantics remain pending.
- **CR-ECC-07:** The Holy See's *Ecclesia Dei adflicta* (1988) and SSPX's own original defence of the episcopal consecrations are both accessible. Historical status must not be conflated with subsequent canonical circumstances.
