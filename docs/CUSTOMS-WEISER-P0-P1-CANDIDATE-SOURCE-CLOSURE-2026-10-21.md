# Weiser P0/P1 Candidate Source Closure — 21 October 2026

## Scope and result

This batch reviewed the 11 distinct candidate groups marked P0 or P1 in `weiser-54-editorial-owner-reconciliation.v1.json`: six P0 and five P1. The 568-observation inventory and 54-card owner-reconciliation ledger were not re-audited.

The six P0 identities now have source-backed scope and owner parity with canonical Customs Atlas records already merged to the application: `ADVT-001`, `ADVT-002`, `ADVT-003`, `XMAS-001`, `HTHU-001`, and `ROGA-001`. This batch did not publish or alter any application record.

The five P1 candidates have independently supported, regionally bounded practice descriptions. They remain provisional and are not new canonical Customs records: Austrian/German Epiphany star singers; the French Epiphany cake and optional poor-person's share; Corpus Christi route flowers and carpets; the German/Austrian Easter Monday Emmaus walk; and the household Mary Garden.

Structured record with bilingual copy, 1962 calendar context, owners, sources, claims and gaps: [source-closure JSON](../data/customs/research/weiser-p0-p1-candidate-source-closure.v1.json).

## Independent sources

- **Las Posadas:** [USCCB Advent December 16](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year/advent/advent-december-16); [Mexico Ministry of Culture](https://www.gob.mx/cultura/es/articulos/y-tu-como-celebras-las-posadas?idiom=es). The Ministry notes competing origin accounts, so the 1587 Acolman account is not stated as settled.
- **Opłatek:** [Culture.pl](https://culture.pl/en/article/polish-christmas-eve-traditions); [Polish Bishops' Conference](https://episkopat.pl/doc_pr/181602.polish-christmas-traditions-2). Both describe Christmas Eve family sharing; neither establishes an exact early origin.
- **Holy Thursday visits:** [EWTN News](https://www.ewtnnews.com/world/us/why-do-catholics-visit-7-churches-on-holy-thursday); [Archdiocese of Sydney guide](https://sydneycatholic.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Holy-Thursday-Visitation-of-the-Seven-Churches.pdf). The reports support the voluntary devotion, not a universal number, indulgence or obligation.
- **Advent wreath:** [Archdiocese of Cologne history](https://www.erzbistum-koeln.de/presse_und_medien/magazin/Der-Adventskranz-Bedeutung-und-Ursprung-des-Adventskranzes/?channel=MSN); [Vatican State magazine](https://vaticanstate.va/images/trimestrale/2-numero-en.pdf); [USCCB household blessing resource](https://www.usccb.org/prayers/blessing-advent-wreath). The history distinguishes the German Protestant origin from Catholic reception.
- **Rogation family walk:** [Catholic Culture family activity](https://www.catholicculture.org/culture/liturgicalyear/activities/view.cfm?id=1234); [National Catholic Register family witness](https://www.ncregister.com/blog/behe-rogation-days-memories); [Catholic Encyclopedia on historical processions](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12446c.htm). The latter documents the wider historic ecclesiastical practice, not an ancient family rite.
- **Manger straw:** [Catholic Culture family account](https://www.catholicculture.org/commentary/straws-in-manger-preparing-our-hearts-for-christs-birth/); [Polish Bishops' Conference on hay beneath the Christmas Eve table](https://episkopat.pl/doc_pr/181602.polish-christmas-traditions-2). The Polish hay custom is explicitly kept distinct and is not corroboration of the manger-straw origin.
- **Sternsinger:** [Austrian Catholic Jungschar / Dreikönigsaktion history](https://www.dka.at/sternsingen/hintergruende-zum-sternsingen); [Archdiocese of Cologne account](https://www.erzbistum-koeln.de/presse_und_medien/magazin/kirchen-glaubensorte/Heilige-Drei-Koenige-Herkunft-und-Bedeutung-des-Feiertags/).
- **Galette:** [French Bishops' Conference](https://eglise.catholique.fr/approfondir-sa-foi/la-celebration-de-la-foi/lesgrandes-fetes%20chretiennes/epiphanie/372242-fete-de-lepiphanie-en-occident-la-fete-des-rois-et-qu/); [French Ministry of Agriculture](https://agriculture.gouv.fr/apres-les-fetes-place-la-galette); [Institut de France culinary history](https://podcasts.institutdefrance.fr/emissions/les-chroniques/histoire-et-gastronomie/le-gateau-ou-la-galette-des-rois). Roman Saturnalia is retained only as a theory, not direct descent.
- **Corpus Christi carpets:** [Catalan Government Heritage](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/en/collection/arbucies-festival-enramades); [Galicia Tourism guide](https://www.turismo.gal/osdam/filestore/1/8/4/5/2_42077859a15ba54/18452_e939f6434093b45.pdf). These are separate local attestations; neither licenses a generic procession date or map pin.
- **Emmausgang:** [Diocese of Essen](https://www.bistum-essen.de/pressemenue/lexikon/e-lexikon/emmausgang); [Vatican News on Easter Monday in Austria](https://www.vaticannews.va/de/kirche/news/2026-04/ostermontag-feiertag-emmausgeschichte-arbeitsruhe.html). The French/Canadian visiting-grandparents variant from Weiser is not established by these witnesses and remains out of scope.
- **Mary Garden:** [University of Dayton Marian Library archive](https://udayton.edu/marianlibrary/marysgardens/b/background-on-marys-gardens.php); [University of Dayton Magazine](https://udayton.edu/magazine/2024/05/mary-garden.php); [Howth Parish garden leaflet](https://howthparish.ie/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Mary-Garden-leaflet-WEB-2020-01-18.pdf). Plant names and legends vary; the modern named-garden movement is documented from the twentieth century.

## Calendar and pastoral guardrails

- These records are household, popular or regional customs. None creates a feast, obligation, Mass action, indulgence or universal parish event.
- Calendar links remain seasonal discovery aids. Only a locally announced event should supply a time, route, open church, procession or blessing.
- Priest-only acts stay with an authorised minister and an applicable ritual edition. This report supplies no blessing script.
- Opłatek is not the Eucharist. Sternsinger door lettering is not a priestly house blessing. Galette is not a sacramental. Rogation household prayer is distinct from clergy-led procession or field blessing.
- No folklore promise, magical effect, guaranteed protection or supernatural claim has been added.

## Remaining gaps retained

1. **Las Posadas:** exact first origin and diffusion remain disputed.
2. **Opłatek:** precise early history is unresolved; regional wafer customs should not be collapsed.
3. **Seven Churches:** the history of Rome's basilica pilgrimage and later Holy Thursday altar visits has multiple accounts; no set number or benefit is asserted.
4. **Advent wreath:** first Catholic household adoption is less certain than its German origin and later Catholic spread.
5. **Rogation family walk:** independent sources support a present-day family adaptation, not a historic universal family custom.
6. **Manger straw:** no independent historical evidence yet confirms French origin or antiquity.
7. **Sternsinger:** Austrian and German evidence should not be expanded to all European Epiphany customs.
8. **Galette:** the Saturnalia explanation and universality of the poor-person's share remain uncertain.
9. **Corpus Christi decoration:** earliest origin varies by locality; processions can move locally and must be verified.
10. **Emmaus walk:** exact historical beginnings and Weiser's French/Canadian family-visit variant remain unverified.
11. **Mary Garden:** distinguish the modern garden movement from medieval Marian flower symbolism; plant legends remain variable.
