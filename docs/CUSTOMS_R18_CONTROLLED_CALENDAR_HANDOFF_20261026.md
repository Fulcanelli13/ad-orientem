## What can surface in the original Calendar — after individual human approval

This is only a **proposed classification**. Voluntary household practices can become regional, historically labelled day/season suggestions. Visiting customs, local charity drives and processions can be shown as contextual traditions without fabricating participating parishes or dates. Shared Eastern/Western or cross-confessional crafts must be labelled as such and may be appropriate only as heritage context. A dated 2026 event is evidence of that event, never an evergreen 1962 liturgical requirement.

The existing 1962 Calendar alone resolves feasts and mobile dates. Suggested folklore occasions neither change liturgical precedence nor authorize ministerial rituals. No local-event recurrence, notifications, procession schedule, geographic expansion, rite formula or public article is enabled by this packet.

1. **St Patrick crosses — source precision resolved:** The National Museum of Ireland explicitly states **1681** for Dineley's observation while Galway City Museum uses **1680s**. The direct audit's unsupported 'Tipperary informant' wording has been corrected. The school locality cannot establish the informant's residence without additional evidence; original Adare/Kerry references remain separate.

The older working-master `calendar_audit.local_event_required=false` needs reconciliation with the actual event-dependent claim.
