### Priority corrections and safety boundaries

**Irish Christmas candle.** The originally registered Dúchas page challenged automated access. A distinct accessible UCD Schools Collection account [from Graine school, County Kilkenny (informant's residence not established)](https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbes/4758582/4756445/4934128) independently recounts a lit candle in the window for Mary and Joseph on Christmas night. Add this witness to source provenance; do not turn folkloric spirit or guaranteed blessing beliefs into doctrine. Recheck original record before final signing.

**St John bonfire.** The [Bayonne diocesan announcement](https://diocese64.org/evenement/feu-de-la-saint-jean-a-oloron-sainte-marie/) is specifically **20 June 2026**, although the Nativity of the Baptist is 24 June. A Calendar suggestion may describe the cultural association but must not imply a perennial parish service on the feast itself.

**Polish and Ukrainian Easter eggs.** [UNESCO's pysanka entry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/pysanka-ukrainian-tradition-and-art-of-decorating-eggs-02134) explicitly says this is centuries-old family art with pre-Christian elements and participation regardless of religion; not a sacramental blessing and not exclusive to Latin Catholicism.

**Herbergsuche.** The [actual Großpetersdorf parish notice](https://www.pfarre-grosspetersdorf.at/pfarre/1131/aktuelles/article/12277.html) documents an Advent Marian-image household relay and short prayers. This supports the image-transfer action, **not** every Alpine dramatic inn-search performance.

**Provençal Christmas meals.** The [French Ministry's historical intangible-heritage dossier](https://www.culture.gouv.fr/Media/Thematiques/Patrimoine-culturel-immateriel/Files/Fiches-inventaire-du-PCI/Les-fetes-de-Noel-en-Provence) was checked in its original PDF page 4 (zero-based screenshot p. 3): the meatless *Gros Souper* precedes midnight and the richer *réveillon* follows. This is an observed regional cycle, not an implied exemption from any 1962 fasting discipline.

**St Barbara, Tivoli, Sassari and Bavarian Frauendreißiger.** Archdiocesan, diocesan, city, shrine or national heritage references directly describe these customs, but their locally advertised **2025/2026 programmes are historical instances**, not guaranteed annual local services. No new liturgical season, priestly formula or universal rite is inferred.

**Unverified subsidiary claims remain open.** The Polish 1930 Pentecost greenery archival photograph, long historical origin legends, initial regional boundaries for Polish/Ukrainian craft, and alternate linked pages have not all been inspected in this pass. They must be checked or qualified before full-article signoff.
