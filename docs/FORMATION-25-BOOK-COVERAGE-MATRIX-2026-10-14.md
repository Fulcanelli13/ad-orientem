## Weak clusters requiring sources before any certification

1. **Crisis in the Church (all 81):** original conciliar texts, pre- and postconciliar acts and legislation, documented histories of reform, SSPX and sedevacantist proponents in their own words, direct official responses. The 26 zero-candidate cases are especially concentrated in modern liturgical reconstruction, sedevacantism/SSPX, synodality, and modern governance.
2. **Sexual Ethics (150):** original sexual/marital moral sources (including *Casti connubii*, *Humanae vitae*, *Veritatis splendor*, *Familiaris consortio*, *Donum vitae*, *Dignitas personae*); credible empirical material and correctly represented opponents. The 35 zero-candidate questions concern gender and sex development, assisted reproduction, sexual coercion and abuse, and newer online/AI questions. This audit does **not** substitute historical treatments for safeguarding or medicine.
3. **Apologetics (60):** 59 have preliminary historical candidates, but direct-source corroboration is still missing. APOL-060 (Freemasonry and secret societies) has no suitable candidate assigned from this particular library. Judaism, Islam, biblical criticism, the Crusades and Inquisition require additional specialist scholarship and original opposing sources. Non-Catholic historical books B20–B25 are to be treated as independent perspectives, not accepted as current scholarly consensus.

## Verification and integration gate

For each canonical question: (1) preserve its existing owner and original wording, (2) verify a **specific chapter/passage** and publication edition, (3) establish whether it truly addresses the question, (4) verify claims against original authority and independent adversarial material, (5) correct only deficient answer/commentary paragraphs, preserving inline direct hyperlinks and EN/FR parity, (6) independently certify or keep review-pending. A secondary author cannot certify a magisterial statement merely by repeating it. Independently verify authorial quotations and opponent positions in their original context.
