## Closure workflow

1. **53 directly linked**: bring over text by existing ID/reference, never copy into another canonical article; verify individual paragraph citations against original-language passages, accuracy of real opponent attribution, bilingual prose and canonical/doctrinal claims. Fill uncovered portions of a dossier only after its existing draft has been audited.
2. **57 thematic candidates**: track each indicated A/C range or named research bank to a surviving source text. Promote only exact originals with provenance. If missing, keep the explicit SOURCE_HOLD and write a new question only as a clearly new commissioned item after duplicate-owner review.
3. **31 with no link**: reconcile real doctrinal teaching in St Pius X, Mass Formation, sacramental guides, PRAY, and live Apostolate. These are *not* 31 authenticated missing historical texts. Build an apologetic reply only where the existing owner does not already supply that distinct defensive argument.
4. **Release**: separate source validation from theological/canonical and native French approval; publish dossier by dossier after actual mobile and citation-link acceptance. Nothing in this register changes user-visible routing.
