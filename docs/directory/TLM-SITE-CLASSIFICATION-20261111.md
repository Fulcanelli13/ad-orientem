# TLM Atlas — source site-category census (11 November 2026)

This is a scoped, app-loaded classification census, **not** a claim that the
operator of every building has been independently verified. It preserves the
established CL2 physical-site identities and CL27 source-ministry relationships.

## Coverage

| Category | Physical sites | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |

Of 2,580, **2,161** carry a usable source-reported category and **419** remain
operator/category holds (74 directory-only, 345 unresolved). Separately, 1,335
distinct sites have existing source-ID exact ministry links for the 1,418
FSSP, ICKSP, SSPX, IBP and AASJMV CL27 records. These links do not certify
the current Mass or whole-building ownership.

The verified sources include 11 additional UK cathedral/monastery identities, notably Wrexham, Portsmouth, Leeds, Newcastle, Belmont Abbey, Tyburn, Farnborough, Douai, Prinknash, Buckfast and St Dominic’s Priory. These sources do not certify 1962 use at the sites.

`src/find/tlm-exact-venue-categories.js` applies the 63-item witness overlay

## Second exact-site classification batch

A further **26** exact-site records were added to the witness overlay:
14 diocesan/parish contexts, 7 religious houses, and 5 mixed/host venues.

The source types include local parishes, diocesan registries, a Dominican
province, the FSSP's official Mass-location records, and official university
chaplaincy and shrine pages. These are **venue-context classifications only**;
we do not infer that an institute owns every host church, nor that an advertised
Latin Mass uses a particular Missal edition.

Notable distinctions:

- **St Kevin's, Dublin** is a diocesan parish hosting the Latin Mass
  Chaplaincy and the Oratory in Formation. It is not flattened into any one
  organisation.
- **Fisher House** is the University of Cambridge Catholic chaplaincy.
  Neither its Sunday sung Latin Mass nor its student association makes it
  a traditional clerical society.
- **St Anthony's, Calgary** is a parish served by FSSP priests.
- **Saint-Irénée, Montréal** is a canonical archdiocesan nonterritorial
  mission entrusted to FSSP priests; the venue and ministry remain distinct.
- **Prandtauerkirche** is a Catholic rectoral church with an officially
  stated 1962-Missal programme. Its category is not proof of who currently
  celebrates any individual Mass.
- **Downside Abbey** remains held as a contemporary operator classification:
  the Abbey itself states that its Benedictine community has relocated.
  Historical monastery identity cannot establish current resident ministry.

The unresolved tally after this round is **356** (282 source-label holds
and 74 directory-only groups). The original site index and previously released
source-exact CL27 ministry liturgy remain unchanged. The three published
non-CL2 additional cards are also unchanged.

## Third exact-site classification batch — LMS source reconciliation

A further **28** LMS-discovered sites are individually matched to first-party
diocesan, parish, or institutional sources. This pass has **26 diocesan/parish
contexts, one multi-rite hosted parish, and one Institute-operated shrine**.

- **Withermarsh Green**: Diocese of East Anglia identifies a local 1962 Rite
  Community within Sudbury Parish. It is a diocesan setting, not an inferred
  autonomous institute.
- **New Brighton**: ICKSP explicitly operates the Shrine Church of SS Peter,
  Paul and Philomena. Its institute identity can guide the emblem lane, while
  legal building ownership is not asserted.
- **St Birinus, Dorchester-on-Thames**: Archdiocese of Birmingham explicitly
  describes its Sunday sung Latin Mass under the **1970 Missal**. The category
  must not generate a 1962 Mass or label every Latin liturgy traditional.
- **St Etheldreda, Ely Place**: the Westminster parish is served by the
  Rosminians; its official site explicitly says its Sunday sung Latin Mass is
  **Novus Ordo**.
- **St Thomas More, Iford**: diocesan parish, Ordinariate mission and older
  Latin Mass events coexist; the site is a **mixed/host venue**.
- **Isle of Wight**: St Mary Ryde, St Michael Bembridge, St David East Cowes,
  and St Thomas of Canterbury Cowes were placed in the August 2026 Holy Spirit
  parish merger; the building IDs remain unchanged.
- Other source-specific parish confirmations include Christ the King Bedford,
  OLEM Cambridge, St Anne Line, St John Vianney Clayhall, St Pancras Ipswich,
  St Joseph Basingstoke, Ss Joseph Patrick and Cuthbert Coxhoe, and more.

All 28 records retain their original physical CL2 identity and provenance.
No Mass occurrence, Missal, Una Cum, ownership or image is inferred or
changed in this pass. Exact evidence URLs are attached individually.

## Image and liturgical phases (not completed by this pass)

1. Assign the approved **church photograph** to diocesan-parish contexts,
   with an appropriate placeholder when a photograph is absent.
2. Assign the corresponding institution/society/community **emblem**, when
   identity evidence matches; otherwise use a neutral, appropriately
   labelled category mark. Mixed/hosted sites must not misrepresent ownership.
3. Use existing **source-ministry** liturgical norms for the FSSP, ICKSP, SSPX,
   IBP and AASJMV; preserve direct rite evidence for Dominican, other religious
   and exceptional liturgies. Do not infer Missal, una cum, rubrics or
   canonical standing from a generic category or third-party directory listing.

**Authority order**: exact celebration > explicit local ministry > exact
source-linked institutional norm. This source-label census sits beneath these
and never overwrites them.

## Implementation

- `data/directory/cl2-source-site-category-matrix-20261111.v1.json` is
  exhaustive across the currently frozen 140 literal affiliation labels.
- `src/find/tlm-site-category-associations.js` validates 2,580 rows,
  140 labels and exact source-ID joins, then adds `site_category` metadata.
- `src/find/preliminary-directory-r49.js` loads the manifest after
  institutional joins; old offline snapshots can omit it without failing.
- `tests/tlm-cl2-site-category-census.mjs` and `test:directory` enforce
  complete coverage, explicit holds and non-mutation of liturgical data.

No changes to the Excel master, CL2 anchors, map positions, public Mass
schedules, approved media or GitHub-only deployment.
