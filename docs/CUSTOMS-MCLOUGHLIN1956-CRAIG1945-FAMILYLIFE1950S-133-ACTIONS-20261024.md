## What improved in scope

The source intake deliberately shifts away from giant regional processions toward practices relevant to home and parish:

- **Easter:** Easter cross/wreath of victory, Christian egg decoration and Ukrainian pysanky, household Paschal and baptismal candles, bringing Easter water/fire home, home Easter altars, symbolic foods and prayers.
- **Ascension and Pentecost:** Ascension household reflection, triptych and images, seven-gifts symbolic roses, red candles, home Pentecost/Cenacle models, figures and family devotion.
- **All Souls and death:** soul cakes, visiting and tending graves, prayers before a funeral, Rosary during a wake, local cemetery processions and anniversaries, French family mourning and Louisiana grave care.
- **Other localized household customs:** domestic Nativity cribs, Christmas Christ candles, Slovak oblatky delivery, Sunday missal reading and home hospitality.

This does **not** assert that all described modern craft adaptations were historical regional practice. Source records distinguish recommendations and author's own examples from community or historical reports. Folk claims of magic or supernatural protection are recorded as historical beliefs, not endorsed.
