## Source rule
1962 Missale Romanum, Ritus servandus VIII.5: after the elevation of the Host, the priest does not separate his thumbs and forefingers except when touching/handling the consecrated Host; this discipline ends with washing of the fingers after Communion. Source: https://musicasacra.com/pdf/missale62.pdf

2. Ablution of fingers after Communion: thumb and forefinger washed with wine and water above the chalice; not the end of the Canon or the concluding doxology.
