## Corrections with original-text references

- **CSE029 — celibacy and psychological well-being:** [the 15-priest qualitative study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25617141/) describes both benefits and difficulties, not a population estimate or proof of causation. No claim that abstinence necessarily damages health or that every celibate is healthy.
- **CSE031 — engagement and marriage:** [CIC can. 1057](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann998-1165_en.html) specifies that spouses' consent **makes** the marriage; intention to marry is not consent that already establishes a valid bond. [*Persona Humana* §VII](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19751229_persona-humana_en.html) expressly considers even premarital relationships in which partners firmly intend to marry.
- **CSE035 — cohabitation:** [Pew 2019](https://www.pewresearch.org/social-trends/2019/11/06/why-people-get-married-or-move-in-with-a-partner/) gives 23% 'test relationship' among cohabiters and 38% financial and 37% convenience as major motives. These descriptive US survey findings **do not establish** that cohabitation improves outcomes. Chaste co-residence, sexual relations and scandal remain distinct judgments.
- **CSE038–040 — looking, flirting and pre-marriage intimacy:** distinguish involuntary attraction, deliberate lust and deception of an agreed exclusivity; remove gratuitous item-by-item sexual inventories from public argument. [*Persona Humana* §VII](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19751229_persona-humana_en.html) anchors the normative answer rather than a general sex-education website treated as opposition.
- **CSE043–045 — marriage and infertility:** [CIC can. 1084 §3](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann998-1165_en.html) explicitly says sterility does not bar or nullify marriage; it distinguishes this from the impediment of antecedent and perpetual impotence. [*Casti Connubii*](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-xi_enc_19301231_casti-connubii.html) preserves the traditional ends of marriage while recognizing spousal help. A Pew preference survey is a description of preferences, not a proponent's moral doctrine.
- **CSE049–050 — indissolubility, separation and remarriage:** [CIC can. 1141](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann998-1165_en.html) protects **ratified and consummated sacramental** marriage; canons **1142–1150** include narrowly specified canonical dissolutions of other cases. Civil divorce by itself does not establish nullity or freedom to remarry (can. 1085). **CIC can. 1153** permits safety-related separation, including an immediate departure where delay is dangerous. No victim is required to endure abuse to preserve marital cohabitation.
- **CSE054 — marital coercion:** [*Humanae Vitae* §13](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-vi_enc_25071968_humanae-vitae.html) explicitly rejects imposed intercourse ignoring a spouse's condition and reasonable wishes. Consent is not canceled by marriage; protection and can. 1153 remain relevant.
- **CSE071, 074, 076 — masturbation, pornography and private images:** [*Persona Humana* §IX](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19751229_persona-humana_en.html) distinguishes moral species and concrete subjective culpability. [CCC §2354](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_two/article_6/ii_the_vocation_to_chastity.html) gives the pornography definition, focused on displaying real or simulated sexual acts **to third parties**. It is not a precise blanket ruling on every kind of private spousal digital image; separately apply prudence, dignity and privacy.
