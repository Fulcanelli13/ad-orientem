## Editorial identity evidence that changes the image review

- **Loreto:** The present statue at the Santa Casa dates to **1922**, replacing the earlier statue lost in a 1921 fire. The Holy House, previous destroyed image, modern statue and reproductions cannot be treated as interchangeable. [Loreto sanctuary account](https://www.santuarioloreto.va/it/storia.html).
- **Rocamadour:** The sanctuary describes its venerated medieval dark wooden statue in the chapel. The existing guide-stop record names the chapel; the statue is a distinct depicted object. [Rocamadour sanctuary](https://www.sanctuairerocamadour.com/sanctuaire/vierge-noire/).
- **Walsingham:** The current Catholic Slipper Chapel statue dates from **1954**; it must not be confused with the destroyed medieval statue or the separate Anglican shrine. Review the sculptor’s underlying work rights. [Catholic Shrine](https://www.walsingham.org.uk/the-statue-of-our-lady-of-walsingham/) · [Catholic Bishops’ Conference](https://www.cbcew.org.uk/walsingham/).
- **Chartres:** Notre-Dame du Pilier is the named original sculptural devotional subject at the cathedral, distinct from Marian relics, Notre-Dame de Sous-Terre and reproductions. [Cathedral’s history](https://www.cathedrale-chartres.org/en/mediasrc/our-lady-of-the-pillar-part-2-2/).
- **Aparecida:** The original statue is in a precisely identified niche; contemporary architectural mosaics around it have separate potential artwork rights. [National shrine description](https://www.a12.com/santuario/circuito-de-visitacao/nicho).
- **Sainte-Anne-de-Beaupré:** The sanctuary’s site map lists a Saint Anne statue separately from its relics, while its heritage information also describes an **exterior** Saint Anne statue rescued from an earlier basilica. Source evidence does **not yet establish** which sculptural object the unpinned user preview depicts. [Sanctuary site map](https://sanctuairesainteanne.org/pages/carte-du-site) · [Façade history](https://sanctuairesainteanne.org/en/pages/facade).
- **Paray:** The custodian documents the precise Chapel of the Apparitions, distinct from the saint’s shrine/reliquary and artworks inside the sanctuary. [Paray source](https://sacrecoeur-paray.org/lieux/la-chapelle-apparitions/).
- **Banneux:** The custodian identifies the 1933 Chapel of the Apparitions as the chapel of the Virgin of the Poor. The proposed Commons file is archival postcard evidence, **not** a modern site photograph. [Banneux chapel history](https://banneux-nd.be/en/chapel-of-apparitions/).
- **La Salette:** The original sanctuary is at **La Salette-Fallavaux in Isère**, whereas the previously associated Commons memorial photograph is of **La Rabatelière in Vendée**. Never move a file solely because both sites have the same dedication. [Actual sanctuary](https://lasalette.cef.fr/lhistoire/) · [French heritage authority identifying the different memorial](https://pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/merimee/PA85000012).
