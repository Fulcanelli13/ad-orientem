## Critical editorial boundaries

- Gangi's origins story (papal consultation and image discovery) is explicitly **oral legend**; its claimed history must not be certified as papal authority or archival fact.
- Gratteri's claim of unusually rapid regrowth after a procession and St Francis/St James relationship are **popular narratives**.
- Geraci's olive-branch holy-water treatment of prepared dairy paste and distributing supposedly blessed cheese require **independent checking of minister, rite and period**. A reported 'talisman' is not authorized Catholic sacramental efficacy.
- Pitrè is a *collector* of others' testimonies too. A local correspondent or the older cited Filippini text is not counted as directly inspected independent source. Pitrè's references back to his **1881** book are same-author dependencies.
- Marineo's multi-day festival, costumed angel flight, alternative cart/Dimostranza, emblematic confraternities and diaspora banner belong to a single historic **festival complex**, not dozens of proven independent customs. A historically shifted August date is not instruction for the existing Calendar.
