## Corrections already made

**APOL-003 — Evil:** The old `documented_position` was Augustine's *Enchiridion*, a Catholic explanation rather than the opponent to the question. It now accurately attributes the real problem-of-evil argument to Hume's *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion*, Part X, and responds to natural evil, animal suffering and the limits of a free-will argument. Original opponent: https://davidhume.org/texts/d/10

**APOL-004 — Miracles:** The former canonical synthesis had shifted to Fatima's third secret. A four-part answer to the actual miracles question now uses Hume's *Enquiry*, Section X, the Catechism §156 on *motiva credibilitatis*, and the Holy See's Compendium on Christ's miracles. Fatima stays in the **original recovered research anchors**, without posing as the principal philosophical objection. Originals: https://davidhume.org/texts/e/10 and https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_one/section_one/chapter_three/article_1/iii_the_characteristics_of_faith.html

**APOL-010 — Trinity and Incarnation:** The former discussion accurately concerned Nicene anti-Arian doctrine but gave insufficient distinct treatment to the Incarnation. It now cites the Council of Chalcedon's 451 definition on two natures of the one divine Son, keeping Nicaea and Chalcedon conceptually distinct. Council original: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3811.htm

All three corrections are bilingual, have claim-linked registry URLs, preserve canonical IDs and prior research attachments, and remain explicitly **unapproved drafts**. User-facing traditional commentary was scrubbed of editorial meta-discussion.

**APOL-004** — Corrected scope: former four-part answer concerned Fatima's third secret rather than possibility/credibility of miracles. Main debate now cites Hume's original sceptical argument and Catechism §156; original Fatima research links remain intact.

**APOL-006** — The documented-position section quotes a Catholic rebuttal of pluralism, not the actual pluralist interlocutor. Attribute a genuine opposing religious or philosophical original, without flattening distinct religions.

**APOL-008** — Jehovah's Witness original source and John 1/20 are linked. Independently verify exact Witness position, Greek grammatical claims and distinctions from ancient Arianism before approving.

**APOL-011** — Dominus Iesus is a Catholic rejection of religious pluralism, not the pluralist's own argument. Identify source of the opposing claim and differentiate ordinary non-Christian belief from equivalence theory.

**APOL-012** — Complex Justin Martyr/Septuagint charge is a historically sourced allegation, not proof of intentional Jewish falsification. Requires passage-by-passage original textual witness review and strict prohibition on collective accusation.

**APOL-017** — Irenaeus's warning about bad successors is an internal Catholic qualification rather than an opponent's challenge from scandal. Add verifiable external accusation with responsible attribution; maintain victim-centred correction.

**APOL-020** — Vatican I ex cathedra conditions and Vatican II non-definitive teaching distinction are drawn. Verify exact distinctions and magisterial degrees; avoid conflating infallibility with administrative impeccability.

**APOL-021** — Vincent of Lérins supplies a Catholic development criterion, not opposing side. Add a documented rupture/progressive or Protestant argument where an actual controversy is claimed; preserve same sense and meaning.

**APOL-022** — Leo XIII original Apostolicae curae primary text confirms matter/form/intention argument. Anglican Saepius officio is correctly treated as genuine response; examine exact Anglican text/translation and exceptional orders separately.

## Why this is not yet certified

The next pass should concentrate on the authentic-opposition deficiencies (APOL-001, 005, 006, 007, 011, 015, 017, 019, 021), then review high-risk original doctrinal/historical arguments (APOL-002, 008, 012, 020, 022), and independently assess the three revised drafts. Work continues in large batches; no new Apologetics registry, duplicated Crisis debates or unsourced user-facing notes.
