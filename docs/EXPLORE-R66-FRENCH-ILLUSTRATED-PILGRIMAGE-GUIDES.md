# Explore R66: first three illustrated French shrine guides

This pass expands the existing Destinationes-inspired Ad Orientem Place reader to three major French sites with **already approved local photography**: Lourdes, Paray-le-Monial and Sainte-Anne-d'Auray.

## Editorial additions
- Lourdes: Bernadette's eighteen apparitions from February to July 1858, episcopal inquiry/recognition of 18 January 1862, the Grotto and basilicas, sick and disabled pilgrims, Rosary, confession and processions. Historical anniversaries are distinct from current seasonal schedules. Original sources: sanctuary Lourdes France.
- Paray-le-Monial: chapel of the Visitation, Saint Margaret Mary's 1673–1675 Sacred Heart accounts, historic choir, prayer and reparative devotion, and the **concluded** 2023–2025 jubilee. Original sources: Sacré-Cœur de Paray sanctuary.
- Sainte-Anne-d'Auray: 1623–1625 Nicolazic apparition and statue traditions, first pilgrimage Mass on 26 July 1625, early chapel and Carmelites, revolutionary rupture and the Breton pardon; current-year events remain independently checkable. Original source: sanctuary historical timeline.

Each guide provides original-custodian-cited English and French historical sections, inner-site devotional stops, anchored historical timeline, relevant feast/programme dates and official visitor links.

## Scope and safeguards
Separate France corpus in `data/shrines/illustrated-place-guides-france.v1.json`. The standard loader combines this with the already published **five Irish guides**, without importing anything into the Mass, Calendar or Biblical Atlas pipeline. No map pins, official classifications, 20-featured choices, approved photographs or shrine/pilgrimage counts are modified. Historical anniversaries are never advertised as a current event schedule. New remote hero photography and artwork remain subject to an explicit image rights, provenance and crop review.
