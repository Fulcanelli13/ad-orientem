# Sacred Places P25 — 151-row source-screen and refreshed 500-lead queue

_Date: 24 October 2026. Research only; no canonical publication._

### Why this pass differs from small release batches

P22/P24 already placed 26 new physical sites in the live master and avoided duplicating Covadonga. P25 addresses the **remaining 151 candidates** in the P21 first-200 lane and updates the **500-lead acquisition queue** against the current merged 387-Place/685-source registry.

The source sweep inspected **20 new source excerpts** (direct church/diocesan or precise civic sources, plus two indirect references) and left the remaining 131 explicitly unreviewed, rather than asserting that automated classification verifies original pages. P21's earlier 49 plus P25's 20 now provide **69 of the first 200 candidates with some individually read source evidence**. Source evidence has variable strength, and none of the 20 new candidates is certified for publication or exact building geometry.

The two weaker cases are Albanian Our Lady of Good Counsel (independent ecclesiastical catalogue, requires competent Catholic custodian source) and Mariamabad (Catholic aid organisation refers to the shrine, not enough for physical unit verification). None of these gets a new map pin.

Country diversity is maintained in the 500-candidate queue. Candidates admitted in P22/P24 and the pre-existing Covadonga chapel/basilica are marked already represented, not rescheduled as missing sites.

### Remaining physical-release gates

A candidate must match a distinct physically mapped Catholic building or accurately typed site, with attribution and reliable coordinate precision. Do not infer one building from whole pilgrimage complexes, old/new sanctuaries or free-text title resemblances. Continue 100–200 evidence screening and 300–500 source lead acquisition. Later qualified publications use only the P22 minimum (one identity source and exact geo provenance); optional Mass Times links, biographies and image rights stay separate.
