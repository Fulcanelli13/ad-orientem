## Corrective boundaries
The canonical alias families are keyed exclusively by observed IDs, not search strings, translated names or a hardcoded civil feast date. Saturday BVM additionally verifies that the resolved date is actually a Saturday; the actual selected Common Mass remains unchanged. They change neither observed day source identity nor active Proper text, Mass rank/colour, reading sequence or commemorations. **Only the Calendar’s visible title changes.** EN/FR output and failure/unknown fail-closed behavior are contract tested. The special 2026 Pentecost Vigil composition and 2024/2027 datum are not conflated. Source witnesses: [2027 GCatholic](https://gcatholic.org/calendar/2027/Extraordinary-en), [FSSPX 1962 St Bibiana](https://ordo.fsspx.org/day/2026-12-02?lang=en), [Una Voce St Paul](https://unavocelacrosse.org/feasts/commemoration-of-st-paul/).

No implementation of the explicit II-class violet Rogation Mass is implied ([#714](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/issues/714), [#718](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/issues/718)). ## Canonical source-week lineage of the 55 generic ferias

The [2024/2027 full-year reconciliation worklist](../data/calendar/1962-editorial-reconciliation-2024-2027.v1.json) records **29** dates in 2024 and **26** in 2027. **53** of the 55 have one matching IV-class `tempora:Epi*/Quadp*/Pasc*/Pent*` source weekday ID. Their English/French Calendar headlines derive the weekday and Sunday family from that observed identity, with a precise weekday/date consistency guard. They do **not** derive the Proper from a translated-title regex, turn a weekday into a Sunday, or change the Mass text.

The other **two dates** — **7 and 8 January 2027** — have no Temporale candidate. The pinned `DayResolver.inferObservance` explicitly walks backward to the **6 January Epiphany** principal observation. The projected label requires a successfully resolved **`Sancti/01-06` inherited Mass Proper**, an IV-class white observed feria and the Jan 7–12 rubrical period; **date alone is insufficient**. Compare [Missale Online](https://missale.online/festkalender/en/2027/druck) (*Weekday after Epiphany*) and [GCatholic](https://gcatholic.org/calendar/2027/Extraordinary-en) (Epiphany feria).

**Six dates** near November 2024/2027 inherit a `tempora:Epi4-*` source weekday when an impeded Epiphany Sunday is assigned after XXIII Sunday after Pentecost by [1960 General Rubrics, n.18](https://isidore.co/divinum/www/horas/Help/Rubrics/General%20Rubrics.html). Labels acknowledge the *transferred Fourth Sunday after Epiphany* and avoid falsely treating it as an ordinary January week or inventing the variable Pentecost-week number from the raw weekday ID.

The regression gates check all 55 actual DayResolver dates, both languages, exact canonical weekday/proper identities, resumed-source cases, false-positive guards, and continued rank/colour/commemoration ownership. **This certifies the bounded headline-projection rules, not the 731 Mass Propers or local particular calendars.** [Implementation PR #764](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/pull/764).
