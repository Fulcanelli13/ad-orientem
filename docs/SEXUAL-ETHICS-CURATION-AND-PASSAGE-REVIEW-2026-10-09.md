## Further original-text check — avoiding pregnancy and moral means

- [Pius XII, Address to Italian Midwives (29 October 1951), original Italian](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/it/speeches/1951/documents/hf_p-xii_spe_19511029_ostetriche.html): the paragraphs beginning *Si presenta inoltre oggigiorno* and *Occorre innanzi tutto considerare due ipotesi* distinguish naturally infertile intercourse from deliberately contracepted acts, and require serious grounds for the **habitual restriction** of relations to infertile periods, including situations lasting a long time or throughout marriage. The address does not supply a universally applicable diagnostic checklist or certify any specific family's circumstances.
- [Paul VI, *Humanae Vitae* §10](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-vi_enc_25071968_humanae-vitae.html): the paragraph headed “Responsible Parenthood” addresses physical, economic, psychological, and social conditions together with **serious reasons** and observance of moral precepts. §11 affirms legitimate intercourse need not produce conception, while §16 distinguishes recourse to infertile periods from contraception. The English/French answers and source locators for **CSE062/CSE065/CSE066** have been tightened accordingly. These scoped checks do not certify their complete debates.

The 1975 declaration directly addresses sexual union before marriage; traditional technical distinctions require their own exact manual and scholastic collation.

Any device-specific conclusion and any claim of disagreement among theologians **still requires exact original-passage proof**, particularly in English/French pastoral presentation. Visibility removal is not certification.
4. **CSE056 — opponent attribution:** Neither a general consent webpage nor material on consensual non-monogamy automatically verifies the words put in a reconstructed opponent's mouth. Preserve the distinction between a fair illustrative objection and original advocacy.
5. All 55 original debate records and all 440 source-review stages retain their previous *not yet certified* flags. Do **not** interpret a citation URL, excerpt, or these six scoped passages as proof of every stage or as approval for publication.
