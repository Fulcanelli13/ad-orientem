## Source-critical flags

1. Cosmas and Damian processions include a claimed curative effect of water after carrying jugs (source OCR ~4910): **folk testimony, not proof of sacramental blessing or healing**.
2. Touching cloths to statues (source OCR ~5066) must **not** be converted to a Roman Ritual text or ecclesiastical authorization.
3. The reported Palermo 1748 monastery procession privilege and Piazza Armerina Marian banner finding rely on **prior authors/documents not read in this pass**.
4. Palermo's 1890s festival restorations, changes in routes and changing dates are **historical-period variants**, not present-day calendar instructions.
5. The Assumption's grand festival at Piazza Armerina had irregular multi-year recurrence in the historical report; do not impose an annual grand celebration from the date alone.
