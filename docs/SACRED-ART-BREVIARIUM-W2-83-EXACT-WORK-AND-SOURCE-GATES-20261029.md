## Crucial exact-work corrections

1. **Champaigne — Breviarium's Last Supper is La Grande Cène**, Louvre **INV 1124**, *not* user-preferred **La Petite Cène** INV 1125. The two are different physical oils. No portrait photo or title-based import must substitute the Grande Cène for the approved Petite. [Breviarium/WGA original file metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Philippe_de_Champaigne_-_The_Last_Supper_-_WGA4710.jpg) · [Louvre Petite Cène](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010062076). The Petite has an alternate 2,816 × 2,112 Commons photograph, but its photographic edge/colour/rights state is *not certified*.
2. **Zurbarán — Breviarium's tiny 400×554 St Francis is Timken Museum accession 2015:001**, not National Gallery London **NG230** or **NG5655**. The Timken original has a separate [2,847 × 3,688 Commons museum photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saint_francis_in_meditation_075217.JPG), which must undergo full frame/colour and individual-photo rights review before any archive promotion.
3. **La Tour — The Newborn, Rennes INV 794.2.6**. The source image is 4,075×3,357 but the French object record credits the photograph to the Museum of Fine Arts of Rennes. A Commons PD-Art tag alone cannot override a conflicting institutional photo-rights notice. [French national museum record](https://pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/joconde/00000075381).
4. **Pereda — Saint Anthony with Christ Child**: existing source only 973×842; the [WGA catalogue](https://www.wga.hu/html/p/pereda/index.html) locates the physical original at the **Museum of Fine Arts Budapest**. No different painting may be used merely because its title looks identical.
5. **Ribera — St Jerome and the Angel of Judgement**: the source file corresponds to Capodimonte, Naples, 2,075×3,344, with JPEG camera/edit metadata. [Original Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jos%C3%A9_de_Ribera_016.jpg). Verify image edge, glare, tonal integrity and real photographer provenance before promotion.
6. **Reni — St Joseph and Child**: museum partner catalogue identifies the **Rienzi collection, MFA Houston**. [Official partner record](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/saint-joseph-and-the-christ-child-guido-reni/HgH0SCjz6vSFVQ). The Breviarium Commons original is 1,994×2,401, but its reproduction fidelity is not certified.
7. **Poussin — Translation of St Rita**: exact object is Dulwich Picture Gallery **DPG263**. [Museum original](https://www.dulwichpicturegallery.org.uk/explore/explore-the-collection/the-translation-of-saint-rita-of-cascia/). It has a large Commons image that requires frame/rights inspection.
8. **Caravaggio — Flagellation**: Capodimonte owns the exact original; distinguish from Caravaggio's separate *Christ at the Column*. [Museum work context](https://capodimonte.cultura.gov.it/mostra/capodimonte-doppio-caravaggio-lecce-homo-e-la-flagellazione-di-cristo/). Wikimedia full-size retrieval previously HTTP 429; this is no reason to substitute low-res content.
9. **Caravaggio — Madonna of the Rosary**: this work is a contextual candidate, not a replacement for the user's **approved Lotto Madonna del Rosario** in Prayer.
10. **Rembrandt — Return of the Prodigal Son**: distinct from Murillo's approved Confession module original. Its >250 MB Commons source is metadata-only under the €0 source-archive policy.

## Operational decision

The next acquisition should **not repeat source discovery for these 83**. First reuse physically archived exact-work originals where available. For the 35 small source files, locate genuinely different *museum-authored/free-use* photographs and verify exact inventory IDs. For the 28 quality-eligible files, perform native full-field and corner QA, then distinguish photo-rights evidence from visual acceptance. For oversize masters, preserve only provenance until a truly durable, free source archive is verified.

This research batch adds **83 explicit per-work dispositions and 10 institutional leads**, repairs two crucial physical-identity misconceptions, and preserves both earlier Caravaggio originals. It has **zero additional photo downloads**, **zero** pristine final certifications and **zero** app activations.
