# Explore R65 — remaining three Irish pilgrimage Places

## Publication
Three site-verified physical Places and fully bilingual source-backed long-form guides are added on top of the existing Knock and Lough Derg pilots. The Ireland catalogue now holds five illustrated-guide profiles, 223 physical places globally (up from 220), 219 shrine/site records (up from 216), 209 pilgrimages (up from 206) and 366 original registered sources (up from 355). No featured destination has been promoted automatically.

### Croagh Patrick
The published pin is **Sport Ireland's Murrisk car park, 53.77811, -9.639078**, explicitly *not* a mountain summit marker. The Murrisk Development Association is the primary authority for the traditional Reek Sunday observance and local safety guidance. Its last-Sunday-in-July tradition yields **26 July 2026**, carefully labelled *calendar-derived* and not a current Mass schedule. The guided mountain ascent is weather-dependent and potentially strenuous.

### Saint Oliver Plunkett, Drogheda
The published pin is **Saint Peter's Catholic Church, West Street, 53.7149, -6.35242** from the regional tourism authority. Original Armagh archdiocesan sources establish the shrine, the 1681 martyrdom, the 1921 installation of the head relic, the 1975 canonisation and the 1995 reliquary shrine. The historical feast of **1 July** is not presented as a confirmed 2026 programme.

### Saint Brigid, Kildare
The published pin is **Solas Bhríde Centre and Hermitages, Tully Road, 53.14821, -6.90623** (original Solas Bhríde address cross-checked against an attributed OSM node). Pilgrim stops at the **Catholic St Brigid parish**, **Saint Brigid's wells**, and **historic Church of Ireland cathedral** are distinct and remain *unpinned*, pending separate physical-site and custodianship review. Under no circumstances may the current Anglican cathedral be claimed to provide Catholic Mass. The confirmed 1 September 2026 walking event is a historical past programme rather than a recurring timetable.
