## Other verified answers correctly retained

- **CSE131–135:** [1983 Code of Canon Law cann. 979, 987–988](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann959-997_en.html) directly supports kind/number of unconfessed grave sins and discretion by confessors. Reasonable estimation where count is genuinely unknown is pastoral explanation, not an extra legal quotation. A firm purpose of amendment is not certainty about future perfection.
- **CSE139–140:** [canon 916](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann879-958_en.html) has the stated narrow exception requiring grave reason, lack of opportunity to confess, perfect contrition and intention to confess soon; correctly identified.
- **CSE126–130:** Current answers distinguish online pornography, persistent personal images and AI-mediated sexual stimulation without inventing an official ruling covering every digital technology. No universal clinical predictions or external harms are asserted without context.
- **CSE136–138:** Keep involuntary thoughts distinct from freely given consent and avoid encouraging cycles of scrupulous self-accusation. Pastoral advice is not medical diagnosis.
- **CSE141:** The [original 2013 Spadaro interview](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/speeches/2013/september/documents/papa-francesco_20130921_intervista-spadaro.html) does say Francis rejected emphasis on abortion, same-sex marriage and contraception divorced from pastoral context, **without abolishing** teaching. Existing summary accurate.
- **CSE143–150:** Existing answers distinguish moral guilt from personal worth, objective acts from subjective culpability, medical facts from ethical premises, and express a positive Catholic vision of chastity rather than exclusively prohibitions.
- **CR-GOV-05:** [*Apostolos suos*, complementary norms arts. 1–2](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/motu_proprio/documents/hf_jp-ii_motu-proprio_22071998_apostolos-suos.html) expressly specifies unanimous approval *or* two-thirds plenary approval with apostolic recognitio for conference declarations to constitute authentic Magisterium; commissions lack independent teaching authority.
- **CR-GOV-07:** [Canons 915–916](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann879-958_en.html) differ in addressee and public/private criteria. Ratzinger's dated 2004 memorandum does not legislate blanket Communion refusals by political-party membership.
- **CR-GOV-08:** The 2009 Murphy Commission's findings concerning failure to prioritize the protection of children, concealment and institution reputation are reproduced in the [contemporaneous Irish parliamentary record](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/debate/dail/2009-12-03/10/); testimony is specifically about Dublin, not automatically every diocese.
- **CR-GOV-10:** The [original 2023 five-cardinal dubium and Francis response](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_risposta-dubia-2023_en.html) are authentic distinct voiced positions. Consultation does not by itself replace the Pope's and bishops' actual legal jurisdiction.
