**The four territories are work units, not a certified census of four complete printed paragraphs.** The earlier Phase 6 statement that “two complete paragraphs” on page 257 had been extracted was wrong. One complete preliminary paragraph plus one paragraph-opening fragment is the supported result. Our corpus count remains **four selected historical excerpts over three printed pages**, none granted publication status, and **zero fully proofread whole printed pages**.

The upper running headings `CANON 257 CANON`, the signature `III.—17` at bottom left and the page number are **print furniture, not body text**. The bottom signature is **not a theological footnote**. No independently numbered footnote apparatus was observed on this page, but inline bibliographical citations and an attributed Latin quotation must be preserved.
