## Critical wrong-image protections

- Juan Ribalta's **Mark and Luke**, Prado P002965, is a different painting from **Matthew and John**, Prado P001065. Search thumbnails for the latter are not an aesthetic review of the former.
- Ingres's Metropolitan Museum *Virgin Adoring the Host* object 438434 is not the same painting as the famous Orsay variant. No visual score assigned on a mismatched museum preview.
- Céline Martin's exact 1925 oil of Thérèse must not be scored from her earlier sepia/drawn devotional likeness or from retouched historic photographs.
- Rubens's authentic **All Saints** is a small, loosely painted preparatory sketch. Important historical research, but not automatically the right monumental homepage masterpiece.
- Tintoretto's **Christ before Pilate** is thematically relevant to Christ the King, but not the specific appointed John 18 Gospel episode.
