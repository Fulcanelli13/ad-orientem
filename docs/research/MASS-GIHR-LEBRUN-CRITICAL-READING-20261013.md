# Roman Mass and Scripture — Gihr, Le Brun and Beaulac access assessment
13 October 2026. Research only in PR #1082; no Mass UI, Scripture registry, Atlas or Sacred Art changes.

## Access quality correction

Gihr/Moccand 1895 Volume II has a complete 478-page online facsimile index. Many indexed individual pages do NOT have published proofread transcriptions; the link opens a new-page editor containing uncorrected OCR. I read its OCR fields for the pages below. These are NOT facsimile-verified quotations or publication certification. Likewise, some Le Brun 1843 pages have raw OCR; other pages are proofread. Preserve scan-image collation as a later step.

Beaulac, Toute la Messe par questions et réponses: [the full PDF](https://www.liberius.net/livres/Toute_la_Messe_par_questions_et_reponses_000001333.pdf) is indexed but a direct download attempt and browser opening both failed. A separate public [searchable excerpt](https://fr.scribd.com/document/715335231/Toute-La-Messe-Par-Questions-Et-Reponses-000001333) reveals that the revised copy carries 1943 ecclesiastical approbation alongside a 1941 copyright notice. This is not confirmation that the edition linked at Saint Libère was actually read. Beaulac stays a promising catechetical source, not a cited primary explanation in this batch.

## Gihr primary-author OCR passages examined

1. **Kyrie**, [printed p.25](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/37), [p.26](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/38). Gihr presents repetitive supplication for mercy, early variability and later ninefold form. He then separately supplies a Trinitarian interpretation, citing Aquinas III, q.83 a.4. His proposed development date is an authorial historical claim, not a directly verified Roman Ordo. Do not present later symbolism as the historical invention of the rite.
2. **Gloria**, [p.30](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/42), [p.31](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/43), [p.45](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/57). Gihr explicitly separates the angelic words at Bethlehem from the rest of the developed liturgical hymn; he acknowledges uncertainty of authorship and describes Greek antecedents and change in liturgical use. His pre-1962 eligibility discussion cannot govern the 1962 Mass or fix modern feast exceptions.
3. **Collect and Lessons**, [p.46](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/58), [p.79](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/91). Gihr describes changing Collects and the dignity of inspired readings. His citations of Psalms, Judith, Ecclesiasticus, Romans and Peter in these explanatory pages belong to HIS interpretation of prayer and Scripture, not to the literal Proper texts for every liturgical date. Never convert commentary references into selected Mass readings.
4. **Offertory anticipatory sacrifice language**, [p.145](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/157). Gihr explains the early use of words such as immaculate host in anticipation of Consecration, and rejects treating the preliminary offering of bread and wine as a distinct essential Eucharistic sacrifice. This resolves an interpretative problem much better than falsely finding an Old Testament ritual identical to the entire Offertory.
5. **Preparation and Chalice**, [p.166](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/178), [p.173](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/185). Gihr describes handling/preparation of bread and wine and the offering of the chalice. Older Cluniac bread-preparation customs are historical illustrations, not normative 1962 rubrics.
6. **Self-oblation of priest and faithful**, [p.180](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/192), [p.183](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/195). Gihr explains interior self-offering united to Christ's sacrifice and cites Romans 12:1. This is a source-attested doctrinal relation; Romans 12:1 is NOT the text of Orate fratres or Suscipe sancte Pater. Treat Gihr's subsequent epiclesis explanation historically and avoid conflating it with the Roman consecratory formula.
7. **Lavabo**, [p.194](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/206). Gihr identifies practical/suitability and moral-purification explanations for washing; he offers historical antiquity claims that require corroboration. Compare with [Vulgate Psalm 25](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/psa025.htm) for the actual Psalm wording and with O'Connell for the 1962 ceremonial requirements.

## Le Brun original French author's argument

Le Brun's [1843 volume-I extracted p.13](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Lebrun_-_Explication_litt%C3%A9rale_historique_et_dogmatique_des_pri%C3%A8res_et_des_c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monies_de_la_messe_-_Tome_1_(1843).djvu/60) joins his understanding of Malachi 1:11, Psalm 39 and Augustine to the Eucharistic fulfilment of Old Testament sacrifices. This is Le Brun's theological exegesis, NOT a claim that the Bible gives precise directions for 1962 Offertory rubrics. [p.21](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Lebrun_-_Explication_litt%C3%A9rale_historique_et_dogmatique_des_pri%C3%A8res_et_des_c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monies_de_la_messe_-_Tome_1_(1843).djvu/68) discusses the Eucharistic sacrifice under praise, thanksgiving, propitiation and petition. His [original preface p.xv](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Lebrun_-_Explication_litt%C3%A9rale_historique_et_dogmatique_des_pri%C3%A8res_et_des_c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monies_de_la_messe_-_Tome_1_(1843).djvu/30) opposes the reduction of ritual gestures to mere theatrical reenactment. These are distinct from the 1962 Missal's legislative authority.

## French research syntheses (drafted for internal author review only)

**Offertoire.** À l'Offertoire, le pain et le vin sont mis à part pour la consécration. Quand les prières parlent déjà de l'Hostie immaculée, elles anticipent le sacrifice eucharistique qui va être accompli. La présentation des espèces ne constitue pas, à elle seule, un autre sacrifice eucharistique indépendant. Les fidèles sont appelés à s'unir intérieurement à l'offrande du Christ : Romains 12,1 en éclaire la dimension spirituelle, sans reproduire les paroles liturgiques. [Gihr p.145](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/157), [p.180](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/192), [p.183](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu/195).
**Tradition et symbolisme.** Le Brun lit les sacrifices de l'ancienne Loi à la lumière du sacrifice de Jésus-Christ, tout en avertissant contre des interprétations qui transforment chaque geste de la Messe en épisode dramatique de la Passion. Les scènes bibliques peuvent éclairer la Messe, mais n'expliquent pas automatiquement l'origine historique de chaque cérémonie. [Le Brun p.13](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Lebrun_-_Explication_litt%C3%A9rale_historique_et_dogmatique_des_pri%C3%A8res_et_des_c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monies_de_la_messe_-_Tome_1_(1843).djvu/60), [préface](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Lebrun_-_Explication_litt%C3%A9rale_historique_et_dogmatique_des_pri%C3%A8res_et_des_c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monies_de_la_messe_-_Tome_1_(1843).djvu/30).

## Decisions

- Keep Gihr 1895 volume II as a major accessible theological/historical author, but label the scanned index and uncorrected OCR accurately.
- Keep Le Brun as selective evidence for sacrificial doctrine, historical criticism and limits of allegory, not as a compulsory commentary for every prayer.
- Retain Beaulac in research intake until an actual French full-text passage can be read independently, not just a searchable excerpt.
- Preserve the 1962 Missal and O'Connell as distinct rubrical authorities, Crampon/Clementine as edition-aware biblical texts, and Gihr/Le Brun as explanatory/historical authors.
- No printed-1962 word-for-word certification, no app publication, no artificial 48-card links, no invented Bible theatre positions or artworks.
