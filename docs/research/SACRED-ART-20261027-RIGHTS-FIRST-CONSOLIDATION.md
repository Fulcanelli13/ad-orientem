## First rights review — authoritative published terms, not legal signoff
- **Clearer published zero-fee terms:** The Met designated OA images are CC0 and available even commercially without requesting permission; National Gallery of Art (Washington) open images likewise explicitly allow commercial/noncommercial use without fees; Cleveland OA artwork images are CC0. **Still verify that the exact image for the approved accession is designated OA**, retain its file URL/checksum, and perform visual QA. [Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/de/policies/frequently-asked-questions-image-and-data-resources) · [NGA](https://www.nga.gov/terms-and-notices) · [Cleveland](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access).
- **Commons PD-Art is not universal global clearance.** It supports hosting of faithful 2D public-domain copies under US policy; national photography protections and source site contract issues may differ. The EU DSM Directive art. 14 generally prevents new related rights in faithful reproductions of public-domain visual art in Member States except sufficiently original new photographs, but this does not automatically reconcile every source contract, third-party right or global app distribution. Use a documented file-specific, jurisdiction-aware evidence assessment. [Commons guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reuse_of_PD-Art_photographs) · [EU art. 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32019L0790).
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA are usable zero-cost licenses with conditions, not equivalent to CC0.** Preserve photographer attribution, source and license URLs, and derivative modification notice; consider ShareAlike when adaptations are distributed. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) · [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode.en).
- **No noncommercial-only/no-derivatives fallback** for an application needing flexible worldwide distribution and image resizing/cropping. Do not treat CC BY-NC-ND as an unrestricted hero-image licence.

## QA after rights
Physical original ID / owner / accession -> source photograph or API file & license evidence -> byte SHA256 & source size -> no frame / full painted surface -> 100% image-quality review (skin/faces, shadows, craquelure vs noise, faithful museum colour) -> desktop/phone contain mode and optional curator-approved crop -> permanent genuinely €0 archive -> publication QA and rollback. Preserve prior permanent SHA collections and checked files; never count expiring 7-day workflow artifacts as durable originals. A licensed photo can still fail quality, and a beautiful preview can still fail rights.
