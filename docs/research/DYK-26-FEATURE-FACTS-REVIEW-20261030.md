# Ad Orientem · Did You Know? — 26 editorial candidate review sheets

**Source-reviewed draft copy — NOT approved, NOT published.** Prepared 30 October 2026 in draft [PR #2096](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/pull/2096).

This review deliberately chooses **18 Weiser historical observations** and **eight Pitrè festival stories**, at most one featured Pitrè story per regional festival family. These are suggested editorial treatments of previously recorded research, not 26 new independent historical customs or an already deployed deck.

The existing Home DYK mechanism already has its own header; therefore the **EN** and **FR** sentences below are affirmative body copy, without repeating “Did you know that…”. The separate source record IDs are retained so reviewers can trace and challenge each sentence.

**For each ID:** mark *retain*, *revise* or *defer* in a PR comment. A source-page check is not an independent historical corroboration; the published rite, modern continuity, precise scope, French style and final Home deep links require separate review.
