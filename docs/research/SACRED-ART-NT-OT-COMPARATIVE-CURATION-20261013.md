## Core artistic selection conclusions

1. **Best ready-to-compare acquired works:** Bourdon *Baptism* (`met-435755`), Maratti *Flight into Egypt* (`met-816629`), Bassano *Miraculous Draught* (`nga-96688`), Luti *Samaritan Woman* (`met-702752`) and Velázquez *Supper at Emmaus* (`met-437871`). Existing SHA-256 originals are in the archive, but the source photograph still requires full-resolution and phone test for release.
2. **Best additional institutionally identified NT oil paintings:** Poussin Met *Saints Peter and John Healing the Lame Man* (Acts 3), Pynas Met *Paul and Barnabas at Lystra* (Acts 14) and Rembrandt's Hermitage *Return of Prodigal Son*. These need a correct source master and rights certification; external image searches are not substitutions.
3. **Historically important but composition/format limited:** Raphael's V&A painted cartoon *Christ's Charge to Peter* (John 21) is art-historically outstanding and should serve two currently duplicate John 21 subject slots by one image. Tintoretto's vast Prado *Washing of Feet* is panoramic and requires full-composition contain. Perugino *Keys* depicts Matthew 16 papal commissioning, not only the earlier confession sentence. Raphael-design Athens tapestry is woven textile, not oil.
4. **Not automatically strong Gospel illustrations despite painter name:** Velázquez *Martha and Mary* has its actual Gospel scene in a tiny inset; no image required unless justified. Crabeth *Doubting Thomas* is exact but full condition/pixel QA outstanding. Brunswick *Tabitha* is only probably Acts 9 and is partly restored. Delacroix paints Christ asleep in the storm, not the instant of stilling waves.
5. **Old Testament preferred anchored examples:** Poussin *Flood / Winter* Louvre INV 7306; Poussin *Crossing of Red Sea* NGV 1843-4; Poussin *Golden Calf* National Gallery NG5597. They may carry the Noah and Exodus narrative at their accurately named subevents. Do not force a corresponding painting for Noah's rainbow covenant or every Exodus scene. Bacchiacca *Joseph Pardons His Brothers* NG1219 is exact, but highly elongated and therefore only a secondary image. Pontormo *Joseph Sold to Potiphar* NG6451 is a documented false match to Genesis 37 brothers' sale.

## Important source-verified subevent rules

- The NGV explicitly places Poussin *Crossing of the Red Sea* after the **return of the waters and drowning of the pursuers**. Caption as that subevent; not a scene of first water-parting. [NGV collection](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/explore/collection/work/4271/), [research essay](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/essay/the-poussin-project/).
- The National Gallery's *Adoration of the Golden Calf* was painted as the **companion** to the NGV Red Sea painting, and specifically depicts the idol and Moses breaking the tablets (Exodus 32). [Museum catalogue](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/nicolas-poussin-the-adoration-of-the-golden-calf).
- The Louvre's *L'Hiver* is alternatively titled **Le Déluge**, but this is not the **Covenant with Noah**. [Louvre INV 7306](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010066113).
- In Luke 24, the **Supper at Emmaus** and **conversation on the road** are different stages. One remarkable supper painting is sufficient for the more significant recognition moment.
- The historic Met/NGA image IDs are physical originals, not license to transfer the attribution to a different similarly titled work. Several museum web photos show frames or partial views; crop QA must act on exact SHA-backed master.
