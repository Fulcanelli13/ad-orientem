All 50 have distinct historical opening-question identifiers, with one adjacent historical question supporting a follow-up. The text below contains editorial **English/French paraphrases**, not original quotations, officially approved translations or authenticated personal sayings of Giuseppe Sarto.

**Production:** 50 families · 100 conversation turns · 200 localized EN/FR draft responses. The source is a papally prescribed Catechism; there is no implication Pius X personally spoke each answer. Cross-batch semantic uniqueness remains unverified.

### SPX-1905-S-517

**Italian evidence:** [Part IV](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/), opening `PX1905-Q517`, companion `PX1905-Q518`. **Speaker:** educational documentary paraphrase.

**Visitor (EN):** What does the fourth part of the longer Catechism teach?

**Draft answer (EN):** It treats the sacraments: the sacred signs through which Christ bestows grace.

**Visiteur (FR) :** Que traite la quatrième partie du grand Catéchisme ?

**Projet de réponse (FR) :** Elle traite des sacrements, signes sacrés par lesquels le Christ donne sa grâce.

**Related question (EN):** What does the Catechism mean by a sacrament?

**Draft follow-up (EN):** An effective, perceptible sign of grace instituted by Jesus Christ to sanctify souls.

**Question liée (FR) :** Qu'entend-il par sacrement ?

**Suite (FR) :** Un signe sensible et efficace de la grâce, institué par Jésus-Christ pour sanctifier les âmes.
