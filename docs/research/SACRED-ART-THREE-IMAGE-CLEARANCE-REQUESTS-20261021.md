Research packet · 21 October 2026 · **DRAFT ONLY / NOT SENT**  
Related implementation: [draft PR #1532](https://github.com/Fulcanelli13/ad-orientem/pull/1532)

## Non-negotiable scope

These three **paintings** were explicitly approved by the curator on 19 October. The question is the **photograph** of the *same* original artwork. Do not reopen artistic selection or substitute a different painting.

Ad Orientem is a traditional Catholic liturgical companion app using the 1962 Roman calendar. Its initial distribution may be free and its business model may later include paid access. To avoid licence drift, request permission covering **free and commercial distribution**, downloadable/offline assets, worldwide distribution, Apple/Android/web interfaces, image resizing into `webp`, and accompanying attribution, for indefinite use if available. Request actual cost and duration; **do not accept a binding quote or licence on behalf of the user**.

No request below has been sent; no licence exists merely because an addressee and terms are known.

- **Date/application placement:** Advent I, 29 November 2026.

## Explicit handling

- The two Metropolitan Museum source photographs (Ugolino and Saraceni) have documented official CC0 Open Access rights; they need **photograph presentation acceptance**, not an additional licence enquiry to the Met.
- Other Commons photos remain separately tracked for jurisdiction/usage due diligence.
- Do not treat any enquiry as sent unless an actual mail-send operation succeeds and its message record is stored.
- Do not treat a favourable response as a licence without the exact terms, duration, platform, image version, commercial scope and attribution requirements.
- Do not change source art/rating/approval IDs, dates, or paint replacement policies in response to licensing issues alone.
