## Specific independent 1962 OCR loci located

- [**1962 Benziger full OCR**](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt), title-page lines 3–53: 1962 Benziger **editio iuxta typicam**, approved by Cardinal Spellman, explicitly distinct from the Vatican *editio typica* unless independently collated.
- Missal *Ritus servandus* around OCR lines **10579–10586**: after offering the chalice, the priest bows with joined hands on the altar and says *In spiritu humilitatis* silently. Direct corroboration of **N03** ritual setting; entire Latin prayer also found in other Latin Ordinary transcription.
- OCR lines **31328–31335**: *Deus qui humanae substantiae*, words concerning sharing in Christ's divinity, supporting **N02**. Note the two columns intermingle this with *Per intercessionem beati Michaelis*.
- OCR lines **33789–33792**: the ordinary *Sanctus Deus Sabaoth* wording; Isaiah 6:3 Clementine reads *Deus exercituum* (**MB11**).
- OCR lines **11969–11975**: *mysterium fidei* is part of the received 1962 chalice consecratory wording (**MB15**). Luke 22:20 contains no such expression.
- OCR lines **35420–35426**: *Calicem salutaris accipiam*, supporting **MB25**, corresponding to Clementine [Psalm 115:3–4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalmi+115%3A3-4&version=VULGATE).
- OCR lines **11215–11225** and **11680–11685**: 1962 Requiem instructions omit the first private *Domine Iesu Christe, qui dixisti* Communion preparation prayer; corroboration of the conditional statement in **N05**.
- OCR lines **32250–32253** and **35420–35426** are section-sensitive: chant settings, main Ordo and text may be separated in OCR. Never cite the entire OCR text as proof of exact image layout or cross-column boundaries.

## Editorial source control

The [general Latin Ordinarium transcription](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Ordinarium_miss%C3%A6_(1962)) was useful for locating incipits but **contains demonstrable typos**. By contrast the [1962 Benziger book OCR](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt) can be cited as another transcription; it too contains OCR defects and is **not final word-for-word publication authority**. Source-level edition labels must distinguish Vatican editio typica, American editio iuxta typicam, modern transcriptions and later commentary by O'Connell.

**Psalm numbering safeguard:** the Clementine Vulgate's split Psalm 115:3–4 corresponds to Psalm 116:12–13 in many modern editions. Latin biblical websites vary: some group the psalm and number its verses 12–13; do not call that Clementine's split verse index.

**Types of connections:** biblical direct quotations (Psalm 42, Psalm 115, John 14 fragment), adaptations (Gloria, Sanctus, Daniel prayer, centurion), named biblical figures (Melchisedech), doctrinal echoes (2 Peter), and specific visual allusions (Isaiah's coal) are separate. `Dirigatur` is conditional on incensation and cannot be asserted as universally used at every Low Mass.
