Via Crucis

Fourteen oil paintings, painted 1747–1749 by Giandomenico Tiepolo for the Oratory of the Crucifix, Church of San Polo, Venice. This is the complete painted series—not the artist's printed or etched Stations.

Research gallery, not the live Prayer reader. Existing user-approved Station paintings remain unchanged. These modern photographs are under CC BY-SA 4.0, not PD/CC0; the production gate remains closed until the applicable licensing and visual approvals are resolved.
