# Sacred Atlas — exceptional seven publication review

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: Seven source-documented pilgrimage locations; attribution of individual objects remains unverified. Refer to data/explore/relic-exceptional-seven-publication.2026-10-09.json for independent object claims, original-source links, coordinates and access notices.

## Seven site-level decisions

- Turin: Cathedral is the identified custodial location of the Shroud. Its protective enclosure is ordinarily closed to public viewing. Source: https://sindone.org/en/home-english/the-shroud/where-is-the-shroud-now/
- Aachen: Four distinct attributed textiles are together in the Marian shrine. The cathedral identifies the next major pilgrimage as 2028; do not infer events from a recurring interval. Source: https://www.aachenerdom.de/en/the-aachen-pilgrimage/
- Trier: Cathedral confirms custody of the Holy Robe tradition and expressly says its authenticity cannot be conclusively established. The garment remains hidden during ordinary visits. Source: https://www.trierer-dom.de/bauwerk/der-heilige-rock/wallfahrten/index.html
- Cahors: Parish confirms normal visibility from outside the chapel and restricted entry during special solemn veneration. Source: https://saintecoiffedecahors.com/index.php/2020/02/13/les-activites-de-pelerinage/
- Oviedo: Cathedral confirms Sudarium custody in the Cámara Santa; the original is displayed in special periods, with year-dependent visitor arrangements. Source: https://catedraldeoviedo.com/visita-cultural/prepara-tu-visita/preguntas-frecuentes/
- Fécamp: Municipal heritage source documents differing medieval Precious Blood traditions, not the actual provenance of the purported material. Source: https://www.fecamptourisme.com/experiences/nos-incontournables/abbatiale-sainte-trinite/
- Manoppello: Capuchin custodians document the sanctuary and its venerated Holy Face image, without independently establishing identity with the historical Roman Veronica. Source: https://www.voltosanto.it/volto-santo.html

## Editorial controls

One marker per physical Place. Group four Aachen objects; keep Argenteuil and Trier distinct; keep Turin and Oviedo distinct. Do not equate medieval reports with object authentication. Visitor access to a building is different from public exposition of the object. Keep bilingual access notes and original hyperlinks, with no invented feast dates, 1962 Mass timetables, or automatic ostension schedule.

Published exceptional-object group records must preserve legacy research candidate references and keep the separate 39-site principal-saint shortlist intact. This publication is not a certification of material class or historical attribution. Full checks belong to the Directory Foundation workflow.
