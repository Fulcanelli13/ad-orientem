# Biblical Atlas — bounded 151-milestone chronology preview (12 October 2026)

**Scope:** one selected-event Bible chronology reader; **not** a universal Bible timeline, historical Gospel harmony, definitive geography atlas, genealogy release, or cinematic theatre. This branch is derived from the existing #1069 canonical event engine and its stable source keys, not an independent alternative dataset. Merge the narrow reader before updating the broad research branch to consume the same owner.

### Included
- 151 selected Scripture event references, 11 editorial eras, French and English fallback labels, native Catholic Scripture opening and return, no extra Bible translation viewer.
- 35 **unique** research-qualified geographic anchors used as context (traditional, city-context, settlement, mountain-region or site); event coordinates absent for Sinai, Cana, Creation and contested locations.
- Scripture-first unlocated presentation; map appears again for independently qualified source locations; a 320/390/768/1280px phone and desktop capture suite.
- Five source-critical *verse slots* remain missing in two **UNCOLLATED** imported witnesses covering three passages: Mark 4:35–41 (both arrays omit standalone 41 despite printed Crampon having it), Acts 7:54–60 (Challoner numbering ends 59), Exodus 40:1–38 (Challoner ends 36). Corresponding bilingual caveats must render on selected events. **148/151** references have imported-range slot coverage in both sources; this is not original printed-volume certification.
- Narrative caveats for John 2 cleansing and a selected-Gospel/non-dated reading order. Genealogy, 181-scene study, 90 Genesis edges and Apostolic research remain owned by #1018/#1069; Mass–Bible source research stays in #1082.

### Deliberately excluded
- Research-only `biblical-lineage-curated-review.v1.json` and `biblical-cinematic-studies-review.v1.json`, their runtime viewers, and related UI tabs.
- Art files/rights, unverified historic itineraries, original genealogical kinship inferences and unreliable coordinate candidates.
- Claim that chronology covers all events of the 73-book Catholic Bible, precise Jesus-ministry dates, published/certified full original 1962 Missal cross-links or printed Crampon collation.

### Acceptance contract
1. Integrity tests enforce 151 unique source keys, within-Gospel order, explicit era breaks, 35 qualified anchor IDs and absent speculative pins.
2. Scripture range checks preserve all five known imported witness gaps and visible warnings, with no invented text.
3. The timeline renders as **one** Explore/Scripture reader, with **no genealogy or cinematic view/payload**.
4. Real mobile tests exercise user gestures and Scripture→Atlas return and upload screenshots.
5. Mainline Mass/Prayer/Calendar and Explore regression workflows remain green on this PR's **own current head**. Manual visual review and print-volume historical verification remain separate, explicit gates.

**Decision:** A technically passing chronology *preview* is eligible for isolated integration after review; it is **not** historical/research certification or permission to close #1069/#1018/#1082.
