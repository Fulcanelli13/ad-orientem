## Decision (historical Phase 1 scope; product framing superseded)

There must be **one** Mystery of the Mass curriculum integrated with the existing `learn.mass` owner, not a new parallel Mass reader. The reader consists of an eight-lesson explanatory pathway and contextual, source-backed prayer/rite explanations that are **reused**, not reauthored independently, across the **39 current source-first presentation units** (30 historical Guide macro owners overlaid with 14 Canon refinements). Existing LIVE card ownership, rubrics and gestures remain untouched.

The historical 48-card LIVE product design is **not superseded** by the 39 source-first content ownership view. The source-first map is an *editorial content index*; neither its 39 units nor its 14 Canon segments authorize replacement of the 48-card experience.

## Mandatory merge and anti-duplication decisions

1. **Psalm 42:** reuse polished `MB02` as the principal Bible study for `AO.CARD.001`; `MB01` is background/source input, not a duplicate second explanation. Preserve omissions for Requiem and relevant seasonal/preceding rites.
2. **Melchisedech:** combine the sacrificial meaning in polished `MB19` with supplementary `MB20` (Hebrews 7), original Guéranger `FRG41–43` and Lanslots's qualified Genesis 14 interpretation at **one** `AO.CANON.09` owner. Exclude speculation about a consecrated meal for soldiers as historical certainty.
3. **Isaiah and Sanctus:** one `AO.CARD.013` owner can cite both Isaiah 6:3 and Revelation 4:8; one `Benedictus` subsection can explain Matthew 21:9, without multiplying parallel cards.
4. **Supplices te rogamus:** combine FRG44–45 and Lanslots's source-critical discussion at `AO.CANON.10`; present Guéranger's Christological reading alongside alternatives without asserting dogmatic identity of the angel.
5. **Communicantes:** source criticism must reflect St Joseph's **8 December 1962** insertion in the current Canon; never overwrite 1962 liturgical text from the 1897 source.
6. **Communion roles:** the priest's `Domine, non sum dignus` is in `AO.CARD.022`; the faithful's separate branch must not silently inherit the priest's wording or gesture rules.
7. **Variable Mass materials:** Proper Epistle/Gospel examples (`MB06`, `MB07`), Gradual, Preface and other proper material are illustrative or date-dependent, not the selected literal text of every Mass.
8. **Cross-references to Bible Atlas:** store Scripture passage identity once and link to existing Bible context, not new duplicate Bible narratives or artwork records.
