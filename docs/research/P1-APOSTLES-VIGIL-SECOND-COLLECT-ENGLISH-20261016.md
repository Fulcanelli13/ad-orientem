# P1 — Apostles' Vigil, alternate English Collect (editorial correction only)

**Status:** `TRANSLATION_DISCREPANCY_CONFIRMED`; proposed English rendering is **not** approved or imported into the Ad Orientem Missal. Source: the same `Commune/C1v` in the existing app's original Latin, English and French source maps.

## Actual source-owner distinction

`Oratio 1 loco` and `Oratio 2 loco` are **different original Latin orations**, not spelling or title variants. In the inherited English source `Oratio 2 loco` repeats the first English Collect instead of representing the Latin second. The existing French second Collect correctly differs from the first, making the English problem more evident. `Oratio pro Evangelistae` is a **third conditional variant** derived by an explicitly named source substitution; it is not the universal vigil Collect.

Original second Latin Collect (exact source in `Commune/C1v`):

> Quǽsumus omnípotens Deus, ut beátus N. Apóstolus, cujus prævenímus festivitátem, tuum pro nobis implóret auxílium: ut a nostris reátibus absolúti a cunctis étiam perículis eruámur.

Existing source owner `Commune/C1v`; the usual conclusion `$Per Dominum` follows in the native source. The Latin quotation is research evidence only, not a new Mass text store.

### Original English translation proposed for `Oratio 2 loco`

> We beseech Thee, almighty God, that blessed N., Thine Apostle, whose feast we anticipate, may implore Thy help on our behalf; that, absolved from our offenses, we may also be delivered from every danger. Through our Lord.

**Not** to be substituted for `Oratio 1 loco` or used as a generic default. The placeholder N. must be inflected and resolved only under the actual selected liturgical variant. The phrase `a nostris reatibus absoluti` requires a final translation review (absolved from our guilt/offenses); `a cunctis etiam periculis eruamur` expressly calls for deliverance from dangers and is missing in the inherited erroneous English text.
