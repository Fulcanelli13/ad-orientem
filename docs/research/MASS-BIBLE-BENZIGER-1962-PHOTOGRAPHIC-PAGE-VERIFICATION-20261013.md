# 1962 Roman Missal — first photographed printed-page verification batch

**13 October 2026 · Research-only · Existing Mass–Bible PR #1082.**

## What is proven, and what is NOT

- **16/31 existing/proposed Mass–Bible teaching loci** were compared by direct visual reading of pages photographed from a physical **1962 Benziger Brothers altar Missal**, and the image links/pages are listed below. This is materially stronger than the earlier OCR-only source review, which remains 31/31.
- **0/31** are independently collated with the separate **1962 Vatican editio typica**; **0/31** are approved for public release. Visual reading of an original printed page in one edition does not itself certify its identity with a second edition or validate an explanatory theological claim.
- **Print identity confirmed** at photographed title page [scan 0012](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0012.jpg), bearing `EDITIO IUXTA TYPICAM`, US printer [scan 0013](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0013.jpg), and Cardinal Spellman's episcopal approval [scan 0014](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0014.jpg) dated 9 May 1962. The printer is **Benziger Brothers, New York**, not the Vatican printing house.
- **Photographic-mirror source caution:** the raw scans were hosted by an independent polemical website whose theological, conspiratorial and discriminatory editorial content is **not relevant evidence and is not endorsed**. Consult **only the linked photographed book pages**, not its commentary. These scans should be replaced by a neutral institutional facsimile where feasible; their host is not the book's publisher or an ecclesial authority. Do not use the watermarked images as in-app illustrations.
- **Significant physical intervention:** photographed [printed p.306, scan 0383](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0383.jpg) has an **inserted/pasted correction strip** above the page body stating St Joseph's inclusion (`et Beati Joseph ejusdem Virginis Sponsi`), while the uncorrected lower text of Communicantes on the same page omits the name. This is therefore **a physical correction to an earlier printed copy**, not proof of uninterrupted original typesetting; record the post-1962 amendment independently.
- Historical corroboration of amendment chronology: [1962 decree summary and AAS 54 (1962), p.873 reference](https://domvsdei.com/rites/anaphoras/roman-canon/), [Propria neutral liturgical-library note about the original printing and Joseph's late insertion](https://propria.org/additional/). AAS original decree page has **not** been directly inspected here; its summary is corroboration rather than a full decree certification.

## New exact orthography evidence: Matthew / Luke and the Pater noster

- [Printed p.310, scan 0387](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0387.jpg) and [p.311, scan 0388](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0388.jpg) visibly syllabify the phrase **`Pa-nem nostrum co-ti-di-a-num`**. This Benziger original leaf's spelling corresponds to **cotidianum** (sometimes standardised to *quotidianum*) and is **not** Matthew's Clementine **supersubstantialem**. Thus the prior generic claim that the exact printed page says *quotidianum* was too strong.
- Check the Latin Bible versions independently: [Matthew 6:11](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat006.htm) has supersubstantialem; [Luke 11:3](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk011.htm) uses quotidianum in this online presentation. The doctrinal sense daily bread is stable; printed orthography is edition-dependent.
- **Corrective GitHub writes already made:** both original MB21 research source record, the extended 31 draft dataset, and the full bilingual manuscript distinguish the witnessed cotidianum spelling from other normalised forms.

## Canon, Communion and conclusion — stronger evidence now available

- Printed [p.307, scan 0384](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0384.jpg) visibly sets the Host formula `Hoc est enim Corpus meum` and chalice words with `mysterium fidei` **inside** the 1962 consecration formula. This directly supports MB14 and MB15 and prevents silent substitution of a later Mass text.
- Printed [p.308, scan 0385](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0385.jpg) visibly prints the anamnesis `Unde et memores`, then names Abel, Abraham and Melchisedech before `Supplices te rogamus`, supporting the **actual 1962 Canon order** and MB16–MB19.
- Printed [p.313, scan 0390](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0390.jpg) contains the ordinary Agnus petition as well as the Requiem change, with only a later rubrical note for the dead. This underpins MB23 but does **not by itself** prove every Requiem ceremonial variation.
- Printed [p.314, scan 0391](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0391.jpg) gives the Peace prayer and immediately states that both the earlier prayer and the pax are omitted in Masses for the Dead. This supports N05's conditional clause.
- Printed [p.315, scan 0392](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0392.jpg) records the centurion adaptation `sanabitur anima mea` and the chalice Psalm words `Quid retribuam` / `Calicem salutaris`, supporting MB24 and MB25.
- Printed [p.319, scan 0396](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0396.jpg) and [p.320, scan 0397](https://www.ourladyisgod.com/images/eBooks/Missale-Romanum-1962-color/Missale-Romanum-1962-Roman-Missal-color-0397.jpg) show John 1:1–14 and explicitly prescribe the genuflection at `Et Verbum caro factum est`, supporting MB26 **when Last Gospel is prescribed**; its omission cases must still be verified against rubrics separately.

## Independent corroboration and limits

- [Propria.org original Vatican typical-edition facsimile sample (11 PDF pages)](https://propria.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/sample-pages-from-propria.org-missale-romanum-1962.pdf) was separately opened and visually screenshot-checked, but covers **nonmatching Proper pages** (for example Precious Blood feast p.533). It cannot certify the **specific** 16 Benziger loci as identical to the Vatican edition.
- [Isidore 29 MB full original-page OCR PDF index](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/) exists; direct retrieval returned file-size failure, so its scanned images were NOT seen during this batch.
- The photographic set is indexed as 1135 page images and includes added sectarian preliminary pages and watermarks. Thus the `image index` is not the `printed page`; within the visually inspected run, **image index = printed page + 77**. This alignment was verified at each read image, not presumed globally across the whole book.
- No photographic host licence for reusing image assets in a commercial app is established.
