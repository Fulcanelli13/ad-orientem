# St Pius X — Batch 9: 50 bilingual documentary conversation drafts

**Status:** research drafts only, not certified or published. **Historical source:** [1905 longer *Catechismo maggiore*, Part IV, Sacraments](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/). Source-opening question range **567–616**, with next numbered questions supporting follow-ups. Every family preserves its Italian source question for subsequent collation. The EN/FR replies are original editorial paraphrases and **not** quotations or official translations, nor documented personal words of Pius X.

**Scope:** Baptism (Q567–576), Confirmation (Q577–595), Eucharist (Q596–616). **Production totals:** 50 conversation families · 100 turns · 200 English/French draft responses.

### SPX-1905-S-567

**Source:** [Italian 1905 Catechism](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/), opening `PX1905-Q567`, companion `PX1905-Q568`. **Original Italian question:** Si può supplire in qualche modo alla mancanza del Battesimo?

**Visitor (EN):** Can the absence of sacramental Baptism be supplied in another way?

**Draft answer (EN):** The 1905 Catechism teaches that martyrdom, called Baptism of blood, or perfect love of God or contrition joined to at least an implicit desire for Baptism may supply for its absence.

**Visiteur (FR) :** Peut-on suppléer d'une autre manière à l'absence du baptême sacramentel ?

**Projet de réponse (FR) :** Le Catéchisme de 1905 enseigne que le martyre, appelé baptême de sang, ou l'amour parfait de Dieu ou la contrition parfaite unis au moins au désir implicite du baptême peuvent suppléer à son absence.

**Related question (EN):** What obligations follow upon Baptism?

**Draft follow-up (EN):** To profess the faith and observe the law of Christ and His Church.

**Question liée (FR) :** Quelles obligations découlent du baptême ?

**Suite (FR) :** Professer la foi et observer la loi du Christ et de son Église.
