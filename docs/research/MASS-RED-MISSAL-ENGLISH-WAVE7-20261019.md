# Red Missal 2013 — Wave 7: final Mass prayers and Last Gospel

**Source:** User-provided *Latin–English Booklet Missal for Praying the Traditional Mass*, Coalition in Support of Ecclesia Dei (2013), PDF scanned pages **18, 20, 30, 46, 48 and 50** (printed pp.17,19,29,45,47,49); compare Last Gospel Latin on PDF p.49. No complete copyrighted page text has been copied into this repository.

**Source precedence:** The *Missale Romanum* 1962 and existing canonical/cue/rubric registry control the Latin, state and form-specific rite. The Red Missal is the user's preferred English editorial witness, **not** a reason to promote older optional observances into the normative Mass.

## Last Gospel — three source-owned English decisions

- **C0272 (John 1:12)**: Current English says `But as many as received Him, He gave them power to be made the sons of God`. The booklet and Latin `dedit eis potestátem fílios Dei fíeri` support the clearer `But as many as received Him, to them He gave power to become the sons of God`.
- **C0273 (John 1:14, genuflection)**: English `AND THE WORD WAS MADE FLESH.` becomes `AND THE WORD WAS MADE FLESH,`. This preserves the separately highlighted genuflection cue while joining the sentence to the following words, matching the booklet's actual English printing on p.50.
- **C0274 (John 1:14 continuation)**: Change initial `And dwelt among us; and we saw his glory` to `and dwelt among us, and we saw His glory`. The complete English clause now reads naturally across **two distinct** cue identities without changing the Latin, actual kneel/genuflection trigger, Last Gospel speech owner or any separate audio/staging state.

All three patches are exact source-identified English replacements in the pre-reader canonical correction layer, with source-original and reviewed English stored in the versioned source manifest. The frozen LOW and SUNG donors, all Latin, and all French remain unchanged.

## Another 51 English cues reviewed and retained

Review of printed Red Missal pages covers:
- Pre-Collect dialogue and `Munda cor meum`, and Gospel title/response (9).
- Preface dialogue plus `Sanctus` and `Benedictus` (14): do not re-split the approved single Sanctus/Benedictus LIVE card, nor modify priest/Schola ownership.
- Optional Communion response texts (4): **older/local conditional only**, never normative in 1962. The booklet itself warns the second Communion Confiteor is omitted in 1962.
- `Ecce Agnus Dei`, repeated `Domine non sum dignus`, Communion of the faithful and two ablution prayers (7).
- Final dialogue, dismissal, `Placeat tibi`, blessing (11).
- Last Gospel invitation, John 1:1–11 and `Deo gratias` (6).

The existing English `Placeat tibi` phrase `propitiation` is a defensible rendering of Latin `propitiábile`, even though the booklet uses a different expression. Retain it rather than change meaning for consistency alone.
