# St Pius X — Batch 10: 50 bilingual Eucharist and Mass conversation drafts

**Status:** documentary editorial research only, not certified or published. **Source:** [1905 longer Catechism, Part IV — Sacraments](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/), opening questions Q617–Q666. Each family preserves its original Italian question and cites one adjacent historical question for its follow-up. The answers are **new English/French educational paraphrases**, not certified original Italian sentence translations or personal utterances by Pope Pius X.

**Coverage:** Eucharistic Real Presence, reservation, fruits, worthy Holy Communion, preparation, historical fasting and observance, the Sacrifice of the Mass, Calvary, the four ends, Mass intentions, and participation at Mass.

**Production:** 50 families, 100 turns, 200 localized English/French draft answers. Individual source-sentence, doctrinal, bilingual and historical-voice approval remain pending. These are **not** 50 certified semantically distinct answers.

### SPX-1905-E-617

**Source:** [Italian longer Catechism, Part IV](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/); original opening `PX1905-Q617`, follow-up `PX1905-Q618`. **Original Italian question:** Quando si rompe l’ostia, si rompe il Corpo di Gesù Cristo?

**Visitor (EN):** Does breaking the consecrated host divide Christ's Body?

**Draft answer (EN):** No. The Eucharistic appearances of bread are broken; the Body of Jesus Christ is not divided.

**Visiteur (FR) :** La fraction de l'hostie consacrée divise-t-elle le Corps du Christ ?

**Projet de réponse (FR) :** Non. Ce sont les apparences eucharistiques du pain qui se brisent ; le Corps de Jésus-Christ n'est pas divisé.

**Related question (EN):** Where is Christ's Body when the consecrated host is divided?

**Draft follow-up (EN):** Christ's Body remains whole under each portion of the divided consecrated host.

**Question liée (FR) :** Où se trouve le Corps du Christ lorsqu'on divise l'hostie consacrée ?

**Suite (FR) :** Le Corps du Christ demeure tout entier sous chacune des parties de l'hostie consacrée divisée.
