## Cross-module integration policy

- Existing SHA-bearing artwork IDs are reusable by reference across saint feasts, prayers, calendar and the biblical narrative without adding separate image files.
- Bible Theatre canonical numerical event IDs reside in another active pipeline; **no numerical ID was invented or altered**. Link via explicit scriptural reference provisionally, then reconcile with the separately maintained event IDs when the other pipeline advances.
- The 1962 calendar and actual appointed Gospel decide whether a painting can appear on a given Sunday or feast. Do not mark the whole Liturgical Year as illustrated based on seasonal thematic matches.
- Two previously accepted paintings, Zurbarán's *Saint Benedict* and Gandolfi's *Holy Family*, plus the Morlete Ruiz *Heart of Jesus*, retain their historical selection provenance; no silent substitution into the 101 current subjects.
- No image is release ready until exact SHA-backed original, reproduction rights, aesthetic approval, no-frame/no-glare master, caption, and full composition mobile crop are individually checked.
