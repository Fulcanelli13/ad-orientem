## Engineering and curation defects to correct in B56 before next review

1. **Unlinked exact objects are dropped:** `submit(pid)` immediately returns when `pid not in paintings`. This silently eliminates Met 437821 for Laetare from the otherwise accurate `exact_research_links`. Show an explicit `EXACT_SOURCE_OUTSIDE_GALLERY` with link and image-research hold. Never replace with generic unrelated gallery object because it happens to be available.
2. **Generic theming outranks relevance to proper:** weeks II/III/IV Advent B56 cards start with Annunciation paintings; these are not the stated Sunday Gospels. Put a truthful `CONTEXT_ONLY` category on these options, with the actual Gospel stated alongside. A painting can remain a valuable candidate without a false exactness claim.
3. **Candidate duplicates across days:** Holy Tuesday and Spy Wednesday should not be forced to use identical Raphael's *Agony* just because the same long Passion scene occurs in both Gospels. Distinct art helps read the narrative's development.
4. **Two kinds of “exact”:** a painting of the precise narrated event (Gospel exact), a painting of the feast's theological theme/saint (feast exact), and a painting of a **subscene** within a lengthy Passion account have different values. Use these labels; do not discard art just because the old title score was misaligned.
5. **Visual judgement remains human/editorial:** none of the 15 are declared approved in this file; score/artist/keywords cannot prove full original, colour fidelity, zero frame, religious dignity, or suitability for mobile.
