# Acquired sacred artworks — reuse audit against 134 research subjects

**12 October 2026 · PR #887 (draft) · selection-only analysis · not app release**

This register assesses the *266 paintings with acquired SHA-256 originals* already in the source registry before further acquisition. It is **not** a new catalogue, nor permission to replace any of the user's 101 current artwork selections. Matching means source relevance, not user acceptance of the painting's style, colour, composition, cropping or reproduction rights.

- **134** outstanding subjects audited; **27** have at least one acquired relevant lead; **107** lack a verified lead in this deliberately conservative pass.
- **59** subject-to-original links point to **50** distinct already acquired artwork originals. One original SHA-256 may be linked to multiple subjects without duplicating the image file.

- **Zero** user artistic approvals newly granted, **zero** production publications; **101** pre-existing selections remain intact.

## Important exclusions and limitations

- **DEV-02:** Immaculate Conception paintings are NOT the Immaculate Heart devotional subject. Prior first-pass reuse table erroneously included these; corrected here. (`met-437423`, `cma-135659`).
- **SNT-AP-05:** Saint Philip Neri is not Apostle Philip. (`met-712539`).
- **SNT-AP-07:** Saint Thomas Aquinas is not Thomas the Apostle. (`met-435617`, `met-459041`).
- **SNT-OT-01:** Joseph of Cupertino is not Saint Joseph husband of Mary. (`met-724811`).
- **SNT-OT-06:** Saint Anthony Abbot is not Saint Anthony of Padua. (`nga-505`).
- **SNT-OT-22:** Painting explicitly features Saint Nicholas and Saint Anthony Abbot; it does not establish Saint Agnes. (`nga-505`).

The prior [focused v1 reuse file](../../data/calendar/sacred-art-134-existing-originals-reuse-20261012.v1.json) contained the Immaculate Heart / Immaculate Conception conflation. **This v2 JSON and report supersede it**. The maintained machine-readable source for all matches and exclusions is [the v2 register](../../data/calendar/sacred-art-134-existing-originals-reuse-20261012.v2.json).

A manuscript image depicting the Expulsion from Eden does not portray the instant of the Fall. The Supper at Emmaus is the recognition meal, not the road conversation. A multi-saint altar piece remains secondary for an individual saint, especially on a mobile portrait. Where the precise Gospel healing differs between accounts, keep the relation unverified until the object is checked. An institutional object URL is not itself clearance to use any downloaded reproduction commercially. Kress/Commons source holdings remain on their separate rights tracks.

## Next batch strategy

1. **Review images already acquired first**: finish the exact-original comparison of shortlist candidates, with object identity and small-screen readability checks; do not automatically approve stylized panels or oil sketches for the Home hero.
2. **Reuse one painting across properly justified contexts** (calendar, saint, Rosary, Bible Atlas) by its same canonical artwork ID and SHA-256. Keep original scene identity; never manufacture a gospel-feast association.
3. **Investigate the remaining 107 research subjects by necessity**, not by rigid quotas: 71 Bible Atlas scenes, 33 saints and 3 devotions. Historical maps, genealogy or ritual-architecture views sometimes explain a subject better than an unrelated devotional painting.
4. Only after the broad source inventory and art-quality screening, prepare the already-planned comprehensive user review; present shortlisted exact images and ask the user for only exceptions (reject, misplaced, investigate). Crop, rights and release remain independent gates.
