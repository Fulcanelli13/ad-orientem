## Methodology: unit of work and independent release gates
**1. Liturgical identity:** Take actual 1962 `principal_id` and appointed Gospel, Epistle, feast and rite. Separate proper event from thematic imagery. Don't silently substitute a related miracle, a later Gospel episode, another saint, or repeated octave art.

**2. Physical painting discovery:** For each date, research 3–5 distinct physical originals in credible museum, church, catalogue raisonné or heritage records. Record artist/attribution, full title, date, physical custodian, accession/identifier, primary URL, scriptural fit and uncertainty. Provenance is separate from the availability of a copyable image.

**3. Iconographic grading:** Mark works `EXACT_PERICOPE`, `EXACT_FEAST_MYSTERY`, `LITURGICALLY_APPROPRIATE_THEMATIC`, `CONTEXTUAL_ONLY`, or `WRONG_EVENT`. A thematic work can be approved deliberately but never described as biblical exact if it is not.

**4. Artistic and UX review:** In chat show actual painting images at useful size, credit artist and object, explain 1–2 visual/accuracy strengths/weaknesses, and recommend one. Prioritise compositional accomplishment, dignity, dark restrained Baroque where appropriate, theological fit, originality, readable focus on mobile. Do not constrain to one artist or assume darker is always better. Avoid user-excluded styles; maintain a separate backlog of paintings user wants researched further.

**5. User decision:** Review 4–5 feast dates per round, ideally 3–5 painting candidates each. Accept one explicit choice, 'research further', 'none suitable', or unanswered/undecided. Preserve protected choices and prior holds. No automatic candidate score may override a date-specific approval. Write dated, append-only decision provenance into the existing register.

**6. Post-selection photographic gates:** Source actual unframed/full-work photos; store *source URL, attribution, photographer, image licence and original SHA-256*. Review sharpness, faithful colour, contrast, painted edges, restorations, watermarks, compression and compositional safety for desktop and mobile. Keep research thumbnail and release master distinct; an excellent painting can still have no acceptable available photograph.

**7. Storage and publication:** A truly no-cost durable master/archive (within explicit service quotas) is prerequisite; a 7-day Actions artifact is temporary transport only. Image must pass rights, photo master, crop/composition, checksum and editorial approval checks independently. Only then add app mapping by liturgical identity, exercise rollback, test live Calendar views and ship.

**8. Reporting cadence:** Report the denominators separately: `candidate sources found / source checks complete / physical paintings selected / good photo masters / rights cleared / images released`. Flag hard exceptions. A date is finished for users **only when** its approved, source-provenanced, legally reusable and visually inspected image is actually live.
