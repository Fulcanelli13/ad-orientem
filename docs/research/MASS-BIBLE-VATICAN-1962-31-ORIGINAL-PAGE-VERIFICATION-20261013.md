# 1962 Vatican *editio typica* — direct-image source verification of 31 Mass–Bible teaching loci

**13 October 2026 | Research branch PR #1082 | No public app changes.**

## New research breakthrough and independent provenance

Earlier research used (1) open OCR of a Benziger *editio iuxta typicam* and (2) photographs of one physical Benziger copy. Large Vatican PDF downloads could not be inspected; those files alone did not certify any direct Vatican original pages. This block resolves the access problem via [GregoBase's 1962 Vatican Missal **individual source images**](https://gregobase.selapa.net/source.php?id=42&images=1), credited to the [Church Music Association of America](https://musicasacra.com/). Individual source images are black-and-white direct scans of the actual printed leaves, not a retyped web missal.

**Direct images actually opened and read** across Vatican original-book pages 216–222, 294, 301, 302, 303, 306–309, 310, 312–314, 316–318, 319–324, 325–327, 384, 389–390 (some scans sampled individually rather than all pages in these ranges). Every row below identifies the exact separate Vatican edition page containing its particular Latin formula or Proper reading. This proves **31/31 LITURGICAL LOCUS matches**, NOT certification of all biblical exegetical wording, patristic source interpretations, commercial redistribution or every exceptional seasonal rubric. No publication approval has been issued.

## Four edition-critical corrections

### 1. No more Vatican PDF download dependency

The [full Vatican edition scanned PDF](https://media.musicasacra.com/pdf/missale62.pdf) is large and previous attempts to open it failed for size reasons. The same edition's source leaves are exposed as independent page images from GregoBase, enabling this actual 1962 printed-page reading. **No original facsimile was fabricated or inferred merely from OCR**; all 31 records carry the exact GregoBase page-image URL and page-locus observation.

### 2. Two 1962 editions have different pages and sometimes different physical print states

- [**Vatican p.302**](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/384.png) directly prints within **Communicantes** `sed et beati Ioseph, eiusdem Virginis Sponsi` as uninterrupted typesetting. The earlier photographed **Benziger p.306** had an inserted/pasted amendment to include the saint. Those facts do not contradict each other: the physical copies reflect **different printing/amendment states**, and one cannot assign the pasted strip to the base Benziger typesetting. This also does NOT date when each copy was actually printed merely from its 1962 edition label.
- [Vatican p.313](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/395.png) **and** earlier Benziger p.310–311 visibly chant `co-ti-di-a-num`. The witnessed spelling is **cotidianum** in these imprints; compare Clementine Matthew 6:11 `supersubstantialem`, Luke 11:3 `quotidianum` (online orthographic display). Do not silently normalise printed spelling into a false transcription.
- [Vatican pp.389–390](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/471.png) / [Gospel](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/472.png) show **Dominica X post Pentecosten** with **1 Cor 12:2–11** and **Luke 18:9–14**. In the earlier photographic/OCR *Benziger* sequence, p.384 had been recorded for those readings. **These page numbers are not interchangeable.**

### 3. Roman Canon direct page evidence

- [Vatican p.306](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/388.png): `Hoc est enim Corpus meum` in the proper 1962 institution narrative.
- [Vatican p.307](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/389.png): the chalice formula **retains** `mysterium fidei` inside the words pronounced over the chalice (not an exact Gospel quotation).
- [Vatican p.308](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/390.png) / [p.309](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/391.png): `Unde et memores` and `Supra quae` name Abel, Abraham, Melchisedech. The biblical figure is explicit; each narrative, theological typology and historical interpretation remain separately supported.
- [Vatican p.318](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/400.png): both ordinary and Requiem *Agnus Dei*, and the peace prayer incorporating John 14:27. [Vatican p.319](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/401.png) specifies omissions of kiss of peace and preceding prayer for Requiem.

### 4. Early Ordinary and Offertory — 15 former gaps closed

- [Vatican p.216](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/298.png): `Introibo`, Psalm 42, `Confiteor`; [p.217](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/299.png): `Kyrie`.
- [Vatican pp.218–219](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/300.png): `Gloria`; [p.219](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/301.png): `Munda cor meum`, expressly identifying Isaiah's lips and burning coal.
- [Vatican p.220](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/302.png): `Et incarnatus est` and genuflection in Credo; the prayer `Suscipe sancte Pater` follows.
- [Vatican p.221](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/303.png): `Deus qui humanae substantiae`, `In spiritu humilitatis`, `Veni sanctificator`, conditional incense `Dirigatur` and Lavabo Psalm.
- [Vatican p.222](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/304.png): `Suscipe sancta Trinitas` and `Orate fratres`.
- [Vatican pp.389–390](https://gregobase.selapa.net/sources/42/471.png): ten-Sunday Proper example; the actual source text is assigned **only** to that Mass.

## Acceptance boundaries

- 31/31 bilingual explanations have first-pass biblical/theological context checks, and now 31/31 have **original Vatican 1962 Latin printed-page locus verification**. This does not imply 31 completed critical editions or 31 approved theological conclusions.
- Only 16 of the 31 had **additional Benziger physical photographed checks** in the prior batch. The Vatican facsimile is not a retroactive Benziger original-page check for the other 15.
- Need editorial page-by-page **interpretation and text acceptance**, Catholic Bible edition (Clementine / Crampon) quote validation, image/text reproduction rights, special feasts/rite-type exceptions, and explicit human release decision per subject. **0/31 user-facing publication approval**.
