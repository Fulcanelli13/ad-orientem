## Source boundaries

**SPX-010** uses the [Museo San Pio X transcription of the Salzano catechism](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-di-don-giuseppe-sarto-arciprete-di-salzano/), editorial Q198–200. Its source preface explicitly explains that **the manuscript questions were unnumbered**; the website's Q-numbers were assigned in transcription. **Q198**: Peter denied knowing Christ. **Q199**: Judas returned the money, confessed wrongdoing and despaired of divine mercy. **Q200**: Peter remembered Christ's warning, wept and received pardon.

**SPX-023** uses the same Italian manuscript transcription, editorial Q476–481. **Q477** distinguishes three ways of speaking of Communion; **Q478** defines spiritual Communion as desire without actual reception; **Q479** explicitly states that grace, though not the sacrament, is received. **Q480–481** distinguish sacramental reception without grace from fruitful reception. The newer script does **not** turn a historical teaching into a verdict on an individual's worthiness.

**SPX-032** uses [*E supremi*, §13, Vatican English rendering](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-x/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-x_enc_04101903_e-supremi.html). It teaches patience in rebuke, identifies harsh reproach as often harmful, calls charity patient and kind, and says charity may wait for fruits of its labour. **This verification used the Vatican English text, not a word-for-word collation of the original Latin.** French responses are freshly drafted, not quoted from an authenticated French edition.

## Correction log (v1 → v2)

- **Peter:** Replace “betrayal” with **denial**, which is what the specific Salzano witness says. The second visitor question now asks whether Judas acknowledged his wrongdoing; Sarto's answer explicitly mentions returned money and confession. Remove the speculative question about what prevents despair.
- **Spiritual Communion:** Replace generic value/personal benefit language with the manuscript's exact sacrament-versus-grace distinction. Keep the question about one person's eligibility as an **out-of-character editorial limit** with no speaking historical sprite.
- **Charitable correction:** Replace a second paraphrase of the same objection with questions directly answered by §13: the coexistence of rebuke and patience, and charity's willingness to await fruit.
- **FR:** Maintain one-to-one doctrinal counterparts of each English proposition. No faux-plural papal `Nous`; avoid making the French a separately elaborated teaching.
- **Original authoring data:** Inherited original Q&A and v1 experiment **not overwritten**; the HTML review reads v2 in its experimental comparison slot only. No release script has been merged or deployed.
