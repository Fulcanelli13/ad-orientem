## Rejected examples that explain the failure mode

- **Notre-Dame-de-Pontmain:** four photographs depict the similarly dedicated chapel in **Malestroit, Morbihan**, rather than the indexed traditional-Mass chapel at 2 rue de la Grange, **Pontmain, Mayenne**. Commons independently describes the Malestroit chapel: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Chapelle_Notre-Dame-de-Pontmain_(Malestroit).
- **Igreja São Benedito:** the photographed building is in **Itu**, not the Directory's São Paulo city venue, despite a shared saint and state: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Igreja_S%C3%A3o_Benedito_(Itu).
- **Vyšší Brod monastery church:** the photographs refer to the distinct **Klokoty** religious complex at Tábor: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Klokoty_Monastery.
- **Chapelle Saint-Aubain in Namur:** photographs of a memorial tablet in **Cathédrale Saint-Aubain**, not the separate chapel in Rue Delvaux 8: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Interior_of_the_St_Aubin%27s_Cathedral,_Namur.
- Further source photos depict a funerary sculpture in the Agen museum, an unrelated cathedral side chapel in Besançon, Sainte-Honorine references inside another church, a priory in Sainte-Croix-en-Jarez or Dordogne (not Croix, Nord), and Saint-Caprais churches at Carsac-Aillac/Toulouse rather than the target Mechmont building.

## Corroborated physical church subjects (not published)

- **Fatimakapelle, Schardenberg:** direct building-specific Commons category and the FSSP's **summer** Mass venue. Important exception: the FSSP uses the **parish church instead in winter**. The two physical buildings must not be conflated. Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Fatimakapelle_(Schardenberg) and https://petrusbruderschaft.de/pages/wo-wir-sind/oesterreich/niederlassungen/linz/messzeiten-termine.php.
- **Wallfahrtskirche Maria Lebing, Hartberg:** four images in the dedicated Commons church category, official FSSP site at Lindenweg 1, and independent Austrian monument record showing the same church/address. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Maria_Lebing ; https://petrusbruderschaft.de/pages/wo-wir-sind/oesterreich/niederlassungen/wien/hartberg.php ; https://www.bda.gv.at/dam/jcr%3A731c5ac3-1376-4b69-aba7-b44a25994ee8/Verordnung_Hartberg.pdf
- **St Mary's Cathedral, Sydney:** Commons source explicitly documents the cathedral name, street address, landmark identity, photograph viewpoint and Quality Images grouping. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sydney_(AU),_St_Mary%27s_Cathedral_--_2019_--_3107.jpg
- **St Clemens Basilica, Hannover:** four cathedral- and crypt-related photographs in the building's exact Commons category, matching the address 'Platz an der Basilika'. Crypt photographs are **not** automatically suitable as primary cover images. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:St._Clemens_(Hannover).
- **Cathédrale Sainte-Marie, Bayonne:** the misleading filename says *Sainte-Cécile*, but the photographer's caption, Mérimée identifier PA00084329 and object location establish the **correct** Sainte-Marie Cathedral. Filename-only rejections would have wrongly discarded it. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:France-Bayonne-Cathedrale_Sainte-Cecile.jpg
- **Chapelle Saint-Éloi, Réville:** exact dedication and village, supported by image's French Commons documentary category and matching frozen site; maintain publication hold until image composition and source checks. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Quality_images_of_Manche
