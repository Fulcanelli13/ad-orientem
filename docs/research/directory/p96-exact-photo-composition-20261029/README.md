## Site-side comparisons

- **Maria Lebing (Austria):** #15 better entrance portrait than oblique #14, although weather and trees remain.
- **Prague St Peter at Poříčí:** #52 best distinctive towers image among #51–54; #54 is only a window detail and should not be used as a whole-church hero.
- **Notre-Dame des Armées, Versailles:** #140 is stronger than #137 as a facade portrait. #138 (stained glass) and #139 (Station of the Cross) are for possible detail contexts only. Peter Potrowl's Commons file pages list CC BY-SA 4.0 and request visible attribution; **no special paid permission or owner contact is needed for research**.
- **Duingt:** #149 is the clearest entire side elevation with Alps; #150 pleasant three-quarter exterior, #148 useful aerial context, #147 a valid nave but darker and with modern freestanding altar visible.
- **Toulon St François de Paule:** #156 best interior/nave image, #157 strongest exterior portrait; #155 and #158 have traffic and urban visual clutter.
- **Blandford Forum:** #195 best unobstructed exterior of four; #193 secondary, #194 sun flare, #192 foreground car. Commons reports the four photographs as self-published CC0, **but original file metadata and reuse status are not independently cleared here**.
