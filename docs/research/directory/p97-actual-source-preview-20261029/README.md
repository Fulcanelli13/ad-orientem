## Findings that change photo selection, not church identity

- Hannover St Clemens: #61 is stronger full-building exterior than #60 (dome only); #62 is an interior crypt chapel; #63 is **outside** the crypt, dominated by a crypt sign and stair.
- Wrexham Cathedral: #178 is the clearest entire facade; #176 depicts windows only; #177 is obscured by a large commercial van.
- Saginaw Holy Family: #344 depicts church doors, #345 side elevation, #346 a small roof-cross detail at 835×664 resolution.
- Grafing Marktkirche #76 is a Saint Florian statue, **not** a church-wide exterior. Earlier #75 is the stronger whole-building portrait.
- St Thomas Ann Arbor #375 is a window/stone detail; #374 heavily obscured exterior, #376 clearer tower view. Previously reviewed #377 remains available as a broader building candidate.
- Bayonne #96 depicts the correct Sainte-Marie cathedral from the cloister; the misleading title 'Sainte-Cecile' is not a different church.
- Saint-Michel Ingouville #119 is a recognisable exterior, though bare branches cast harsh shadows. Honolulu #326 is only a doorway.
