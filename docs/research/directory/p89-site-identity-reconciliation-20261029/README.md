## New P89 sourced identity failures

- **Photographs 1–4 (Argentina):** the photos actually depict *Capilla de Nuestra Señora del Rosario, Santo Domingo Este, Dominican Republic*, NOT the SSPX chapel at **Pasaje Independencia 3277, Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina**. **The Commons category is explicit**; `SDE` stands for Santo Domingo Este, not for an Argentine locality. References: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Capilla_de_Nuestra_Se%C3%B1ora_del_Rosario%2C_Santo_Domingo_Este and https://fsspx-sudamerica.org/es/capillas.
- **159–161 (France):** filenames specify Saint-Germain churches in **Cintheaux**, **Montceaux-lès-Provins** and **Saint-Germain-sur-Ay**, whereas the canonical Directory Mass church is Saint-Germain in **Auros, Gironde**. Evidence: https://pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/merimee/PA00087114 and https://www.cirkwi.com/en/point-interet/4835011-eglise-saint-germain-xvieme-xviiieme; intended source https://icrspfrance.fr/?org=3.
- **274–277 (Mexico):** images show **Hotel Misión Mérida Panamericana**, not the traditional Mass ministry or worship building indicated by **“Misión — Merida.”** The word *misión* in a venue name is not a license to import photographs of an unrelated hotel. Source: https://fsspx.mx/es/capillas-2.
- **299–302 (Philippines):** the photos are from **Our Mother of Perpetual Help Chapel at Camp Olivas Police Regional Office 3, Barangay San Nicolas**; the indexed traditional-Mass chapel is in **Perpetual Heights Subdivision**. These have the **same dedication and city but are distinct physical institutions**. References: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Our_Mother_of_Perpetual_Help_Chapel_(Camp_Olivas%2C_San_Fernando%2C_Pampanga) and https://claretianpublications.com/directory/military-ordinariate-of-the-philippines/.
- **320–322 (US):** images of the **Ave Maria chapel in Beltinci, Slovenia** incorrectly assigned to **Ave Maria Parish, Florida**. Reference: https://www.documenta-pannonica.eu/built-cultural-heritage/st-ladislaus-church/.
- **358–359 (US):** photographs from **Saint Lawrence Church, Cincinnati, Ohio** incorrectly assigned to **Sacred Heart Church, 321 South Broadway, Lawrence, Massachusetts**.
- **360–362 (US):** source photo titles identify buildings in **Springfield, Massachusetts; Springfield, Ohio; Springfield, Kentucky** although the target site is **Sacred Heart Church, 730 S 12th Street, Springfield, Illinois**.

## Why this is not another superficial metadata exercise

The preceding nine-sheet manual image inspection could reject cars, landscapes and stadiums but passed recognisably religious photographs of **the wrong buildings**. P89 now compares **the photographed subject's documented city/site** with the actual physical Mass-site address in the frozen CL2 source register. Wrong-site rejection requires affirmative, independently grounded contradiction; uncorroborated matches remain on hold, not approved.

Among the 183 original site IDs, the frozen site register contains 77 street-or-number text claims, 44 other locality/building-only descriptions and 62 without a proven site address by conservative text screening. **Those are source-text evidence grades, not fresh geocoding or exact-site confirmations**. They determine what type of independent verification is needed next.

The permanent complete source ledger is `p89-full-385-source-reconciliation.json`, with all 385 source/file identities and reason strings. P87 remains a historical preview archive only, not an approved editorial shortlist. Do not ask the user to sort the 315 unreconciled images.
