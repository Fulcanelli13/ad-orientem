P87 AD ORIENTEM: ONE OFFLINE WORLDWIDE PHOTO REVIEW

Unzip this complete research package. Open index.html in a browser. Thumbnails live in thumbs/ and require no network.
This is the SAME 385-source P86 review with stable review IDs and the existing decisions storage namespace.
Every image is a small transform of a Wikimedia Commons source, NOT a verified image of the exact listed building.
Heuristic technical flags, source conflicts, blurred/low contrast images and cross-site visual matches need human visual inspection.
DO NOT treat a Keep decision as approval, rights clearance, building identity certification or permission to publish.
Do not treat failed downloads as rejection; they remain holds. No original files or approved image derivatives are in this ZIP.
The contact-sheets/ folder contains whole-world visual sheets for rapid internal inspection.
Export all decisions once from the single review HTML. Keep localStorage and preserve all prior Keep/Reject/Hold decisions.
