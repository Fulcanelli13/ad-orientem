## Editorial triage conclusions

1. **Verbatim source preservation:** 35 original long Guide texts remain unchanged; **no source-faithful EN/FR replacements** are approved or generated.
2. **Author quotation integrity:** these are historical **source-synthesis manuscripts**, not direct quoted words of Guéranger, Oakeley, Lasance, O'Connell, St Vincent Ferrer or St Francis de Sales. Do not attribute any editorial sentence directly to an author without finding its exact original textual witness.
3. **Practical rubrics:** original workbook mixes the celebrant's prescribed actions, a server's duties and faithful postures/local customs. Each instruction needs actor/ritual-form screening.
4. **Different historical epochs:** full Guides include pre-1960 descriptions, actual 1960–62 rules and postconciliar comparison. Such comparisons can be historically useful but must never supply the controlling 1962 Mass authority.
5. **Temporal/cue issues:** retain separate earlier 18 short-copy correction candidates, especially both elevations (not simultaneous with the beginning of the consecratory words), the doxology *Per ipsum*, the conditional Communion bell, dismissal and blessing. These are not approved copy replacements.
6. **Specific Low Mass participation:** the 1958 Instruction of the Sacred Congregation of Rites, *De musica sacra et sacra liturgia*, §§28–34 distinguishes attentive interior participation, suitable prayers/hymns and spoken Latin dialogue responses. Source: https://adoremus.org/1958/09/instruction-on-sacred-music/ . This is not an unconditional obligation to shadow the unheard priestly words or a universal faithful-posture chart.
7. **Ongoing evidence holds:** source locators labelled `Archive OCR`, uploaded nonpublic PDFs or pre-/post-1962 books require file/page identity and exact claim confirmation. A provenance URL is not evidence of full facsimile verification.
8. **No duplicate source owner:** maintain original Guide ID `LOW.001..LOW.035` and original evidence IDs. Do not generate a parallel published Guide registry or another reader.
