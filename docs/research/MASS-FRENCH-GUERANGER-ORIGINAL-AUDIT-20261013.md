## Witness and source hierarchy

Original French witness: Prosper Guéranger, *Explication des prières et des cérémonies de la Sainte Messe*, notes taken at his conferences, second edition, Solesmes, 1885. Readable [French transcription](https://www.domgueranger.net/explication-des-prieres-de-la-sainte-messe-2/); bibliographic [1885 original](https://books.google.com/books/about/Explication_des_pri%C3%A8res_et_des_c%C3%A9r%C3%A9mo.html?id=7Dl_LJtRf78C).

**Critical edition note:** This 1885 text is a posthumously edited conference-note compilation; its own preface acknowledges transcription imperfections. It is original-French evidence for Guéranger's teaching, **not** a verbatim autograph and not the controlling 1962 ceremonial text. Verify contested historical dates or exceptional-rite claims against 1962 Ritus/Code and scholarly evidence.

Normative controls: [Ritus servandus 1962](https://archive.ccwatershed.org/pdfs/5276-ritus-servandus-translated-english/download/) and [1960 General Rubrics](https://propria.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/the-new-rubrics-of-the-roman-missal-and-breviary-1960-by-rev.-patrick-l.-murphy.pdf); the 1962 priest sources and Mass form/profile precedence already in the repo.

**Method:** All guidance fragments below are *original, concise modern French paraphrases*, not copied modern translations and not literal Guéranger quotations. Each is tied to the corresponding original **French heading** and stable cue ID; a French explanation does not establish the postures of all the faithful. No generic sentence is to replace the in-text factual priest rubric. These are proposed dynamic-guide inputs only.

## Particularly important findings compared with previous Guide material

1. **Gospel topology:** the priest's station at the Gospel side is related to the deacon's proclamation in the Solemn Mass prototype. Guéranger describes the deacon's three small signs on himself and the cross on the Gospel book; the faithful's gesture must be independently justified (original text `ÉVANGILE`, around `Initium/Sequentia`).
2. **Inchoate Offertory:** Guéranger explicitly distinguishes the still-unconsecrated bread/wine from the sacramental Host that will follow. At `Suscipe sancte Pater`, the prayer anticipates the coming Sacrifice (`OFFERTOIRE`).
3. **Theological commentary versus priest action:** `Hanc igitur` hands extended are historical symbolic interpretation, while the extension itself is governed by `Ritus VIII.4`. `Quam oblationem` is strong enough to justify different commentary at `C0164` and at `C0165–C0166`.
4. **Critical Eucharistic atomic order:** `C0173` / `C0174` and `C0179` / `C0181` contain distinct words, first genuflection, elevation, replacement and second genuflection. These distinct actions belong **inline**, while `FRG22` explains why the already-consecrated Species receive adoration.
5. **No automatic Passion allegory:** Guéranger specifically cautions against forcing every Mass action into a separately matched episode of Christ's Passion (introductory `EXPLICATION DE LA MESSE`).
6. **Pastoral relevance:** at `Sursum corda`, he explicitly counsels the faithful not to remain seated if possible. This is a *historical devotional exhortation*, not a new absolute pew posture law; its 1962 application needs the O'Connell/community profile gate.
7. **Historical anomalies need quarantine:** Guéranger's sometimes categorical claims about history or Requiem/other older missal endings (especially Last Gospel and Ite) must be checked against the 1960–1962 rubric, not copied into current instructions.
