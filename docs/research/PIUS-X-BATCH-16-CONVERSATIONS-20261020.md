# St Pius X — Batch 16: 50 bilingual Creed conversations

**Status:** source-anchored bilingual **editorial research drafts**, not published; historical-voice, theology, EN/FR translation and original-answer sentence certification all pending.

**Primary Italian witness:** [1905 longer Catechism, Part I — Apostles' Creed, Q62–111](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-prima-il-credo-o-simbolo-apostolico/). The original questions below retain their Italian form from the museum's digital transcription. Its original Italian answers are linked, not presented as personally uttered words of Giuseppe Sarto. The catechism was prescribed under his authority but was not personally authored throughout by him.

**Production:** 50 families / 100 turns / 200 localized draft replies. The practical follow-up in each family is grounded in the **same** original Italian question, not an automatically repeated adjacent question. Numbered historical source anchors are unique across Batches 3–16; this is not a claim of 50 semantically unique visitor intents.

### SPX-1905-C-062

**Original Italian [PX1905-Q62](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-prima-il-credo-o-simbolo-apostolico/):** Questo peccato è proprio solamente di Adamo?

**Visitor (EN):** Was Adam's first sin only his own?

**Draft response (EN):** The Catechism distinguishes Adam's personal act of disobedience from original sin inherited by his descendants, who did not personally commit that act.

**Visiteur (FR) :** Le premier péché d'Adam n'appartient-il qu'à lui ?

**Projet de réponse (FR) :** Le Catéchisme distingue la désobéissance personnelle d'Adam du péché originel hérité par ses descendants, qui n'ont pas commis cet acte personnellement.

**Practical follow-up (EN):** Does a newborn bear personal responsibility for Adam's choice?

**Draft follow-up (EN):** No. The text explicitly says the sin is personal in Adam but original in those who inherit the fallen condition.

**Relance pratique (FR) :** Un nouveau-né est-il personnellement responsable du choix d'Adam ?

**Projet de suite (FR) :** Non. Le texte dit expressément que le péché est personnel en Adam, mais originel chez ceux qui héritent de la condition déchue.

### SPX-1905-C-063

**Original Italian [PX1905-Q63](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-prima-il-credo-o-simbolo-apostolico/):** Com’è possibile che il peccato originale si trasfonda in tutti gli uomini?

**Visitor (EN):** How does this Catechism explain the transmission of original sin?

**Draft response (EN):** It teaches that the gifts bestowed on humanity in Adam were entrusted to the first parent on condition of obedience; through his disobedience descendants inherit human nature deprived of those gifts.

**Visiteur (FR) :** Comment ce Catéchisme explique-t-il la transmission du péché originel ?

**Projet de réponse (FR) :** Il enseigne que les dons accordés à l'humanité en Adam lui furent confiés sous condition d'obéissance ; par sa désobéissance, ses descendants héritent d'une nature humaine privée de ces dons.

**Practical follow-up (EN):** Is it a claim that every descendant repeated the same personal act?

**Draft follow-up (EN):** No. It describes a condition received through human origin, not an individually chosen first transgression.

**Relance pratique (FR) :** Cela signifie-t-il que chaque descendant a répété le même acte personnel ?

**Projet de suite (FR) :** Non. Il décrit une condition reçue par l'origine humaine, non une première transgression choisie individuellement.

**Projet de suite (FR) :** Non. L'enseignement concerne la condition déchue commune, non un diagnostic de la souffrance d'une personne.
