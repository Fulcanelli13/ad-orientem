## Concrete source-quality defect to eliminate
At least four gap explanations rely on the translated `Ritus servandus` as their only source witness (as counted in the existing Phase 3 ledger). A link to rubrics is sufficient to identify ceremonial instructions, but cannot independently establish broad Scripture or theological interpretations. For each final claim, record a direct passage-level liturgical, biblical, magisterial or named commentator witness; classify illustrative analogies as interpretations rather than historical derivations.

## Acceptance rule
Mark a topic `CERTIFIED` only when (1) its exact English and French claims match approved passages, (2) original 1962 form restrictions are correct, (3) biblical version/numbering and citation URLs resolve precisely, (4) historical source attribution is accurate and not duplicated, and (5) both languages receive independent review. Mechanical coverage tests alone cannot certify it.
