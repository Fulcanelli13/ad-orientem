## Specific findings requiring preservation

**01 — Conditional Judica.** The 1962 Missal's *Ritus servandus* III.6 omits Psalm 42 at Requiem and designated Passiontide Masses; III.12 provides further complete preliminary-prayer omissions in special circumstances. Guéranger relates the Psalm's opening verse to the altar. Biblical source [Psalm 42 Vulgate](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/psa042.htm); this is not one compulsory universal step. Sources: [Missal](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt); [Guéranger](https://www.domgueranger.net/explication-des-prieres-de-la-sainte-messe-2/).

**02 — Isaiah's coal and Munda cor meum.** [Guéranger, Évangile](https://www.domgueranger.net/explication-des-prieres-de-la-sainte-messe-2/) explicitly relates this prayer for purified heart/lips to the angel purifying Isaiah's lips in [Isaiah 6:5–7](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Bible_Crampon_1923/Isaïe); [Missal](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt), VI, and [O'Connell](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/d-The%20Celebration%20of%20Mass-%20O%27Connel%201962_OCR.txt), *Munda cor meum*, establish the priest/deacon ritual distinctions. It is an author-attested narrative allusion, not words identical to Isaiah and not proof the priest reenacts the vision.

**03 — The Introit has more than one type of explanation.** [Gihr's pages 15–19](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Livre:Nicolas_Gihr,_traduction_Moccand_-_Le_Saint_Sacrifice_de_la_Messe,_son_explication_dogmatique,_liturgique_et_asc%C3%A9tique.djvu) treat the antiphon/verse/doxology as establishing the day's tone; [Guéranger](https://www.domgueranger.net/explication-des-prieres-de-la-sainte-messe-2/) discusses its processional Psalm ancestry; [DTC](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionnaire_de_th%C3%A9ologie_catholique/MESSE_VIII._La_messe_dans_la_liturgie_10._La_liturgie_romaine) examines historical developments. The production Proper must choose the actual day and preserve special-season variants. Gihr's nineteenth-century vigil rubrics are not normative for the 1962 Holy Week.

**04 — Kyrie meaning versus origins.** [Guéranger](https://www.domgueranger.net/explication-des-prieres-de-la-sainte-messe-2/) interprets the three groups of three invocations in Trinitarian terms, while [DTC liturgical development](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionnaire_de_théologie_catholique/MESSE_VIII._La_messe_dans_la_liturgie_1._L%27anaphore_d%27Hippolyte) discusses earlier Greek/litany forms and historically changing arrangement. Never assert that Trinitarian meditation proves original composition of the ninefold form.

**05 — Distinguish ancient offering, Offertory prayer, Scriptural typology.** The Missal's *Suscipe sancte Pater* is not Leviticus copied, nor is Guéranger's date attribution automatically proven. For the chalice, *calicem salutaris* resembles [Clementine Psalm 115:4](https://vulgate.org/ot/psalms_115.htm), but the full prayer is a different liturgical composition. In [Daniel 3:39–40](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Bible_Crampon_1923/Daniel), Azariah's humility provides a notably adapted wording for *In spiritu humilitatis*, which [Missal](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt) VII.5 and [O'Connell](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/d-The%20Celebration%20of%20Mass-%20O%27Connel%201962_OCR.txt) place after offering the chalice. Do not portray furnace scene as taking place in Mass.

**06 — Two distinct Psalms at the Offertory.** [Psalm 140:2 Vulgate](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/psa140.htm) supplies a text about rising incense when the liturgical incensation occurs; [Psalm 25:6–12 Vulgate](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/psa025.htm) is the Lavabo formula. [Guéranger](https://www.domgueranger.net/explication-des-prieres-de-la-sainte-messe-2/) separately discusses practical handwashing and moral purification. Incensation is not a mandatory action at every Low Mass.

**07 — Historical claims require cross-author criticism.** Guéranger says the ancient Canon was everywhere word-identical; [DTC on the early anaphora](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionnaire_de_théologie_catholique/MESSE_VIII._La_messe_dans_la_liturgie_7._Le_De_sacramentis_du_Pseudo_Ambroise) documents significant sequence/text differences. Retain Guéranger's spiritual explanation; withhold universal immutability assertion.
