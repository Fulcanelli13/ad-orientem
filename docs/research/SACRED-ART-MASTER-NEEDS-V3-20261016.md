### Five acts and their artistic emphases

1. **Creation and first promise:** Creation, Fall, Noah, origins, covenant-promise visual anchors.
2. **Preparation of Israel:** Patriarchs, Exodus/Sinai, settlement, judges, David and Solomon, prophets/exile, Tobit, Judith, Esther, Daniel and Maccabees; OT–NT typology.
3. **Fulfilment in Christ:** Annunciation, Nativity, ministry, Passion, Resurrection, Eucharist, Marian and apostolic encounters. Use the strongest multi-module reuse first.
4. **The Church:** Pentecost, Apostles, St Paul, missions, martyrs and the Acts narrative.
5. **New Creation:** Christ in glory, final judgement and New Jerusalem/Apocalypse iconography, with precise depicted scene versus typological assignment.

The 12-arc planning matrix is still research-only in PR #1310. The existing 37 Atlas reading groups remain canonical navigation. A separate earlier 78-chapter narrative outline must not be used as 78 new image obligations.

## Selection and acquisition rules (supersede earlier pixel-first policy)

- Prior **0–5** artistic ratings, contextual fit, and older accepted selections are **distinct independent decisions**. Preserve the original selections; do not infer five stars from a selected record. Do not resurface physically rejected 0–2 originals; Fra Angelico and El Greco remain excluded.
- Artistic suitability, photograph quality, exact work identity, painted-surface completeness, external frame, provenance and reuse rights are independent statuses.
- Mobile image quality is judged at actual screen size, fitting the **entire painted surface**; never demand print-sized sources or crop the art itself. Remove external frames only from a separate reversible derivative.
- Modern saints may use an authentic historical photograph or portrait, not only a painting. A historically verifiable likeness may be preferable to an imaginary posthumous painting.
- One canonical **ARTWORK_ID** may link to calendar feast, Gospel verses, Atlas event, covenant stage, character story, genealogical actors, dates, Mass moment, prayer and place. Exact event/saint/physical-original identity must be sourced; names or a shared artist are not proof.
- Major thematic artworks should appear at meaningful moments, not permanently as backgrounds. User-approved images take priority over speculative discovery.
- Legal reuse rights apply **per photograph**; never confuse rights in the old painting with museum/photographer terms or automatically publish a research download.
