## Product correction — preserve the user's three distinct destinations

1. **Mass:** existing 1962 Mass player; rubrics, practical guide, ceremony, posture, audible/silent and response cues. Explanatory theology is not the player’s core responsibility. No replacement of the 48-card LIVE engine.
2. **Study of the Mass:** stand-alone systematic reference for beginners and advanced readers. It follows **the liturgical sequence**, incorporating the entirety of the recovered historical, theological, biblical, ceremonial and critical material, with progressive depth, original source passages and illustrations where appropriate.
3. **Mystery of the Mass:** a distinct experiential/narrative module with **two selectable arcs**, not a synonym for the existing eight-lesson `learn.mass` curriculum:
   - **Biblical chronology — meditative:** salvation history and genuinely relevant sacred artwork lead; Bible text, passage context and authored meditation; selected Mass prayer/action associations available as supporting references.
   - **Liturgical chronology — descriptive:** the actual 1962 Order of Mass leads; incorporates source-linked **Study of the Mass** explanations and ceremonials; scripture is linked through the canonical Bible reader and can expand to contextual/full passages.

The existing eight lessons, 39 source-first research owners, 48 research subtopics and reader's 48 presentation cards are **four different inventories**, not competing product architecture. Earlier #1193 statements saying the Mystery is merely one eight-stage course plus Guide are **superseded as a product requirement**. Preserve its bilingual research *data* for editorial reuse without imposing its old product framing.

Historical or devotional associations **must not** be dressed up as biblical facts or historical origins of priestly motions.
