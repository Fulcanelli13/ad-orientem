# Rosary — actual original-source contact gallery (research only)

**This is not the final editorial review or an app release.** Each image is an archived original-source photograph selected under the existing Prayer artwork ledger. None has passed full visual/crop QA or app photo-rights certification. The Rosary retains one fixed work per mystery, not bead-by-bead rotation.

**11 of 15 mysteries have original photo files staged**, plus the fixed Lotto cover. Three missing photographs and one held mystery remain. The Salve Regina closing picture is also held.

## Closing prayers: Salve Regina

The closing visual remains on hold. The approved Coronation options do not automatically authorize a Salve Regina image.

## Remaining blockers

- **Nativity (third Joyful mystery):** preserve the user-approved Rubens *Adoration of the Magi* association. Reject Prado-origin photo sourcing; find a different photograph of the **same original**.
- **Ascension (second Glorious mystery):** the selected Teniers/Leandro Bassano work has only a 553 × 800 Commons original so far; the higher-quality photograph must be independently verified and licensed.
- **Pentecost (third Glorious mystery):** the Mateo Cerezo original Commons source is only 567 × 479; it needs a higher-resolution photograph of the **same original**.
- **Coronation (fifth Glorious mystery):** primary held. The two existing user-approved exact-mystery alternatives, Lochner and Carracci, require one final editorial choice, not fresh research.
- **Salve Regina closing:** independent held artwork; never substitute a Coronation automatically.

## Archive evidence and publication restrictions

Source files were checked by the original acquisition workflows for SHA-256, exact museum ID or Commons photo page, reported photo licence and native dimensions. Manual framing, removal of photographed frames, proper credits, mobile/desktop crops and app render QA remain pending. **No new painting has been approved, no rejected work restored, and no image published.**
