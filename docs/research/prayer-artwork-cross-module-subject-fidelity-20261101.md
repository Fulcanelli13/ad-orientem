# Prayer artwork — cross-module subject fidelity and attribution audit (1 Nov 2026)

**Scope:** New non-mutating audit of the 267-slot Prayer curatorial master on draft PR #2144. This note supplements (not supersedes) `prayer-artwork-claude-discrepancy-triage-20261101.md`. It does **not** change a primary painting, user approval, negative decision, candidate exclusion, image, release flag or runtime route.

- `data/prayer/artwork-editorial-20261101/curatorial-master-267-20261101.v1.json` — slot, selected original, historical review, holds, alternatives.
- `data/prayer/artwork-editorial-20261030/decision-ledger-b01-b05.v1.json` — original verbatim user instructions and source order.
- `data/prayer/artwork-editorial-20261030/approved-unified-b06-b11-from-user-message.v1.json` — accepted-as-shown options, not a licence to rotate.
- `data/calendar/prayer-art-reviewed117-user-decisions-20261028.v1.json` — original KEEP/REJECT/ELSEWHERE/PHOTO decisions.
- `data/prayer/artwork-editorial-20261101/selected-work-source-crosswalk-reconciled-267-20261101.v2.json` — editorial identity, **not** photo approval.

A painting that **the user intentionally associates with** a devotion is not required to illustrate the exact literal verse. Distinguish `EXACT_SCENE`, `EXPLICIT_USER_AUTHORIZED_CONTEXTUAL`, `INHERITED_CONTEXTUAL_PENDING_SCOPE`, and `SCENE_MISMATCH_NEEDS_REVIEW`. Do not overwrite user-approved thematic associations with an assistant's preferred iconography. Apply scoped rejections only to the rejected original/placement pair; apply explicit user allowances across relevant modules in their written scope.

## Cross-module state that must not be misreported

- 267 positions. After five deterministic restorations and fourteen held primaries, **248 have a selected individual painting**; five category doors are non-individual pools. The twenty-three provisional positions are a different axis: eleven seasonal Home heroes and twelve Prayer-specific placements. “Selected” and “approval-linked” are not equivalent to an exact-slot personal sign-off.
- Original user approval statements cover iconographically **contextual** reuse. This audit explicitly protects Rubens *Adoration of the Magi* for the Nativity mystery and Caravaggio *Incredulity* for the side-wound contexts; it **does not undo** the current 22 alternate-exclusion flags.
- Fourteen Giandomenico Tiepolo San Polo Stations keep their exact sequential one-image-per-station mapping; do not substitute earlier rejected Prado-derived Descent photograph. Fixed cover locks for the user-supplied St Thérèse photo, Lotto Rosary and MNAC Seven Sorrows remain untouched.
- Regroup by **exact original painting identity**, then date/subject and explicit user instruction, then slot. Do not collapse a photographer's name into the painter field; do not conflate thematically similar pictures or an “after” version with the original.
- No newly approved artwork, no blanket rotation of accepted alternatives, no image publication, and no PR merge follows from this note.
