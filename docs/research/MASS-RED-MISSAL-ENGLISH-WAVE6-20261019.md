## One source-owned correction

**C0189 — Roman Canon, *Supplices te rogamus*, scanned p.38 / printed p.37.** Original English had a comma after “Body and Blood of thy Son,” followed by the next cue “may be filled with every heavenly blessing and grace.” This inserts a comma between the dependent subject clause and its predicate. The Red Missal correctly continues the prayer without a comma.

- Original exact cue: `Body and Blood of thy Son,`
- Reviewed English: `Body and Blood of Thy Son`
- Latin: `Cor ✠ pus, et Sán ✠ guinem sumpsérimus,` — **unchanged**.

The unchanged following cue supplies `may be filled with every heavenly blessing and grace.` The dedicated test asserts the **assembled English C0188+C0189+C0190 clause**, absence of the erroneous comma, and unchanged Latin crosses in both LOW and SUNG.

## Decisions to retain

- **Canon of the Dead C0194:** the frozen Latin donor historically omitted `et` in `lucis et pacis`, but an existing separate canonical Latin correction fixes the active text. Its English already says “refreshment, light and peace”; no new editorial replacement warranted.
- **Nobis quoque, Per quem, Per ipsum:** the existing English is a faithful equivalent of the photographed Red Missal's translations. Do not rewrite these solely for stylistic similarity.
- **Pater noster C0209:** the Red Missal uses “trespasses”, while Ad Orientem uses “debts”; `débita nostra` supports the latter. Keep as a **legitimate translation-policy variant**, not a spelling defect.
- **Hæc commixtio C0221:** existing approved English explicitly retains “consecration”, directly reflecting `consecrátio`; Red prints “hallowing”. The current source decision remains sound.
- **Agnus Dei and Communion prayers:** faithful existing English retained, including the priest's three `Domine, non sum dignus` repetitions and the Wave 2 correction to `Quid retribuam`.
- **Second Communion Confiteor:** the booklet, PDF p.46, explicitly says it is **omitted in the 1962 Missal**. Its local/older optional branch in Ad Orientem remains optional and is NOT marked certified or promoted into the ordinary rite by this review.
