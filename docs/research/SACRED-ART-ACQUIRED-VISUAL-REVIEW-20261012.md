## Previously unsettled museum images — now identified precisely

- **NGA 41644 — The Expectant Madonna with Saint Joseph:** correct 2014×4096 original; principally Marian, Joseph at right, historically valuable but not a solo Joseph portrait.
- **Met 442750 — Segna di Buonaventura, Saint Benedict:** original 1681×3887 confirmed accession **24.78c**, not adjacent Saint Silvester 24.78b; retained framed polyptych/architectural construction makes this an archival saint image, not a premium saint portrait.
- **Met 436302 — Workshop of Domenico Fetti, Good Samaritan:** exact dark, hazy donkey/traveler picture; distinct from autograph copies in other museums and weak for mobile legibility.
- **Kress K2017 — Maestro Bartolomé, Entry into Jerusalem:** 5510×7700 original shows Christ, donkey, welcoming crowds, palms and gates. It was **previously selected in an older editorial round**, so it remains preserved, although the Gothic/flattened aesthetic is less suited to the preferred contemporary presentation. Any replacement needs explicit user choice.

## New discovery: the correct companion Immaculate Heart candidate

The historical [Juan Patricio Morlete Ruiz *The Heart of Mary*](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/the-heart-of-mary/TAFcx9ThkUZqHw) at Mexico's Museo Nacional de Arte is identified by its primary art-institution source as the **companion to the already-selected *The Heart of Jesus***. The Marian Heart is pierced with sword and embellished with lilies. Commons preserves a photographic object record at approximately 2,277×2,960 pixels. **This is source discovery, not another acquired original or user approval**. It is added as an unapproved research lead for `DEV-02`. The Google Arts autogenerated date of `1000` is erroneous and explicitly excluded. This is a real Immaculate Heart candidate; *Immaculate Conception* paintings are not substitutes.

## Preserved curatorial safeguards

- One physical source file per source identity, not one JPEG per module; context links distinguish exact scene, named secondary saint, broader devotion or merely adjacent Gospel passage.
- A **painting of the Rest on the Flight** can enrich a Flight-into-Egypt chapter, but is not an exact depiction of the departure. Faed's **Expulsion** belongs to Genesis 3 aftermath, not the temptation. *Supper at Emmaus* illustrates recognition at table, not disciples walking on road.
- Highly detailed multi-saint altarpieces may be excellent historically yet poor saint portrait thumbnails. A painting's authenticity and theological suitability never automatically make it an Ad Orientem homepage hero.
- Older attribution corrections remain in canonical registry: Cleveland 1950.400 is **workshop of Jacopo Tintoretto** and Met 436302 **Workshop of Domenico Fetti**.
- The historical no-El Greco/no-Fra-Angelico selection preference remains in force. Rights held separately for Commons PD-Art and Kress recordings; CC0 from Met/CMA/NGA must still be reconciled to the precise reproduction for release.
