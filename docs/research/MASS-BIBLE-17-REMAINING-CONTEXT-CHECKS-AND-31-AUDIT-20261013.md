## Mandatory corrections generated by source comparison

### Major: MB21 Pater noster is *adapted* biblical wording

Full Clementine Matthew 6:9–13 is not verbatim the Latin Roman Pater: [Matthew 6:11](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat006.htm) uses **panem nostrum supersubstantialem**, whereas [Luke 11:3](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk011.htm) uses **panem nostrum quotidianum**. The [1962 Benziger Missal OCR](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt), in the Order of Mass around lines 35130–35208, has the same **quotidianum/cotidianum** formulation as Luke, not Matthew's supersubstantialem. This requires changing MB21 classification in the continuing **research** source-layer record from DIRECT_SCRIPTURAL_WORDING to **SCRIPTURAL_WORDING_ADAPTED**. Both its original MB21 source record and bilingual editorial copy have now been corrected. Do not infer the entire Roman text is Luke's Gospel wording, either.

### Minor but genuine: MB12 Hosanna adaptation

Matthew 21:9 in the Clementine Vulgate has **Hosanna in altissimis**; the [1962 Sanctus](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/Missale%20Romanum%201962_OCR.txt) has **Hosanna in excelsis**. The earlier MB12 class was already adapted, but the bilingual copy now spells out the reason. Palm Sunday Jerusalem still must not be made identical to Isaiah 6 or Apocalypse 4.

### Precision: MB02, MB10

- MB02 no longer calls the Psalm itself a 'dialogue'; it describes Psalm 42 *prayed responsorially* in the introductory rite. [Rubrics 424–425](https://isidore.co/divinum/www/horas/Help/Rubrics/Missal1960%20rubrics.html) distinguish the omission of the entire preparatory sequence after prescribed processions from **the Psalm-only omission** in Passiontide and Requiem.
- MB10 clarifies the priest's distinct ministerial act and the faithful's spiritual union with the offering. [Romans 12:1](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom012.htm) teaches spiritual self-oblation but does **not** supply the words of *Orate fratres*.

### Scope controls for context-only entries

- Confiteor Luke 18:13, Kyrie Matthew 20:30–31, Te igitur 1 Timothy 2:1–2, and Orate fratres Romans 12:1 remain explicitly **parallels and theological context**, not claimed Mass prayer quotations.
- The Credo's Incarnation, Genesis Abel/Abraham, Hebrews Melchisedech, and the Canon anamnesis have **specific scriptural subjects/claims**, not literal full rite-to-chapter identity. Genesis 22 does not narrate Isaac's execution.
- John 1:1–14 is the actual Last Gospel when prescribed, but [rubrics 509–510](https://isidore.co/divinum/www/horas/Help/Rubrics/Missal1960%20rubrics.html) make it conditional and sometimes prescribe a proper Palm Sunday Last Gospel; it is not a compulsory generic concluding lesson.
- Source texts provided by New Advent show Latin alongside modern-language copyrighted translations. The research citations do not grant republication rights to those modern translations. The authorial drafts are freshly written; editorial rights review is still needed.
