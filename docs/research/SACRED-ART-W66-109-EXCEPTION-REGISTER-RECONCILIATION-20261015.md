# W66 — Canonical exception register for existing 5-star Sacred Art
**15 October 2026 | Research-only overlay on draft PR #1231 | No live deployment or new ratings**

## Decision and scope
W66 is the **single working exception index for the 109 recovered explicit 5/5 record IDs**. It consumes W64's canonical 109-ID authority and supplements it with the source-specific calendar, Saints Map, Biblical Atlas, pilot Saint, prayer/rosary and W65 work. It does not claim a completed lifetime rating audit, 109 separate physical paintings, or 109 cleared reproduction files. Previous 107-ID source is **complementary and incomplete**, not a competing denominator.

Each record retains the human rating/source evidence; physical-object identity; possible mislabelled reviewed photograph; exact/potential subject placements; independently owned module research links; reuse-rights, pixel, resolution/crop and app-render gates; and a next action. Every record has an explicit `eligible:false` publication status pending verified evidence.

Source: [machine-readable W66 register](../../data/research/sacred-art-w66-existing-five-star-canonical-exception-register-20261015.v1.json).
