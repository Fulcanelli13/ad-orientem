# St Pius X — Batch 14: 50 bilingual conversation families

**Research drafts only.** The original Italian questions come from the [1905 Catechism, Part IV](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/) Q817–855 and [Preliminary Lesson](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/lezione-preliminare/) Q1–11. The 1905 text was prescribed by Pius X, drawing on an earlier catechism: these English and French passages are editorial paraphrases, not verified personal papal quotations or officially approved translations.

50 families; 100 turns; 200 localized EN/FR answers. Each pair uses its one original question as support. Source sentences, doctrine, translations, historical voice and runtime release are **not approved**.

### SPX-1905-O-817

**Source:** [Q817](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/). **Italian:** Ha Gesù Cristo istituito immediatamente tutti i gradi dell’Ordine Sacro?

**EN:** Did Christ directly institute every historical grade of Holy Orders?

The 1905 text distinguishes Christ's direct institution of episcopacy and priesthood from the diaconate and lesser orders established through the Apostles. This is its historical presentation, not a complete modern treatment of the sacrament.

**FR :** Le Christ a-t-il directement institué tous les degrés historiques de l'Ordre ?

Le texte de 1905 distingue l'institution directe de l'épiscopat et du sacerdoce par le Christ du diaconat et des ordres inférieurs établis par les Apôtres. C'est une présentation historique, non un traité actuel complet.

**EN follow-up:** Does this settle all later theological debates about the diaconate?

No. The source's classification requires historical and doctrinal context; do not infer that the diaconate is merely an administrative office.

**FR relance :** Cela tranche-t-il tous les débats théologiques ultérieurs sur le diaconat ?

Non. La classification demande un contexte historique et doctrinal ; il ne faut pas en déduire que le diaconat est une simple fonction administrative.

### SPX-1905-O-823

**Source:** [Q823](https://www.museosanpiox.it/documenti-scritti-da-pio-x/il-catechismo-maggiore-di-san-pio-x/parte-quarta-dei-sacramenti/). **Italian:** È peccato disprezzare i sacerdoti?

**EN:** Why does the Catechism warn against contempt for priests?

It connects contempt for Christ's ministers, precisely as ministers, with contempt for the One who sent them. Its strong moral language must not be taken to forbid reporting abuse or criticizing misconduct.

**FR :** Pourquoi le Catéchisme met-il en garde contre le mépris des prêtres ?

Il relie le mépris des ministres du Christ, en tant que tels, au mépris de Celui qui les a envoyés. Son langage moral sévère ne saurait interdire de signaler des abus ou de critiquer des fautes.

**EN follow-up:** May Catholics still challenge a priest's wrongdoing?

Yes. Respect for sacred ministry does not excuse misconduct or suspend responsibilities to safeguard others.

**FR relance :** Les catholiques peuvent-ils dénoncer les fautes d'un prêtre ?

Oui. Le respect du ministère sacré n'excuse aucune faute et ne supprime pas le devoir de protéger autrui.
