`ao-fssp-nne-enyemaka-shrine-umuaka-p-o-box-605-umuaka-imo-state-nigeria`

The shared Geography registry now contains one confirmed:
`LOCATED_AT` Directory→Place link to:
`place:NG:nne-enyemaka-umuaka`

## Pilgrimage models

- Penrose Park — Marian destination with recurring Fatima Day devotion
- Marian Valley — Archdiocesan shrine with First Saturday / Fatima Day Eucharistic processions
- St Peter Chanel — National saint shrine within Ngā Tapuwae Sacred Footsteps
- Pukekaraka — historic Catholic-Māori Marian pilgrimage place
- Ugwogo Nike — national Marian pilgrimage centre
- Nne Enyemaka — traditional Catholic shrine with Sunday TLM and recurring 30 km Rosary processions
- Namugongo — annual 3 June Uganda Martyrs Day pilgrimage
- Munyonyo — Uganda Martyrs destination within the same wider pilgrimage cycle
