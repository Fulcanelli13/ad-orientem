### 1. A village's patronal feast / La fête patronale d’un village

**Occasion:** Feast of the local patron saint; exact day belongs to the parish  
**Scope:** Documented French local example (Chantelle), not universal  
**Proposed owner:** Customs Atlas / Saints

**English — The custom:** In some French Catholic communities the patronal feast brings the faithful together for Mass and public celebration. Processions and village gatherings may accompany it, but the form and date belong to the local church.

**Meaning:** A community honours its heavenly patron through the worship of God and shared Christian life.

**How a family may observe:** Consult the actual parish programme, attend the Mass if possible, and join any announced procession or gathering.

**Français — La coutume :** Dans certaines communautés catholiques françaises, la fête patronale rassemble les fidèles autour de la messe et de réjouissances communes. Une procession ou une fête du village peut l'accompagner, selon les usages locaux.

**Sens spirituel :** La communauté honore son patron céleste par le culte rendu à Dieu et par la vie chrétienne partagée.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Consulter le programme de la paroisse, assister à la messe si possible et participer uniquement aux célébrations effectivement annoncées.

**Weiser:** ch. 23, printed p. 280; observation `WEI58-23-006`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Église catholique en France — Abbaye de Chantelle](https://eglise.catholique.fr/guide-eglise-catholique-france/eveques-dioceses-france/liste-des-dioceses/a-decouverte-dioceses-de-france/diocese-de-moulins/493837-abbaye-de-chantelle/); [Église catholique en France — Assumption 2008](https://eglise.catholique.fr/espace-presse/communiques-de-presse/368107-le-15-aout-fete-de-lassomption/).

**Release gate:** Match real patron saint and selected 1962 calendar before deep-linking a day · Exact patron and 1962 proper must be selected locally, not derived from civil festival..

### 2. Celebrate your baptismal patron / Fêter son saint patron de baptême

**Occasion:** Feast of a personally identified baptismal patron  
**Scope:** Attested Catholic household practice; date and culture vary  
**Proposed owner:** Saints / Customs Atlas

**English — The custom:** The name day honours the saint chosen as one's baptismal patron. Catholic household guides describe prayer, recalling the saint's life, Mass where possible, and a modest family celebration.

**Meaning:** Baptism introduces a person to Christian life and the patron's example offers a personal model of holiness.

**How a family may observe:** Choose the actual patron deliberately; on that saint's traditional feast, pray, read a short biography and mark the day together.

**Français — La coutume :** La fête du saint patron de baptême est une occasion de prière et de réunion familiale. Les anciens manuels catholiques proposent de rappeler la vie du saint, d'assister à la messe si possible et de marquer modestement la journée.

**Sens spirituel :** Le saint patron offre un exemple personnel de sainteté lié à la vie baptismale.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Identifier son véritable patron puis, au jour de sa fête traditionnelle, prier, lire une courte biographie et partager un moment en famille.

**Weiser:** ch. 23, printed p. 280; observation `WEI58-23-008`.  
**Independent evidence:** [EWTN — The Catholic Family Handbook](https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/catholic-family-handbook-10441); [EWTN — My Nameday, Come for Dessert (1962)](https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/my-namedaycome-for-desert-10465).

**Release gate:** Require selected patron and accurate 1962 feast; never infer patron solely from first name · Map named saint explicitly; support multiple patrons and traditional feast/date variations; opt-in only..

### 3. The Assumption procession / La procession de l’Assomption

**Occasion:** 15 August, where a procession is actually celebrated  
**Scope:** Documented French Marian practice; availability locality-specific  
**Proposed owner:** Customs Atlas / Calendar

**English — The custom:** For the Assumption, some French parishes honour Our Lady in a public procession, sometimes carrying her image through the streets. These are local celebrations, not a rite automatically attached to every Mass.

**Meaning:** The faithful publicly honour Mary's Assumption and turn to her intercession.

**How a family may observe:** Attend Mass and join a Marian procession only if your local parish has announced one.

**Français — La coutume :** À l'Assomption, certaines paroisses françaises organisent une procession en l'honneur de Notre-Dame, parfois en portant son image dans les rues. Il s'agit d'une célébration locale, non d'un rite automatiquement ajouté à chaque messe.

**Sens spirituel :** Les fidèles honorent publiquement l'Assomption de Marie et demandent son intercession.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Assister à la messe puis à une procession mariale uniquement si la paroisse en a annoncé une.

**Weiser:** ch. 24, printed p. 289; observation `WEI58-24-005`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Notre-Dame de Paris — Assomption celebrations](https://www.notredamedeparis.fr/solennite-assomption-notre-dame-de-paris/); [Diocese 64 — 15 août 2025](https://diocese64.org/15-aout-assomption-de-la-vierge-marie/).

**Release gate:** No automatic procession flag or duplicate Assumption card; coordinate with PR #1512 · Regional context only; the parish must actually announce a procession..

### 4. Blessing of fishing boats on the Assumption / Bénédiction des bateaux à l’Assomption

**Occasion:** 15 August in specific maritime communities  
**Scope:** Yport / Diocese of Le Havre witness; not a French-wide or universal obligation  
**Proposed owner:** Customs Atlas / regional context

**English — The custom:** In certain French coastal celebrations of the Assumption, a procession and a priestly blessing of fishing boats form part of the local feast. Yport provides a contemporary documented example.

**Meaning:** Seafaring families bring their work, risks and gratitude before God and ask for His blessing.

**How a family may observe:** Attend a publicly announced maritime celebration where offered; the blessing itself belongs to the priest, not the app.

**Français — La coutume :** Dans certaines localités maritimes françaises, l'Assomption comprend une procession et une bénédiction sacerdotale des bateaux de pêche. Yport fournit un exemple contemporain documenté.

**Sens spirituel :** Les familles de marins confient à Dieu leur travail, ses risques et leurs actions de grâce.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Participer à une célébration locale réellement annoncée ; la bénédiction appartient au prêtre et n'est pas donnée par l'application.

**Weiser:** ch. 24, printed p. 291; observation `WEI58-24-014`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Diocese of Le Havre — Assumption Masses, 15 August 2025](https://www.lehavre.catholique.fr/horaires-des-messes-de-lassomption-15-aout-2025/).

**Release gate:** Verify specific local event and ritual source before any ceremony instructions; no synthetic coastal map pins · Optional ministerial blessing; specific rite edition remains unverified; no generalized coast map pins..

### 5. Wypominki: remembering the dead by name / Wypominki : nommer les défunts dans la prière

**Occasion:** November and local parish memorial services  
**Scope:** Polish parish practice, documented in 2025  
**Proposed owner:** Existing DEAD-003 / Calendar

**English — The custom:** In the Polish custom of Wypominki, families entrust the names of deceased loved ones to parish prayer. The names may be read at designated services, whose arrangements differ by parish.

**Meaning:** Praying for the faithful departed is an act of charity and a profession of hope in the resurrection.

**How a family may observe:** Ask a participating local parish how intentions are received, or privately pray for deceased relatives using existing prayers for the dead.

**Français — La coutume :** En Pologne, les wypominki permettent aux familles de confier les noms de leurs défunts à la prière paroissiale. Ces noms peuvent être lus lors d'offices particuliers, selon les usages du lieu.

**Sens spirituel :** Prier pour les défunts est un acte de charité fondé sur l'espérance de la résurrection.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Se renseigner auprès d'une paroisse qui reçoit ces intentions, ou prier chez soi pour ses défunts à l'aide des prières déjà disponibles.

**Weiser:** ch. 26, printed p. 312; observation `WEI58-26-019`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Parafia Matki Bożej Ostrobramskiej — Wypominki 2025](https://parafiaboernerowo.pl/wypominki-2025/); [Parafia Bartąg — Wypominki Parafialne 2025](https://parafiabartag.pl/wypominki-parafialne-2025/).

**Release gate:** No collecting or publishing people's names in Ad Orientem; never create a competing All Souls entry · Maintain confidential family submission practices; connect to DEAD-003; not a prescribed 1962 rite..

### 6. St Joseph's table for the poor / La table de saint Joseph pour les pauvres

**Occasion:** Around 19 March, in communities keeping this custom  
**Scope:** Sicilian-rooted tradition with specific modern US diocesan witnesses  
**Proposed owner:** SJOS-001 / Calendar / Saints

**English — The custom:** The Sicilian-rooted St Joseph's table brings food, hospitality and aid to people in need into the celebration of his feast. Catholic parishes continue to organise charitable versions of the custom.

**Meaning:** St Joseph's quiet care for the Holy Family inspires practical generosity to neighbours.

**How a family may observe:** Pray in honour of St Joseph and share food or offer ordinary help to someone in need; follow a parish celebration where one exists.

**Français — La coutume :** D'origine sicilienne, la table de saint Joseph associe le partage de nourriture, l'hospitalité et l'aide aux personnes démunies. Certaines paroisses catholiques en organisent encore une forme charitable.

**Sens spirituel :** Le soin discret de saint Joseph pour la Sainte Famille inspire une générosité concrète envers le prochain.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Prier saint Joseph puis partager des aliments ou rendre un service à une personne dans le besoin, librement et selon ses moyens.

**Weiser:** ch. 27, printed p. 324; observation `WEI58-27-019`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Diocese of Baton Rouge — St Joseph Altar 2026](https://www.diobr.org/news/st-joseph-cathedral-st-joseph-altar-1); [Our Lady of the Assumption Catholic Church — St Joseph Table 2026](https://www.olachurch.org/post/st-joseph-s-table-and-food-drive).

**Release gate:** Do not claim that a named pastry, miracle account or priestly blessing is universally part of this custom · Do not generalize distinct fritelli pastry from this record; treat famine miracle as legend..

### 7. St Anthony's Bread / Le Pain des pauvres de saint Antoine

**Occasion:** Especially 13 June; voluntary charity throughout the year  
**Scope:** Franciscan charitable practice with a documented French history  
**Proposed owner:** SANT-001 / Saints / Calendar

**English — The custom:** St Anthony's Bread is a charitable tradition supporting the poor in the saint's honour. A Franciscan historical account connects a French form of this work with Louise Bouffier in Toulon in 1890.

**Meaning:** Devotion to a saint bears fruit in the corporal works of mercy, rather than the expectation of favours in exchange for offerings.

**How a family may observe:** On St Anthony's feast, say a prayer and perform a small voluntary act of charity without treating a gift as a transaction.

**Français — La coutume :** Le Pain des pauvres de saint Antoine est une œuvre de charité accomplie en l'honneur du saint. Une histoire franciscaine de cette tradition française la rattache à Louise Bouffier, à Toulon, en 1890.

**Sens spirituel :** La dévotion au saint doit porter des fruits de miséricorde, sans attente de faveur en échange d'une offrande.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Le jour de la fête de saint Antoine, prier et accomplir librement un petit acte de charité sans le traiter comme une transaction.

**Weiser:** ch. 27, printed p. 326; observation `WEI58-27-023`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Ermitage St Antoine — Pain des pauvres](https://st-antoine.org/fr/sanctuaire/faire-un-don/loeuvre-du-pain-des-pauvres/); [Ermitage St Antoine — Saint Antoine de Padoue](https://st-antoine.org/fr/a-propos/saint-antoine-de-padoue/).

**Release gate:** No donations feature, promised favours or unqualified miracle story · Charity rather than guaranteed intercession or favors in exchange for donations; no payment workflow..

### 8. St Christopher's blessing for travellers / La bénédiction des voyageurs de saint Christophe

**Occasion:** Near 25 July, on the date announced locally  
**Scope:** Documented specific French parish celebrations in 2026  
**Proposed owner:** SCHR-001 / Saints / Calendar

**English — The custom:** Some parishes celebrate St Christopher by inviting travellers and drivers to receive a priestly blessing, sometimes with their vehicles. The celebration is a local custom rather than a universal service.

**Meaning:** Christians commend their journeys to God's providence and recall the duty to travel with care for others.

**How a family may observe:** Consult a parish's actual announcement; if it offers the blessing, attend and receive it reverently.

**Français — La coutume :** À l'occasion de saint Christophe, certaines paroisses proposent une bénédiction sacerdotale aux voyageurs, conducteurs et parfois à leurs véhicules. Cette coutume demeure locale et non universelle.

**Sens spirituel :** Les chrétiens confient leurs déplacements à la Providence et se rappellent le devoir de prudence envers autrui.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Vérifier l'annonce de sa paroisse et, si la bénédiction y est proposée, y assister avec respect.

**Weiser:** ch. 28, printed p. 336; observation `WEI58-28-011`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Diocese of Laval — Saint-Christophe du Luat, 19 July 2026](https://paroisse.diocesedelaval.fr/benediction-des-voitures-a-st-christophe/); [Diocese of Angoulême — Saint-Christophe 2026](https://charente.catholique.fr/est-charente/agenda/venez-feter-saint-christophe-2026/).

**Release gate:** No promise of safety, lay blessing substitute, or invented availability · Actual date varies with parish schedule; no promised protection; ministerial blessing not self-performed..

### 9. St Nicholas visits children / Saint Nicolas visite les enfants

**Occasion:** Around 6 December in parts of Alsace and Lorraine  
**Scope:** Documented Alsatian and Lorraine regional culture  
**Proposed owner:** SNIC-001 / Saints / Calendar

**English — The custom:** Regional families remember St Nicholas with a visitor dressed as a bishop, modest treats and accounts of his generosity. Elements of the celebration belong to local folklore and should not be confused with what is known of the historical bishop.

**Meaning:** The day can introduce children to Christian kindness and the duty to share.

**How a family may observe:** Tell the saint's story, pray briefly and share a small treat or act of generosity, without frightening children.

**Français — La coutume :** Dans certaines régions d'Alsace et de Lorraine, saint Nicolas est représenté par un visiteur vêtu en évêque ; des friandises et des récits de sa générosité accompagnent la fête. Certains détails relèvent du folklore local.

**Sens spirituel :** Cette fête peut initier les enfants à la charité chrétienne et au partage.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Raconter sobrement la vie du saint, prier et partager une petite attention ou un acte de générosité, sans faire peur aux enfants.

**Weiser:** ch. 28, printed p. 340; observation `WEI58-28-020`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Diocese of Metz — Hagondange St Nicholas visit, Dec 2025](https://metz.catholique.fr/actualites/actualites-paroisses/469066-hagondange-visite-de-saint-nicolas/); [Diocese of Strasbourg — Local St Nicholas traditions](https://www.alsace.catholique.fr/actualites/dossiers-thematiques/418575-6-decembre-fete-de-saint-nicolas/).

**Release gate:** Label regional folklore clearly; no threatening helpers, required gifts or invented historical certainty · Avoid fear/punishment customs in recommendations, no factual endorsement of miracle folklore..

### 10. The traditional St Patrick ribbon cross / La croix de rubans de saint Patrick

**Occasion:** 17 March, historical Irish household and children's custom  
**Scope:** Historically attested Ireland; current continuity varies  
**Proposed owner:** Customs Atlas / St Patrick profile

**English — The custom:** Irish folklore collections preserve the practice of making or wearing a small decorative St Patrick's cross, sometimes of ribbon and card, especially among children. It is a historical custom rather than a universal Catholic observance.

**Meaning:** The cross can serve as a visible reminder of Christian faith and the national saint's witness.

**How a family may observe:** As a voluntary historical craft, make a modest decorative cross and use the occasion to learn about St Patrick.

**Français — La coutume :** Les collections folkloriques irlandaises conservent le souvenir de petites croix de saint Patrick en ruban ou en carton, autrefois portées notamment par les enfants. Cette coutume historique n'est pas une pratique catholique universelle.

**Sens spirituel :** Une croix décorative peut rappeler la foi chrétienne et le témoignage du saint national.

**Comment la pratiquer :** À titre d'activité historique libre, confectionner une petite croix décorative puis découvrir un épisode de la vie de saint Patrick.

**Weiser:** ch. 27, printed p. 323; observation `WEI58-27-012`.  
**Independent evidence:** [National Museum of Ireland — St Patrick Folklife Collection](https://source.museum.ie/en-IE/Collections-Research/Folklife-Collections/Folklife-Collections-List-%281%29/Religion-and-Calendar-Customs/St-Patrick); [National Folklore Collection UCD — Schools' Collection](https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbes/4644827/4613040/4651174).

**Release gate:** Acknowledge variation of forms and decline of custom; not an approved devotional sacramental · March 17 date from 1962 calendar; household craft is not prescribed worship · Flag historical period, not universal current usage..

### 11. Ask St Anthony's intercession for lost things / Prier saint Antoine pour les objets perdus

**Occasion:** Anytime; especially linked to 13 June saint profile  
**Scope:** Broadly known Franciscan/Catholic devotion; historic origin story uncertain  
**Proposed owner:** Existing Pray / Saints profile (enrichment)

**English — The custom:** A longstanding Catholic devotion turns to St Anthony of Padua when something has been lost. Franciscan sources document this association, while popular stories explaining its origin should not automatically be presented as established history.

**Meaning:** Requesting a saint's intercession is an occasion for prayer and trust in God, not a guarantee of recovering an object.

**How a family may observe:** Offer a simple personal prayer asking St Anthony's intercession and continue a sensible search; use any existing sourced prayer rather than inventing a ritual.

**Français — La coutume :** Une dévotion catholique ancienne consiste à demander l'intercession de saint Antoine de Padoue lorsqu'un objet est perdu. Des sources franciscaines attestent cet usage, mais les récits populaires sur son origine ne constituent pas tous des faits historiques établis.

**Sens spirituel :** Demander l'intercession d'un saint est un acte de prière et de confiance, non une garantie de retrouver l'objet.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Dire une courte prière personnelle à saint Antoine et poursuivre ses recherches avec bon sens, sans inventer de rituel.

**Weiser:** ch. 27, printed p. 327; observation `WEI58-27-026`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Franciscan Province of St Anthony of Padua — Cher Saint Antoine](https://chersaintantoine.org/fr/content/aide-moi-trouver); [Ermitage St Antoine — Saint Antoine de Padoue](https://st-antoine.org/fr/a-propos/saint-antoine-de-padoue/).

### 12. Soul breads and almsgiving for the departed / Pains des âmes et charité pour les défunts

**Occasion:** All Souls season; Polish historical local examples  
**Scope:** Historical surveyed Polish communities; not independently established for France  
**Proposed owner:** DEAD-003 related / Customs Atlas historical context

**English — The custom:** Polish ethnographic records preserve examples of bread or baked food being given to poorer neighbours in connection with remembrance of the departed. This is a historically attested form of charity, not an obligation on 2 November.

**Meaning:** Prayer for the dead and generosity toward the living meet in concrete works of mercy.

**How a family may observe:** Pray for the faithful departed and, if appropriate, share food or assist someone in need as an optional charitable gesture.

**Français — La coutume :** Des archives ethnographiques polonaises attestent que l'on distribuait du pain ou des pâtisseries aux personnes pauvres à l'occasion de la commémoration des défunts. Il s'agit d'une coutume historique locale, non d'une obligation au 2 novembre.

**Sens spirituel :** La prière pour les morts et la générosité envers les vivants se rejoignent dans les œuvres de miséricorde.

**Comment la pratiquer :** Prier pour les âmes des défunts et, si cela convient, partager des aliments ou aider une personne dans le besoin.

**Weiser:** ch. 26, printed p. 313; observation `WEI58-26-022`.  
**Independent evidence:** [Cyfrowe Archiwum Polskiego Atlasu Etnograficznego — map 43](https://pae.us.edu.pl/index.php/items/show/4913).

**Release gate:** Do not prescribe a special recipe, claim current prevalence, or infer a French parallel · French variants remain separately unverified · No inference that practice is common today or spiritually effective as payment..
