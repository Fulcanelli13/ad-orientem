## More precise CSE independent-source findings

- **CSE004–005 natural law:** Aquinas [I-II q.94 a.2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2094.htm) explicitly orders inclinations by reason; existing answer is aligned at the inspected scope.
- **CSE006 harm:** Mill's original [*On Liberty*, chs I and IV](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/34901/pg34901-images.html) concerns the justification of coercion. Don't portray it as proof that an act is morally right merely because it causes no harm. A source citation to Mill's *Utilitarianism* alone would be insufficient for this particular argument.
- **CSE007 consent:** [David Archard (2022)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1467-9752.12684) explicitly analyses consent's limits and virtues; [Elise Woodard (2026)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/phpe.70008) explores trust and consent. Neither should be represented as a Catholic position or as endorsing every consensual act.
- **CSE008–010 intention, object, circumstances:** [*Veritatis splendor* §§78–82](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_06081993_veritatis-splendor.html) and [Aquinas I-II q.18 aa.10–11](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2018.htm) distinguish moral species from intentions and changing circumstances. Joseph Fletcher's attributed *Situation Ethics* objections require exact original-edition checking before final approval.
- **CSE016,018 conscience/dissent:** [*Donum veritatis* §§23–31](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19900524_theologian-vocation_en.html) permits examination of serious difficulty with non-irreformable teaching while explicitly denying that subjective conscience alone is an autonomous teaching authority. Current answers track the primary passage; Curran's actual qualification must be checked in context.
- **CSE020 habit/addiction:** [Aquinas I-II q.77 aa.6–8](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2077.htm) and [q.78 a.2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2078.htm) distinguish passion, voluntariness and deliberate habit. This is **not** clinical evidence for any diagnosis or prevalence.
- **CSE023,025 temptation/intrusive thoughts:** [Aquinas I-II q.74 aa.6–8](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2074.htm) provides a more direct thought-consent locator than the broad existing q.77 reference. Retain clear distinction between unwanted occurrence of a thought and deliberate internal consent.
- **CSE012–014 biblical/historical change:** direct New Testament context and qualified modern scholarship, including actual proponents of cultural-context objections, remain required before adversarial certification.
