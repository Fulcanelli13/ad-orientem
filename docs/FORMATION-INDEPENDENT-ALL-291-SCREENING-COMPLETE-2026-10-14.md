## Critical issue found: APOL-002 does not answer its question

**Canonical APOL-002: “Are faith and reason opposed?”** The existing draft's four English sections **actually discuss evolution, human origins, Adam, immediate creation of souls and polygenism**. That material correctly cites [*Humani generis* §§36–37](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-xii_enc_12081950_humani-generis.html), but it is a distinct narrower topic and cannot stand as the answer to the faith-and-reason question. This is substantive content-ownership drift, not missing bibliography or poor styling.

**Correction prepared:** a full **four-section bilingual owner replacement** based on [Vatican I's *Dei Filius*, chapter IV](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-ix/it/documents/constitutio-dogmatica-dei-filius-24-aprilis-1870.html), [John Paul II's *Fides et ratio*](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_14091998_fides-et-ratio.html), and [Hume's original challenge in *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion*](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/4583/pg4583-images.html). Keep the previous evolution/Adam research as **a linked case within faith and science**, not deleted, renamed as APOL-002, or promoted into another duplicate canonical owner.
