## Original opposing positions and doctrinal reply

**Same-sex relationships and civil recognition:** Margaret Farley's *Just Love*, p. 295 (sexual relationships) and p. 293 (civil same-sex marriage), is directly excerpted in the [CDF's 2012 Notification](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20120330_nota-farley_en.html), also in [French](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20120330_nota-farley_fr.html). These are bounded real author quotations, **not independent full-book collation**. The CSE084 sterility analogy is a useful illustrative challenge, **not a verified Farley quotation**. Compare [CIC can. 1084 §§1–3](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann998-1165_en.html) with the [CDF's 2003 considerations on civil recognition](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20030731_homosexual-unions_en.html).

**Psychology and dignity:** The [APA's actual policy](https://www.apa.org/about/governance/council/policy/chapter-12b.aspx) says that homosexuality is not a mental disorder, opposes inaccurate therapeutic promises, and documents stigma and discrimination. The [CDF's 1986 letter §§10–12](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19861001_homosexual-persons_en.html) explicitly condemns violent malice and protects the dignity of persons while maintaining its distinction concerning sexual acts. The public reader and opposition now state both honestly; **no clinical claim that orientation is a disease**.

**Sex, gender, dysphoria and medicine:** The [APA's sex and gender explainer](https://www.apa.org/topics/lgbtq/transgender-people-gender-identity-gender-expression) distinguishes biological characteristics from cultural gender roles, and the [APA's 2024 policy](https://www.apa.org/about/policy/transgender-nonbinary-inclusive-care) advocates individualized noncoercive evidence-based clinical care while acknowledging limits of current knowledge. [*Dignitas Infinita* §§55–60](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240402_dignitas-infinita_en.html), [French text](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240402_dignitas-infinita_fr.html), has a different theological evaluation of bodily identity. **§60 expressly distinguishes interventions to resolve genital abnormalities** from sex-change interventions as ordinarily defined in that declaration. No medical society thereby supplies a magisterial moral conclusion; no spiritual authority supplies an empirical diagnosis. The declaration does **not** issue a universal rule for every pronoun or social accommodation.

**Pastoral imbalance:** The [original 2013 Pope Francis interview](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/speeches/2013/september/documents/papa-francesco_20130921_intervista-spadaro.html), [French](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/fr/speeches/2013/september/documents/papa-francesco_20130921_intervista-spadaro.html), critiques insistence on only three sexual/reproductive issues without pastoral context but does not revoke the teachings. Replaced the media-hosted source link with the official Vatican document.

**Shame, modern difficulties, love and science:** Current research on restrictive messages and shame is correlational and context-specific, not evidence that *Catholic doctrine itself* causes every harmful outcome. Freud's 1908 essay is not a documentary source on contemporary digital pornography or delayed marriage. The phrase 'love is love' is **not a Fletcher original-book quotation**, and arguments by clinical organizations must not be recast as sweeping metaphysical positions they never asserted. Each correction retains the actual doctrinal response rather than reducing it to a discussion about editorial process.
