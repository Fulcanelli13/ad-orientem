**Status:** ten research debates, eighty stages individually classified; not eighty full primary-passage or publication certifications. Canonical 150 questions, 55 debates and existing source registries preserved. The 147-question curated reader is unchanged.

## Substantive bilingual repairs

- **CSE012 and CSE013**: objections about what Jesus expressly named and whether St Paul's ethics are time-bound remain genuine issues for apologetics, but the [CDF's letter to Charles Curran (1986)](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19860725_carlo-curran_en.html) merely lists disputed topics; it does not advance the former argument. [Curran's 1987 essay](https://www.religion-online.org/article/roman-catholic-sexual-ethics-a-dissenting-view/) discusses developments in sexual moral theology, but does not contain the latter objection in its drafted form. Both opposing arguments are flagged as **illustrative, not original quotations**.
- **CSE014**: argument from historical change corrected to distinguish discipline and pastoral practice from a doctrinal reversal concerning the **same moral object and authoritative proposition**. No sweeping assertion that all historical cases are straightforwardly resolved; exact texts must establish any alleged contradiction.
- **CSE016 and CSE018**: [*Donum Veritatis* §§23–31](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19900524_theologian-vocation_en.html) explicitly distinguishes degrees of doctrinal assent, permitted loyal difficulties and the rejection of treating subjective conscience as autonomous doctrinal truth. The response to conscience no longer makes the authentic Magisterium sound like just one optional view. Note that §§24–25 acknowledge prudential shortcomings and tension; the source must not be misrepresented as a simplistic doctrine that every prudential judgment is irreformable.
- **CSE020**: [Aquinas I-II q.77 aa.6–7](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2077.htm) on passion and [I-II q.78 a.2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2078.htm) on deliberate conduct through a bad habit are **not identical**. Substance-dependence science from [NIDA](https://nida.nih.gov/publications/drugs-brains-behavior-science-addiction) cannot be transposed uncritically into a clinical diagnosis of sexual addiction. English/French opposition and replies corrected.
- **CSE021**: [Freud's 1908 original essay](https://www.freudedition.net/werke/modern-sexual-morality-and-modern-nervousness/druckschrift) criticizes restrictive sexual mores and alleged nervous suffering; he does not specifically analyze [Aquinas's chastity as temperance, II-II q.151 a.1](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3151.htm). The answer acknowledges harm from unhealthy repression without conceding that virtue entails psychological pathology.
- **CSE007, CSE008 and CSE010**: [*Veritatis Splendor* §§78–83](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_06081993_veritatis-splendor.html) distinguishes moral object from mere physical event, intention or consequences. Joseph Fletcher's claims are available as [original excerpts quoted in John Murray's opposing critique](https://www.christianstudylibrary.org/article/situation-ethics), **not as a complete independent read of Fletcher's book**; preserve this limitation instead of certifying direct original authorship of a reconstructed objection.
