## Key corrections

- **Wrong inherited crosslinks:** Gaboltov's old source pointer was Marianka; Litmanová's old source pointer was also Marianka; Ptujska Gora was linked to an unrelated `marija.si` pilgrim route; Saint-Victor Abbey was inherited from a Notre-Dame de la Garde article. The P21 gate records exact mismatch and/or replaces with the named Catholic custodian/diocese.
- **Physical building hierarchy:** Covadonga has a Basilica and Santa Cueva; Oropa has two basilicas; Aylesford has a priory, Marian shrine and relic chapel; St Augustine's La Leche includes a historic chapel and Shrine Church; the Peñafrancia Basilica in Naga is distinct from the Old Shrine; the new Balasar Eucharistic sanctuary must not be confused with its old parish church or previous tomb. Existing source evidence on Balasar is a September 2026 announcement of dedication planned for 11 October, **not independently verified completion**.
- **Ecclesiastical classification:** Litmanová Zvir is Greek Catholic; Durham Cathedral is Anglican, Uppsala is Church of Sweden and Larnaca's Saint Lazarus is Church of Cyprus. Fourteen additional title-level scope warnings have not yet been independently certified and therefore remain research-only.
- **Heritage:** Skellig Michael is a historic state-managed monastic site, not an active Catholic monastery. Sainte-Chapelle is a state-operated heritage monument; heritage editorial work is separate from current Roman Catholic shrine publication.
- **Title ambiguity:** 'Our Lady of Fatima, Quebec' may mean multiple distinct Catholic sites (including records at La Pocatière and Saint-Hyacinthe). No original-candidate identity is chosen by similarity.
- **Canonical reuse:** Father Laval's tomb may be a sub-site of the already published Centre Père Laval; Mount Nebo's Memorial Church may be the already published Memorial of Moses basilica. The exact physical-unit relationship requires independent closure, not duplicate creation.
- **Country-code repairs:** older `DY` for Benin and `HV` for Burkina Faso mapped to `BJ` and `BF` for research routing only; original candidate IDs unchanged.
