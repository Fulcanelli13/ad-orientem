# Explore R67 — second French illustrated pilgrimage batch

Six independent, official-source bilingual Place guides were added without a new screen or map. Places: Sainte-Thérèse de Lisieux, Notre-Dame de Chartres, Notre-Dame de la Médaille Miraculeuse (Rue du Bac), Notre-Dame de Pontmain, Notre-Dame de Laghet, and Notre-Dame de Rocamadour.

Together these six contain 28 source-linked paragraphs, site-specific stops, historical timelines, documented anniversary/variable-programme markers and official visitor references. The original R66 France guides and five Ireland guides remain canonical and unmodified; Explore now loads three separate guide collections, totalling 14 published long-form guide identities.

### Editorial safeguards

- **Chartres:** Marian relic identity is traditional; do not assert that the Voile de la Vierge has modern archaeological authentication. The cathedral labyrinth is an internal devotional practice, not a reconstructed walking route.
- **Rue du Bac:** distinguish visions reported in 1830, the subsequently remodelled chapel, historic relic custody and current group reservations.
- **Pontmain:** report the 1871 events and 1872 bishop's recognition correctly; 2026 diocesan anniversary processions and the Assumption celebration are not permanent programmes.
- **Laghet:** site is expressly NOT a Marian apparition site. Its 1652 investigated prodigies, sculpted Madonna, ex-votos and nine-loop cloister novena are distinct kinds of evidence/practice.
- **Rocamadour:** the sanctuary acknowledges unknown origins and a body discovered in 1166 whose identity remains uncertain. Neither the Roland story nor the Amadour identification may be stated as historically proven.
- **Lisieux:** distinguish the modern basilica from the Carmel, Les Buissonnets and other physically separate Thérèse sites. A summer 2026 schedule is an example, not a live timetable.
