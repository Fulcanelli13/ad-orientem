# Calendar Ordo month layer — ICRSP reference, 26 October 2026

## Decision
Enhance the **existing** Ad Orientem 1962 Calendar; do not create another Calendar, change the shared day resolver, or replace the Liturgical Clock and month grid.

ICRSP France's https://icrspfrance.fr/ordo.php is a reference for a compact, readable liturgical *ordo*: each date exposes feast, rank, Mass colour, commemorations, and—where the source explicitly supports it—Mass formulary, Gloria, Credo, preface, discipline, and office notes. Their Ordo is **proper to the Institute of Christ the King**, not a universal norm.

## Phase 1 — this PR
- Add an optional **Grid / Ordo** presentation switch *within* the canonical Month tab, preserving the 42-cell grid and its three All / Temporale / Sanctorale filters.
- Render one expandable Ordo entry for every date actually in the displayed month, backed **only** by the same Calendar day cache. No second calendar, independent feast algorithm or parallel source registry.
- Display day, feast title, rank, colour and named commemorations **only when available from the resolved record**. Saved month previews explicitly remain provisional; unavailable days do not invent titles or rank.
- Keep the existing Day and Mass actions as the handoff. The Ordo's action opens the selected day, not an unverified alternate Mass.
- Include EN/FR strings, accessible native details/summary, 44px targets, and existing responsive font/colour system.
- Local, diocesan and religious propers remain explicitly outside this general-Roman projection; successful source loading is not equivalent to independent historical/rubrical certification.

## Phase 2A — source-bounded Day and Month Mass facts (implemented on this branch)
- `src/calendar/ordo-mass-facts.js` projects **only** the general 1962 calendar's resolved principal Mass. It is not a second resolver, chooser, or Mass reader.
- A Latin Introit incipit is displayed when the resolved Proper has a canonical source path and actual text. It is quoted from that Proper, never guessed from the saint or celebration name.
- Preface **heading** is displayed as source-matched only for an identity admitted by the existing narrow `inspect1962PrefaceSelection` register. A contradiction is shown as an unresolved source conflict, without displaying the wrong heading. An unmapped category is withheld; even a complete Latin text is not independent rubrical certification.
- A future typed `ordoRubricDecision` from the Proper owner must have `CERTIFIED` status, the identical principal ID, accepted source identity and explicit evidence before Gloria/Credo flags may appear. Existing free-floating booleans are ignored.
- Alternative/votive Mass options remain **empty**, not autogenerated. Calendar commemorations are observational; the Mass-form engine alone decides which extra orations apply.
- Existing Day Mass/Scripture action and printer are unchanged. Good Friday remains a distinct liturgy, not a Mass.
- Acceptance uses a separate fail-closed unit suite `tests/calendar-ordo-mass-facts.mjs`, followed by the actual Calendar phone E2E. Both are wired to the existing Month workflow.

## Phase 2B — not implemented yet
Source-backed **Mass-specific Ordo** contract, to be developed from the existing canonical Mass resolver and properly adjudicated source records:
1. The exact observance/profile behind the selected principal Mass, including genuine local or religious overrides.
2. Alternative/votive Masses only when separately attested as permissible for the selected date, locality and liturgical profile; do not infer an option from the same saint's name.
3. Gloria, Credo, proper commemoration orations, preface and any special concluding Gospel or ceremonial notes **per formulary**, not as generic day-level booleans.
4. Sunday/feast transfers, local precedence, additional commemorations and their authority.
5. Optional office notes should be a separate attested source; do not derive them from a Mass Proper.
6. Precise citations and a fail-closed state when the source or permission is missing.

## Regression gates

The existing CI workflow runs it for PR changes to the calendar runtime and test.

### Critical mismatch already documented
The 10-date 2026 runtime Preface audit shows the raw October 7 Rosary Proper can contain `Communis` before source-owned recovery to the Marian Preface. The adapter flags the original mismatch instead of displaying `Communis` as liturgically correct. This is a narrow detection rule, **not** a certificate that the remaining days are correct.

All source-backed instructions for selecting other Masses are intentionally excluded until the date- and jurisdiction-specific rubrics are adjudicated.

## Phase 2C — General Roman 1960 rubric screening (implemented)
The read-only evaluator `src/calendar/ordo-1962-ordinary-rule-review.js` combines the actual 1962 source-resolved day identity with the relevant Missal and Breviary rubrics, without changing the 1962 Proper, Mass selection, Missal reader, calendar observer, or ceremonies.

The 1960 Missal [§§431–432, 475–476](https://isidore.co/divinum/www/horas/Help/Rubrics/Missal1960%20rubrics.html) prescribe the Gloria and Creed. The 1960 Breviary [§§237–238](https://isidore.co/divinum/www/horas/Help/Rubrics/Breviary%201960.html) governs when the Office has a Te Deum, a crucial antecedent to Gloria §431(a). The implementation screens specifically:
- **Sunday**: Credo included under §475(a), including when the Sunday's Office is displaced, provided a principal general-Roman source day is resolved.
- **Violet/black Mass**: Gloria omitted by §§432(b,d), but colour never independently establishes a Credo decision.
- **Source-identified Sanctorale feasts of I–III class**: Gloria follows the Office's Te Deum; first-class feasts carry Credo and ordinary third-class feasts omit Credo. The 2nd-class Rosary is treated only under its exact source identity for §475(c); other second-class Lord/BVM/Apostle feasts remain source-review candidates.
- **IV-class green feria per annum**: Gloria and Credo omitted under the Breviary §238(c) and Missal §§431–432, 475–476. The **real production 26 October 2026** uses the generic source identity `:feria:4:w`, *not* a fabricated seasonal-specific ID; this is explicitly accepted only with same-date resolution, IV class, green Mass colour, weekday, and ready Proper. Other generic ferias remain unresolved pending Office-season mapping.

The UI exposes the supporting section and a link to the original General Rubrics under an optional **Rubrical basis** disclosure. Any certified Proper-owner contradiction with an applicable universal rule is withheld and marked source-conflict, not overwritten.

### Source evidence and bulk coverage
The archived source-resolved 2026 full-year audit (`1962-2026-full-year-audit.json`, GitHub Actions) contains **365** dated records with no unresolved dates, comprised of **171** source-identified Sanctorale principal days, **123** Temporale principal days, **59** generic `:feria:4:w` days, and **12** Commune principal days. There are **40** green IV-class generic ferias. These counts are **resolver population coverage only**, not independent rubrical verification.

The full-year audit CI now records two independent booleans-with-evidence for each real resolved date: `ordoRuleReview.gloria` and `ordoRuleReview.credo`, as well as counts of supported and withheld day decisions. This is a screening pass over **all 365 records**, not the 72 independently adjudicated checkpoints in the year-audit fixture. A failed rule or missing Proper never becomes an implicit "No". Its status is `WITHHELD`.

Future work: 1962 source classification for all nontrivial feast categories (second-class feasts of the Lord, BVM, and Apostle birthdays), dynamic octaves and movable ceremonial exceptions, Office Te Deum on white seasonal ferias, vetted Preface assignment across the annual calendar, and local/votive permissions.

## Phase 2D — 365-date source-identified Preface review

**Implemented (audit and presentation only).** `src/calendar/ordo-1962-preface-review.js` applies 1960 Roman Missal §§482–499 to the **canonical resolved principal Mass**, never to a votive alternative, a commemoration, the institute's own Ordo or a second calendar engine. It is deliberately not a replacement for the Mass Reader's `inspect1962PrefaceSelection` and `recoverReaderProperOmissions` pipeline.

The reviewer classifies the actual Latin Preface heading and a narrowly source-identifiable expected category. An explicit mismatched heading produces `SOURCE_CONFLICT`; unrecognized/unsupported IDs produce `UNMAPPED`; missing English or French, unresolved template markers, mismatched source class and failed day resolution fail closed. A `RULE_TEXT_AGREE` result means **the literal heading and trilingual texts agree with the cited rubrical category**, not that a critical Latin-edition collation or local-calendar authority has occurred. All records carry `autoPublish:false`, `changesReader:false`, and a reference to the underlying rule.

**Initial approved screening scopes:** Epiphany, Purification, BVM Rosary and Immaculate Conception (and Annunciation), St Joseph and Joseph the Worker, Christ the King, Simon and Jude, Sacred Heart, Ascension, Easter and Pentecost Temporale, Advent and ordinary Sundays' Trinity Preface, source-identified Lent/Passiontide, Christmas temporale/octave, and green IV-class ferias per annum. The tool does not derive a generic Common Preface from every otherwise unclassified saint, feast name, colour or commemoration; §482 requires observing an applicable proper first. Any holiday special Mass such as Holy Thursday or the Chrism Mass remains outside simple inference.

**Ten-date known baseline:** January 6, February 2, Easter Sunday, and October 11 provide matched Latin headings. The underlying runtime sample still has a raw `Communis` Preface on Pentecost, Our Lady of the Rosary and the Immaculate Conception. The separate reader recovery retrieves the proper Holy Spirit / Marian texts for some source IDs, but that does **not** erase the raw source conflict or justify an app-wide correction without review.

**Full-year execution:** `tests/calendar-1962-full-year-audit.mjs` now calls the reviewer for **every resolved 2026 date**. The workflow publishes `artifacts/calendar-oracle/1962-2026-preface-review.json` as a date-by-date record and exact exception queue, with counts for heading agreement, source conflicts, unmapped rules, incomplete translations and special/failed cases. It explicitly reports `editoriallyCertified:0`, and the corpus continues to distinguish 365 resolver results from only 72 independently checked Calendar checkpoints. The Preface audit has its own independent contract test and is wired into both existing Calendar CI workflows.

**Existing app view:** The Month Ordo disclosure and selected Day summary now use this strict independent reviewer rather than deriving correctness from raw availability. They can show a matched Latin heading, a warning on source contradiction, or no Preface declaration for unresolved cases. A visible Preface claim carries an optional source link to the specific 1960 rubrical clause. The Mass Reader/celebration selector is not altered.
