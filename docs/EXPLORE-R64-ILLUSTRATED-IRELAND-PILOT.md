# Explore R64 — Ireland illustrated pilgrimage pilot

This branch uses Destinationes Ireland as a reference for editorial depth, not as copied text, imagery or an alternative standalone directory.

## Published in the existing Explore Place reader

- Knock Shrine: original apparition and 1879 witness records, original gable and Apparition Chapel, basilica, parish church, museum, devotional practices, historical timeline, official **13–21 August 2026** National Novena and direct custodial sources.
- Lough Derg: distinction between historic Saints Island and present pilgrimage on Station Island, penitential stations, fasting, vigil and boat arrival, official **29 May–15 August 2026** Three Day Pilgrimage (last start 13 August), and selected one-day retreat dates.
- Two fully bilingual illustrated-guide profiles provide 12 sourced explanatory paragraphs, 7 named pilgrim stops, 9 timeline entries and 4 published date-programme notes.
- Each individual section has original custodian URL attribution. The user reads it in the existing Ad Orientem sheet without leaving the app.
- Existing hero photo for Knock is retained from the approved, credited local media collection. **Lough Derg is intentionally image-held** because no independently reviewed rights-cleared local image exists yet.
- No new shrine or pilgrimage counts, no new map pin, no changed curated 20-site menu, no invented Mass times, no Calendar takeover and no Bible Atlas code changes.

## Research, not prematurely published

Three additional documented Irish destinations have individual source and curation records but no live Place pin: Croagh Patrick (Tuam archdiocese), Drogheda's Catholic St Oliver Plunkett shrine (Armagh archdiocese), and Kildare (Catholic Brigidine pilgrimage places distinguished from the presently Church of Ireland cathedral). These candidates need exact physical pins, custodian reviews and, for photographs, source and licence clearance.
