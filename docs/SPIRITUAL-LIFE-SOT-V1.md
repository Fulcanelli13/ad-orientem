The course is deliberately practical. It is not a condensed seminary manual of ascetical and mystical theology.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Sacred Scripture / doctrine owners** control doctrine.
2. **Papal magisterium and canon law** control reparation, indulgences and current discipline.
3. **Adolphe Tanquerey** is the principal traditional synthetic spine and locator map.
4. **St Francis de Sales** is the principal lay-facing control for meditation, adaptation to state of life, dryness and spiritual reading.
5. **St Ignatius** controls general and particular examen.
6. **Dom Chautard** contributes one focused theme: exterior action and apostolate must remain rooted in the interior life.

No single school is presented as the whole Catholic spiritual tradition.

## Curriculum spine

1. **The Interior Life / La vie intérieure**  
   End, perfection, state-of-life adaptation, methods as means, no spiritual scoring.

2. **Living in God's Presence / Vivre en présence de Dieu**  
   Presence of God, recollection, aspirations, ordinary duties.

3. **Mental Prayer: The Foundations / Oraison mentale : les fondements**  
   Nature, affections, resolution, Christ-centred meditation, meditative reading.

4. **The Saint-Sulpice Method / La méthode de Saint-Sulpice**  
   Jesus before the eyes, in the heart and in the hands; explicitly one method among several.

5. **Distractions, Dryness & Perseverance / Distractions, sécheresse & persévérance**  
   Fidelity without treating sensible consolation as a performance metric.

6. **The General Examination / L'examen général**  
   Thanksgiving, light, review, pardon, amendment; distinct from Confession preparation.

7. **The Particular Examination / L'examen particulier**  
   One fault or virtue; focused resolutions; traditional counting is optional and never gamified.

8. **Spiritual Reading / La lecture spirituelle**  
   Read slowly for prayer and conversion, not merely information.

9. **Sanctifying Ordinary Duties / Sanctifier les devoirs ordinaires**  
   Work, family and lawful duties directed to God's glory; broad Catholic doctrine, not movement branding.

10. **A Rule of Life Without Rigidity / Une règle de vie sans rigidité**  
    Stable core, hierarchy of duties, flexibility for work, family, illness and travel.

11. **Prayer Before Action / La prière avant l'action**  
    Chautard's retained contribution: action must flow from prayer and interior union.

12. **Reparation & Indulgences / Réparation & indulgences**  
    Current discipline, historical distinction, no transaction/points model.

13. **Devotion Without Superstition / Dévotion sans superstition**  
    Interior faith and conversion versus magical or automatic object-thinking.

14. **Building a Mature Spiritual Rhythm / Construire un rythme spirituel mûr**  
    Integration, simplification, adaptation, limits of the app.

## Explicit exclusions

The v1 core does **not** productize:

- advanced mystical states or extraordinary phenomena;
- visions or private-revelation discernment;
- priest- or religious-specific ascetical formation;
- a scruples module;
- a generic life planner;
- AI-generated meditations or invented prayers;
- spiritual-performance dashboards, streaks, ranks or holiness scores;
- a retained sin ledger;
- Opus Dei-specific terminology as though it were universal Catholic doctrine.

## Ownership boundaries

Spiritual Life teaches principles and methods.

It does **not** take ownership from:

- **PRAY** — actual prayer texts, Rosary, Morning/Evening Prayer, Adoration, First Friday and devotional execution;
- **Catechism** — doctrine as doctrine;
- **Calendar** — dates, seasons and temporal obligations;
- **Mass** — liturgical execution;
- **Apostolate** — helping, teaching and answering others;
- **Customary/Explore** — local and inherited customs;
- **Confession** — sacramental preparation and the confession workflow.
