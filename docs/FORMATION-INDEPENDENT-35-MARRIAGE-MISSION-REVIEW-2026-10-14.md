**CSE055 and CSE058 have correct existing §1624 citations.** Their exact source link in [the app's CSE source registry](../src/learn/sexual-ethics-data/sources.js) points to McHugh–Callan **revised by Edward P. The older, longer [Gutenberg version](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/35354/35354-h/35354-h.html) treats that subject at **§2618**, while its **§1624** refers to *foolishness*. This numbering difference must be preserved in the evidence ledger. The initially suspected reference error was disproved; **do not modify the existing answers or source references**.
