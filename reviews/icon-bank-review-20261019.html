First: Gospel side vs Epistle side

Compare the illustrations and names. In a traditional high-altar church, Gospel side is on the congregation's left, and Epistle side on the congregation's right, when looking toward the high altar. The physical drawing, the viewing orientation and the state-to-icon assignment must all agree.
