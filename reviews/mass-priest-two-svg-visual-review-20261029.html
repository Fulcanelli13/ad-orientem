Eyes and hands raised

REDESIGN REQUIRED · C0117 / C0263

At C0117 the priest straightens after In spiritu humilitatis and raises his eyes and hands before Veni, sanctificator; at C0263 he does so before the final blessing. Both phases subsequently join the hands. These are distinct source locations, never the Deus, qui humanae mixing of water and wine.

Failures: indistinct upward gaze, high generic Y-position, no clear chasuble anatomy, no credible short raised-and-rejoined phase, optical style unlike the approved full-body priest silhouettes. A new drawing requires independently verified source imagery and separate C0117/C0263 approval.

Primary source: Ritus servandus VII.5 and XII.1.

Hands over the oblations

REDESIGN REQUIRED · C0161

At Hanc igitur the priest holds both hands open, with palms above and facing the Host and chalice, until Per Christum Dominum nostrum, then joins them.

Failures: two open palms cannot be identified, oblations do not separate into recognizable Host and chalice, sideways line suggests one hand rather than simultaneous palms above the gifts, and body/vestment family differs strongly from the active master. No object-labelled substitute or generic altar-action icon should be used.

Primary source: Ritus servandus VIII.4.
