Photographic Atlas · Image Review

Rate every source image from 0–5, decide where it belongs in Ad Orientem, and leave notes for curation. This is a research and curation tool, not a published liturgical guide.

Photographs are from individual credited collections; they do not establish the fittings of another church or what happened at a particular 1962 Mass. Preview thumbnails load from Wikimedia Commons only when online. All inventory records remain available offline.

Compare 4–5 genuinely interchangeable photographs of the same liturgical object. The 112 canonical source records plus 8 staged P0 research leads are a research pool, not a finished shortlist. A context scene, an illustration, a component photograph or the five vestment colours cannot count as five alternative object photographs. Preliminary metadata screening is not an assessment of visual quality.
