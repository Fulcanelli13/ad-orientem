# M6 P0 · EQ06 altar-bell comparison — additional source research

**18 October 2026 | Research leads only | Not publication-approved**

## Why this supplement exists

The master M6 registry currently has 112 unique photographic/source records across 170 canonical objects. Its EQ06 altar bell entry has two independently registered object photographs, `M6-EQ06-ALTAR-BELLS-PHOTO` and `M6-EQ06-ALTAR-BELL-ALTERNATE`. They are a promising initial comparison pair, **not** the minimum four or five viable photographic alternatives.

The new comparative reviewer applies a conservative metadata screen. All five vestment-colour records for VE15 are component coverage, not five alternatives to one photograph. Most pilot objects have only scene/context or unverified legacy sources. The reviewer therefore deliberately shows an incomplete shortlist rather than manufacturing a winner.

## Strong rejection examples for this acquisition pass

- [Meycauayan church altar bells](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meycauayan_Church_altar_bells.jpg) is original Catholic-church subject evidence but **320 × 240** only: too small for a primary atlas image.
- [Carcassonne Église Saint-Vincent trésor 25](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carcassonne_-_%C3%89glise_Saint-Vincent_-_Tr%C3%A9sor_25.jpg) is a fine historical Catholic church-museum artefact, but it must be classified as **historical reference**, not a representative standalone 1962 altar-bell primary image.
- Ordinary sacristy door bells are a different liturgical object; do not conflate them with the bells used at the consecration.
