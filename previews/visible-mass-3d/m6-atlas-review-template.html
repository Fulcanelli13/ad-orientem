Photographic Atlas

One canonical object checklist, authenticated photographic sources and a review queue. This is a research and curation tool, not a published liturgical guide.

Photographs are from individual credited collections; they do not establish the fittings of another church or what happened at a particular 1962 Mass. Preview thumbnails load from Wikimedia Commons only when online. All inventory records remain available offline.

All original G###, VMQ, M5 and existing sanctuary-image owners are referenced, never rewritten. Scores and placement decisions are held in this browser only until exported as JSON; they do not approve publication or modify repository data. Publication remains gated by editorial, photographer-rights and physical-device approval. No photographer or church endorses Ad Orientem.
