Explore the church

Load a real GLB, use Survey to find the nave, then place a starting point and enter Walk mode. Select sacred objects to read the existing Ad Orientem glossary.

Durro is CC BY and for technical testing only. Its sanctuary has not passed the traditional altar review. Large models may take time or exhaust mobile memory.

Movement

Survey: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Set entrance: click a floor point. Walk: WASD/arrows, drag to look; touch buttons on mobile. Escape exits pointer lock.

Liturgical objects

Place hotspot, then click its location on the 3D church. Markers stay separate from the scanned geometry.

Research-only viewer. No church model is bundled or committed. Model rights and altar arrangements require separate approval. Runtime libraries are loaded from the Babylon.js CDN; this prototype is not yet an offline app module.
