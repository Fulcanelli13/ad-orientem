## Reconciliation result

- **170/170** provisional object IDs from the previously proposed 13 church zones captured here; 13 zones.
- 63 **D = direct** canonical G### concept match; 86 **R = related or parent concept only** (must not be described as a completed object-specific definition); 21 **M = missing exact reference** (needs concept-level editorial decision, NOT automatically a new G### identifier).
- **170 distinct object-specific question candidates** (one per object) are listed. Each additionally has three standard card questions: **What is this?** (`{ID}-Q01`), **What is it used for?** (`{ID}-Q02`), **Why does the Church use it?** (`{ID}-Q03`), plus the object-specific question below (`{ID}-Q04`). These are **680 question-to-object associations**, NOT 680 unique source-certified answers. The previous expanded question inventory in the planning conversation contains further variants needing deduplication and migration. No 1962 rubrical or historical claim is approved here.
- Visibility values indicate the intended **navigation treatment**, not actual scene coverage: **V** = intended direct scene object; **Z** = inspectable component/zoom; **S** = separate setting, room, or seasonal setup. They do not assert the object appears in Brompton/Newman imagery or a GLB.
- The current P0 experimental GLB viewer allows 14 selected G IDs and unvalidated manually placed mesh markers. P1 photographic investigation lists 22 glossary subjects with 20 image-candidate subjects; P2/P3 visitor builds use authentic photography, and P3 uses two distinct 360° churches, not walking geometry. No submitted object manifest proves full 170-object physical coverage; **0/170 scene-verified in this crosswalk**.
- Existing eight Mass Formation stages `MF01–MF08`, 32 Guide moments, Sung source blocks and P0 Ultra Master links remain separate canonical owners and must be linked, never re-authored here. Existing readers, including LIVE, are untouched.

## Link-type definitions

- **D:** exact or sufficiently identical dictionary concept exists; verify object instance details before calling it available in the picture.
- **R:** a linked G entry concerns the parent object, ceremony, gesture, or doctrine; it does **not** yet answer the specific physical object question.
- **M:** no accurate extant canonical glossary entry selected; first search lexemes, aliases, source texts and overlapping formation explanations, then decide whether to add a genuine new canonical concept or an object-detail attachment to an existing one.
