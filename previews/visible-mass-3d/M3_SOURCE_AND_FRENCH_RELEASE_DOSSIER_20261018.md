## Source triangulation and safe use

1. **Sacrifice of the Mass:** [Council of Trent, Session XXII, doctrine, chapters I–II](https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/library/twentysecond-session-of-the-council-of-trent-1489) is a primary magisterial witness that the Mass refers to the one sacrifice of Calvary. Use for `VMQ-0001`, `0002`, `0014`; avoid presenting the cross as an *additional* sacrifice. Source-owned English answers remain unchanged.
2. **Altar, retable and high altar:** [Catholic Encyclopedia, Altar (in Liturgy)](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01346a.htm), [Altarpiece](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01356a.htm) and [High Altar](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07346b.htm) distinguish the principal altar, its decorative backdrop and furnishings. Use for `0001`, `0006`, `0052`, `0053`; the precise meaning of *retable/reredos* varies between English/French architectural usage, so the French text should avoid a rigid universal equivalence.
3. **Altar crucifix:** [Altar Crucifix](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01352a.htm) and [Cross and Crucifix in Liturgy](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04533a.htm) establish devotional purpose and historical development. Their early-20th-century disciplinary exceptions are **not automatically 1962 prescriptions**. Use historically for `0013`, `0014`, `0016`; refer any precise requirement to original ritual books.
4. **Altar kiss:** [Catholic Encyclopedia, Kiss — Kissing the Altar](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08663a.htm) discusses symbolism and ritual history, while the [1962 Missale Romanum, Ritus servandus IV](https://www.mercaba.org/LITURGIA/Tridentino/missale-romanum-1962.pdf) explicitly describes the kiss with `Quorum reliquiae hic sunt`. Useful confirmation for `0005`, but the exact rubrical timing, hands and form differences remain the Mass engine's responsibility, not the photographic guide's.
5. **Candlesticks:** [Catholic Encyclopedia, Altar Candlesticks](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01350a.htm) distinguishes furnishings from lit candles. [O'Connell, The Celebration of Mass (1962), general rubrics](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/ITOPL_OCR-layer-only/11a.%20Liturgy/d-The%20Celebration%20of%20Mass-%20O%27Connel%201962_OCR.pdf) explains low/solemn altar furnishings but is **a secondary manual**, not standalone approval for every Missa Cantata, pontifical or Requiem exception. Keep `0017`, `0021` on 1962-collation hold; `0018` has no answer.
6. **Communion rail:** [Catholic Encyclopedia, Altar Rail](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01356c.htm) supports `0056`, `0057` and part of `0059`; the rail does not itself consecrate the sanctuary (`0058`), and old Communion-cloth instructions must be presented as historical practice. No inference about local eligibility or universal posture from a photograph.
7. **Steps and predella:** [Catholic Encyclopedia, Altar Steps](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01356f.htm) documents a distinguishable raised predella and historical counting conventions. Use for `0062`, `0063`, `0065` without declaring a universal modern number of steps; `0064` remains held.
