## Source-boundary reasoning and omitted context

Every noncontiguous Scripture range is a **visible excerpt boundary**. Appending verses without those boundaries would falsely imply continuous Scripture. These are editorial notes and must not be shown as invented Scripture narration.

### 003. The corruption preceding the Flood

**Why these verses:** Makes the necessity of judgment and Noah’s preserved grace intelligible; retain both the judgment and exception.

**What the compact selection omits:** Genesis 6:9–22 remains available as supplementary Noah material; do not treat the abridgment as the complete pre-Flood account.

**Placement and source ownership:** `BEFORE_HS_M03_FIRST_FLOOD_PASSAGE` (current movement `HS-M03`; proposed chapters: R02, R03, R04). Ordinary covenantal sequence. The editor must approve **both** the verse set and any movement insertion. The parallel thematic collection remains available in its original full-source scope.

## Non-negotiable integration gates

1. **Source fidelity:** exact continuous verse units only; show omissions as separate passage breaks, never stitch disjoint verses as one speech or event. Maintain each Catholic edition's own literal source wording and link to complete original chapter.
2. **Chronological/Gospel integrity:** John 13 footwashing must be visibly identified as a separate Gospel witness from Luke 22. The Good Thief (Luke 23) belongs *during* Calvary, not as an event after the selected John 19:30 death; Emmaus (Luke 24) is the Resurrection-day witness, not an event after the later Thomas appearance (John 20:26–29). Matthew 28 Galilean commissioning must remain distinct from Acts 1 Ascension. No default automatic order is approved.
3. **Exile, canon and visionary genre:** Daniel's Catholic additions may not be silently erased from the full chapter. The proposed compact selection excludes the source-editorial text embedded at Crampon Daniel 3:23, but source collation is still required. Revelation 1 is a literary prelude to Revelation 5 and must not be presented as a subsequently dated earthly event.
4. **Covenant structure:** source chapter movement placements are *proposal zones* only. They do not amend approved/working chapter identity, selected Scripture, art, genealogies or existing 37 research movements. Biblical fulfilments, types and parallels stay outside the primary chronological narration.
5. **Acceptance:** review the list episode by episode, approve a reduced set if appropriate, then build a separate preview of the 54-chapter reading with proposed insertions highlighted. Only after parity-tested approval may the canonical source owner be updated. Do not merge the current research branch as an accepted narrative by default.
