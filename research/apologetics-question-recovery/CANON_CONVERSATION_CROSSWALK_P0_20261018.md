## Why these links need an answer audit

- PROT-001 asks what a Protestant is; none of the existing 60 canonical titles directly covers an accurate and historically bounded classification.
- PROT-011 asks precisely about **two versus seven sacraments**: APOL-023 is broader and cannot be presumed to answer the enumeration dispute.
- PROT-013 and PROT-015 must preserve real denominational variations (e.g., infant Baptism, Eucharistic belief), not homogenize all Protestants.
- PROT-014 is about **whether Protestant communities abolished confession**, not merely why Catholics confess to a priest.
- PROT-024 is a compound question involving images, relics and Purgatory; it points to **three** original dossiers, not one invented synthetic paragraph.
- PROT-025 needs an indulgence-specific doctrinal explanation; the Purgatory topic is an index candidate, not proof that the existing answer is adequate.
