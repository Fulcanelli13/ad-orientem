# Monsieur le Chanoine — Lutheran source-first question-level dialogue bank

**18 October 2026 · Editorial research preview in draft PR #1101 · NOT a live dialogue or approved teaching**

## Result

All **25/25 historic LUT-001–LUT-025 question IDs** are preserved from earlier-chat recovery excerpts, not original raw-chat verified. Each question has a separate English source-based answer draft, original editorial French adaptation of the answer, an explicit serious challenge and reply in both languages, two direct question-ID follow-up references (50 outgoing choices), and precise Lutheran confessional document references. The bank has **57 source pointer occurrences across 26 distinct reference IDs** from a **28-entry shared witness manifest**.

The comparison is substantially different from reusing a generic Westminster/Calvinist APOL objection. The relevant proponent is **the historic Lutheran confessional text** (Augsburg Confession; its Apology; Formula of Concord; Smalcald Articles). That text's account of Baptism, real Eucharistic presence, private absolution, Mass, sacraments, saints, works and free will is retained without conflating it with other Protestant confessions.

**All 25 remain unpublished and await original-context review, Catholic doctrinal owner approval, independent French review and source-claim certification.** The historical recovered question phrasing is **0/25 raw-chat transcript independently verified**. The translations and interlocutor challenges are modern editorial adaptations, not a recovered sixteenth-century text or quotation from a Lutheran source. Main Ad Orientem / Formation / LIVE Mass runtime untouched.

## Most important substantive corrections

1. **LUT-008/009:** historic Lutheranism regards Baptism as an effective divine means of grace and commands infant Baptism; do not call it Baptist teaching or child dedication.
2. **LUT-010–012:** Lutherans affirm Christ's actual body and blood in the Supper, reject mere symbolism and distinguish sacramental union from Catholic transubstantiation; avoid imposing the often misleading label **consubstantiation** as though it were their required formula.
3. **LUT-013/014:** historic Lutheran confessions retain private Confession and absolution; do not say Lutherans abolished it. Distinguish Catholic Penance and Catholic Holy Orders.
4. **LUT-015:** Apology XIII discusses **more than an unqualified two-sacrament arithmetic** and identifies absolution among the pertinent rites; distinguish sacrament-definition and rites.
5. **LUT-018:** Augsburg XXIV denies the historic claim that the Mass was wholly abolished. Nevertheless, the Apology and Smalcald Articles sharply dispute the Catholic sacrificial/propitiatory understanding. Do not confuse retention of a name and ceremonial form with doctrinal identity.
6. **LUT-020:** Lutheranism remembers and honours exemplary saints while opposing their invocation. This is not the same as denying their holiness.
7. **LUT-005–007:** do not quote only the 1998 Catholic response or only the 1999 consensus. Preserve the difference between the historic disputed understandings, **1998 Catholic reservations**, and **1999 Official Common Statement and Annex**; all have distinct dates, scope and authority.
8. **LUT-023–024:** differentiate natural/civic free will from saving conversion; distinguish the Formula of Concord's teaching on election from reductive claims about Calvinist double predestination.
9. **LUT-001/025:** **no suitable current APOL/CR owner selected** in the topical crosswalk. The Canon does not get independent ownership. Add a reviewed source-owned historical Lutheran introduction and a Catholic/Lutheran synthesis in the originating Apologetics/Church-History owner before release.

## Navigation model

The Canon will present an individual short answer. A serious objection is a **modern editorial question** clearly distinguished from any authentic Luther quotation. Two related original question IDs can be opened from each response; if a claim is awaiting research, a hold applies to it even when a related topic exists. Source ownership remains with APOL/Catechism/Formation and actual source records. No parallel Canon doctrinal corpus is authorised.
