### LUKE_PARDON — Why did Jesus forgive the paralytic before healing him?

The nominated Bede catena.luke.5.17:2 (#3), **Grade 3 in this historic commentary**, explicitly places remission of sins before bodily healing. **Editorial warning:** Bede links this particular paralysis to sin; this statement must not be generalized into the assertion that every disability results from personal sin.

| 3 | catena.luke.5.17:23 Ambrose (#24) | **3** | Soul's pardon and body's restoration constitute the whole-person healing |
| 4 | catena.luke.5.17:12 Chrysostom (#13) | **3** | Visible bodily healing is given as a sign of invisible authority to forgive sins |

**Finding:** Two direct explanations are retrieved, but the first two messages would mislead if presented as the principal answer.
