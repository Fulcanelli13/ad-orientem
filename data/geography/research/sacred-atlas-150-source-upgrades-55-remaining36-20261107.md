## Significant decisions and nonduplication protections

- **Sancoale, Goa:** St Joseph Vaz annual feast is located by the archdiocese at the **Old Church of Our Lady of Health**. Do not silently duplicate the physical church as a new separate St Joseph Vaz building.
- **Panaji:** Goa's tourism department documents Our Lady of Immaculate Conception as a functioning parish. It does **not** establish an independent shrine title; keep the title request qualified.
- **Edappally:** Kerala Tourism explicitly describes **three churches** within St George's complex. Preserve that qualification before choosing the illustrative point.
- **Skaistkalne:** Latvia's Catholic directory verifies the parish; the exact claimed *basilica* rank needs its own primary support.
- **Sibenik:** the national shrine of Saint Nicholas Tavelić is within **Saint Francis's Church**, not an automatically separate building.
- **Ľutina:** Greek Catholic Prešov Archeparchy documents distinct basilica, second church and hill chapels. This site is **Eastern Catholic**, not Latin-rite by inference.
- **Seosomun, Seoul:** a history museum and Catholic martyr memorial coexist at the same park, with an underground chapel. Avoid duplicating monument, museum and chapel without a proper parent/sibling model.
- **Guibang, Isabela:** provincial government reports that the national shrine church was **rebuilt and dedicated in 2023**. This replaces an old structure, rather than authorizing two contemporaneous church pins.
- **La Naval, Mary Help, Miraculous Medal, Saint Michael, St Jude, St Anthony, Sorrows, Antipolo:** CBCP-ACSP listing provides individual Philippine shrine and parish titles, including *national*, *international*, and *diocesan* rank distinctions. **El Salvador Divine Mercy is archdiocesan, Marilao Divine Mercy national**.
- **Saint Dymphna, Massillon:** the shrine's visitor page locates the shrine *inside Saint Mary's Church*, not a second church.
- **Darien:** the Saint Thérèse Shrine has a different campus address from the adjacent Museum; retain both physical identities as subfeatures.
- **Chicago Saint Jude:** NASPA records a **Claretian National Shrine** and a **separate Dominican Shrine**. Preserve separate custodians and title scopes.
- **Mission San Juan Capistrano:** the Diocese of Orange documents the Mission Basilica parish. Historic Mission buildings and parish basilica need an explicit physical-site reconciliation.
- **Balasar:** the Braga archdiocesan September announcement scheduled dedication of a new Eucharistic Sanctuary for October 2026. A *prospective announcement* is not independently verified completion. Keep final construction/dedication state on hold pending post-event evidence.
- **Bonaria, Cagliari:** the municipality identifies a historic gothic Sanctuary **and** a different Basilica in the same complex. Both may be valid buildings, but one combined precinct pin should not hide the distinction.
- **Coromoto, Guanare:** bishops' conference identifies the national shrine basilica at the apparition site and distinguishes the cathedral that historically held the relic.
- **Letnica, Kosovo:** European Union's July 2026 reporting identifies a currently used Catholic pilgrimage Church of the Assumption. The proposed country's geo-area prerequisite `XK` is still missing.
- **Tà Pao, Trà Kiệu, Tắc Sậy:** Vietnamese diocesan/government pages describe hilltop statues, pilgrimage chapels and churches; connect these with proper subfeature classification instead of indiscriminate duplicate pins.
- **Denton seminary:** the FSSP school's own contact page verifies Our Lady of Guadalupe Seminary. It is **not** automatically a public Mass venue.
