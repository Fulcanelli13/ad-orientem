## Release safeguards

1. Never infer publication approval from SHA-1 source-byte integrity; 64 originals are verified, but zero are newly editor-approved or published by this triage.
2. Preserve exact canonical Place ID, photographer, original SHA-1, licence text, source link, image role, crop notes, and source proof; reject any altered current original.
3. Approval requires a human visual and editorial decision on exact site/subject, architectural or sculptural rights, photo quality, mobile hero framing, derivative file and attribution. Gallery/context subjects are never auto-converted to hero covers.
4. Only after those decisions may a separate verified-media release PR add assets to `assets/approved/place-media` and records to the existing `approved-place-media.v1.json`.
5. No Vercel, no broad Commons download retries, no private credentials or external rights requests.
