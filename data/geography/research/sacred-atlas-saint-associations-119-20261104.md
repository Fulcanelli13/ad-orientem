## Required source and publication gates

Do not infer a Mass rank, feast or declaration of sainthood from a shrine title.

All additional substantive biographies must be authored and approved in the Saints owner, not injected into the Place registry from this name review. The Atlas may display only explicitly validated exact-identity links. Existing six-pilot biographies cannot become public simply because their names occur here.
