# Sacred Atlas — first 56 source-backed shrine dossiers
_Date: 4 November 2026 · Research / editorial review only · Not a publication approval_

This first working set is selected automatically from the **224 shrines with a bilingual sourced synopsis but without an extended Place guide**. Source links below are drawn directly from the existing Ad Orientem source registry, not invented or re-scraped. A citation identifies where a human reviewer should verify a claim; it does **not** mean the short synopsis justifies a longer narrative without consulting the source.

**Editorial requirements for every proposed guide:** preserve the existing canonical Place ID; verify exact institution and title; write 3–5 source-grounded bilingual narrative paragraphs using only corroborated claims; distinguish historic events from devotional traditions, apparitions and relic-attribution claims; document each paragraph's original URL and issuer; avoid inventing saint biographies or feast observances; second-review before marking the existing guide registry `PUBLISHED`. Approved local media may be reused if exactly matched; do not publish the 23 source-byte-verified, editorially unapproved photograph candidates automatically.

**Selection logic:** prioritize existing official/institutional source availability and documented associations (saints, relics, pilgrimages). Scores are research work-order aids, **not** importance, sanctity, credibility or editorial approval rankings. For contested apparitions or reputed relics, verify the exact scope of ecclesiastical recognition and do not present a nihil obstat as a declaration of supernatural authenticity.
