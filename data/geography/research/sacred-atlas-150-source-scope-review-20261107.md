## Important new findings

1. **St Hildegard, Eibingen — wrong building source:** the present Benedictine Abbey of St Hildegard was constructed in 1900–1904; the historic parish pilgrimage church down in Eibingen retains St Hildegard's relics. The official abbey's history expressly distinguishes these. `SG-INV-0173` must not pin the relic shrine to the abbey. Source: https://abtei-st-hildegard.de/die-geschichte-unseres-klosters/
2. **Toronto — title and building must be disambiguated:** St Patrick's Church is the building, while its national shrine is dedicated to **Our Mother of Perpetual Help**, not a national shrine of St Patrick. The Redemptorist province identifies one shared physical site, so `SG-INV-0425` requires a display-title correction and a one-Place/multi-dedication relationship. Source: https://www.redemptorists.ca/shrines/toronto---shrine-of-our-mother-of-perpetual-help
3. **Bharananganam — two nearby churches:** the St Alphonsa Shrine Church is about 200 m from St Mary's parish church; do not merge distinct structures. `SG-INV-0595`. Source: https://alphonsa.org/about/parish
4. **Kew — separate identity and operation fields:** Melbourne's Catholic archdiocese identifies the National Shrine of St Thérèse at the Kew monastery. The monastery itself reports **no Mass until further notice** after flooding, updated 1 Sep 2026; do not infer that the historic shrine title is invalid or that the church is presently open for regular liturgy. `SG-INV-0741`. Sources: https://melbournecatholic.org/news/shrines-to-visit-in-melbourne-for-stay-at-home-pilgrims-this-wyd and https://mtcarmelkew.com.au/
5. **Izmir — temporary worship/visit closure:** official Catholic source states St Polycarp Church is closed during renovation. Do not publish a normal-open status or copied Mass times. `SG-INV-0364`. Source: https://izmirkatolikkilisesi.com/ayin-saatleri/
6. **Saint-Eugène–Sainte-Cécile — both missals:** the parish confirms liturgies under 1962 and 1970 Roman Missals. Not an exclusively 1962 site. `CL-FR-20261105-23`. Source: https://saint-eugene.net/la-liturgie-a-saint-eugene/une-eglise-deux-missels/
7. **Nishizaka — monument with subfeatures:** Japan Tourism Agency identifies museum, monument, plaza and church at the Twenty-Six Martyrs precinct. The monument `SG-INV-0619` needs deliberate parent/sublocation treatment, not site deletion or duplicate recognition based on proximity alone. Source: https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/en/R2-00468.html
8. **Andacollo and Castelpetroso — complex geometry:** custodians distinguish basilica, grotto, temple, chapels and/or pilgrimage route; choose the intended feature before accepting an illustrative point. `SG-INV-0497`, `CL-IT-20261105-07`. Sources: https://virgendeandacollo.cl/ and https://www.santuarioaddolorata.it/
9. **Annaberg — link repair:** original municipal path now 404s, but the municipality publishes St Anna Wallfahrtskirche at Annarotte 9 under https://www.annaberg.gv.at/Redesign/Tourismus/Kirche_Religion/Kirchen/Wallfahrtskirche_Annaberg. `SG-INV-0193`.
10. **Tianjin/Donglu — inaccessible original:** the Paris Missions archive link supplied for Donglu currently resolves to a generic forthcoming-site page rather than the historical named source. `SG-INV-0611` requires a replacement primary reference.
11. **Hawthorn — national title:** St Anthony's Capuchin site confirms the active church, but the particular source does not establish its claimed *national shrine* title; separate authoritative title check. `SG-INV-0740`.
12. **Unzen — wrong map scope:** the cited Japan Tourism page describes Unzen Hell in general, not a specific named Christian Martyrs Memorial geometry. `SG-INV-0621` needs a separate memorial locator.
