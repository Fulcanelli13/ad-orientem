# Ad Orientem — no-cost image-master access exceptions
**Research freeze:** 26 October 2026
**Never change the 22 user-approved hero painting identities. Never pay a reproduction fee.**

## Production objective

Ask custodians for professionally captured, *entire unaltered painting*, front-on, even illumination, true natural colours, ideally >=2,500 px on its short dimension and with unambiguous image-publication permission. A beautiful high-pixel **photograph of the room, a copy, a preliminary study, or a print/poster** does not qualify as that painting's faithful master.

Do not send any correspondence on the user's behalf without explicit authority. These are source and permission requests **prepared but not sent**.

## Four primary acquisition blockers

### 1. Guardian Angel — Pietro da Cortona, 1656, Rome

- **Correct artwork:** Original 225×143 cm canvas at Galleria Nazionale d'Arte Antica, Palazzo Barberini, Rome.
- **Existing free image:** Commons `Cortona_Guardian_Angel_01.jpg`, only 694×1100, 101kB, with no convincing brushwork detail.
- **Critical identity trap:** Several separately catalogued copies/variants exist (e.g. Fondazione Zeri 52220, *Anonimo, Angelo custode* at Santo Stefano del Cacco). Do not substitute copies even if more pixels.
- **Official no-cost enquiry destination:** Barberini Corsini Gallerie Nazionali imagery office `gan-aar.ufficioiconografico@cultura.gov.it`; their institutional policy says permission and fee/waiver are assessed *case by case*. [Official digital reproduction request instructions](https://barberinicorsini.org/en/request-for-images/).
- **Request:** Can a professional, front-on full-frame image of the exact 1656 original be provided and authorized for worldwide inclusion in a traditional Catholic devotional app at no charge, with prescribed credit? Clarify use in a possibly paid app in the future; do not assume a non-commercial-only permission is sufficient. **Any fee means blocked, not payable.**

### 2. Evening Prayer — Pierre-Édouard Frère, *The Prayer*, 1857

- **Exact original:** Wolverhampton Art Gallery, oil on panel 38×30 cm, inventory **OP6**. [Art UK accession witness](https://artuk.org/discover/artworks/the-prayer-19241).
- **Known copy:** 898×1200 compressed Art UK/Commons image. Art UK says its image is for private/research under standard terms; publication requires separate clearance.
- **Official rights request:** `art.gallery@wolverhampton.gov.uk`, [gallery contact page](https://www.wolverhamptonart.org.uk/wolverhampton-art-gallery).
- **Request:** A colour-faithful high-quality photograph of OP6 plus zero-fee permission to reproduce worldwide in a mobile/web devotional app, with appropriate acknowledgement, potentially also distributed via paid premium tier in the future. If they cannot allow this without charge, hold the module photo and keep looking for a separate unrestricted photographic master; no other Frère painting may replace this user's selection.

### 3. Holy Spirit — Jean II Restout, *La Pentecôte*, 1732

- **Exact painting:** Louvre INV 20303. [Official collection record](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010062538).
- **Found photograph:** Commons `Restout_-_La_Pentecôte_01.jpg`, 4037×2444. **Photographically rejected:** broad white reflections, glare and obvious high-ISO noise even in core Mary/Holy Spirit zone. Its Commons PD-Art tag conflicts with photographer copyright metadata CC-BY-SA; rights unresolved.
- **Different professional Louvre image:** cited as © 1992 GrandPalaisRmn (musée du Louvre)/René-Gabriel Ojéda; image rights must be settled separately and no paid provider used.
- **Request route:** Louvre official collections/image-use pathway or a newly independently licensed clear photograph. Ask for gratis worldwide in-app usage and actual image file permission. Do not assume that the Louvre's publicly displayed image can be freely republished.
- **Fallback:** Retain the older complete WGA 1240×730 as contextual reference only; cannot certify this as the desired archival hero master without better quality.

### 4. Historia Salutis — Jean-Léon Gérôme, *Le Siècle d'Auguste : naissance de N.-S. Jésus-Christ*, 1855

- **Correct work:** Musée de Picardie, Amiens, colossal canvas **620×1014 cm**, inventory RF 1983 92 / M.P.D.998.5.1. [Official museum entry](https://www.amiens.fr/Vivre-a-Amiens/Culture-Patrimoine/Etablissements-culturels/Musee-de-Picardie/Collections/XIX/Notices/Notice-Jean-Leon-Gerome-Siecle-d-Auguste).
- **Bad source:** Commons 6000×4000 Nikon MPO file acquired and checksummed but **REJECTED**; it photographs the entire gallery with architectural ceilings, fixtures, other paintings and perspective skew.
- **Important false alternative:** J. Paul Getty Museum 85.PA.226 (1852–54), 38.1×55.2 cm, is a **different preparatory study**. Its open-access high-resolution image cannot replace the user-approved completed painting.
- **Direct museum contact:** `museedepicardie@amiens-metropole.com`. Official Musée de Picardie also lists photographic archive `g.gillmann@amiens-metropole.com` for collection image requests. [Contact and photothèque page](https://www.amiens.fr/Vivre-a-Amiens/Culture-Patrimoine/Etablissements-culturels/Musee-de-Picardie/Infos-pratiques).
- **Request:** A flat, undistorted, colour-correct, **full completed 1855 painting** photo under explicit no-fee worldwide app reuse and attribution terms (including possible monetization). Do not claim the related Getty study or frame-and-gallery views meet original-object requirements.

## Secondary source-quality improvement: St Michael

- Correct original Guido Reni, Santa Maria della Concezione dei Cappuccini in Rome.
- Existing 1,657×2,441 unframed photo has some compression/softness but better hero fidelity than the two new alternatives.
- Sailko 2,112×2,816 photo is severely angled; 5,152×3,864 church installation image has spotlight glare and distracting altar architecture. Reject **photographs only**, never the painting.
- Seek a direct, evenly lit full-frame photograph with appropriate open reuse rights; refuse substitute copies (the Nationalmuseum Sweden version is explicitly *copy after* Reni).

## Authorization, quality and €0 gates

1. **No money:** any fee, bill, subscription, metered cloud, rights service, or non-free licence means remain blocked.
2. **Transparent scope:** worldwide distribution, future possible paid app tiers, offline image redistribution, mobile crops, credit/attribution. Do not claim purely noncommercial use if that may change.
3. **Artist work vs photograph:** identify both separately and record institution inventory, date, photographer copyright, and image rights.
4. **Image fidelity:** lens normal to artwork, even lighting, no glare, accurate colours, complete composition, original file resolution, unchanged full original retained.
5. **Approval:** exact file original SHA-256, native-pixel human painterly review, source-specific release terms, mobile and desktop crops, actual app QA; not automatic.
