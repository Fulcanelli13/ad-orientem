# Historia Salutis — Consolidated main-narrative spine

**Editorial research proposal · 17 October 2026 · Not publication approved**

## Editorial objective

Tell the complete history of salvation as **one readable sequence**. Essential biblical hinges stay inside the continuous story; concise contextual material supports them, while independent stories remain accessible solely through **already existing** character, biblical event, or Scripture references where those destinations can be verified. There is **no** extended Bible experience and no new secondary story catalogue.

This review proposes **37 contiguous editorial movements** based on the theological and narrative turns in the existing 78-chapter workbook. They are **not** 37 locked pages or a target product chapter count. The original v18 contains 78 chapters and 217 source-selection units; none have been altered.

## Key removals and protections

- **Judges:** retain the covenant-cycle under H26 and the transition to Samuel. The individual Deborah, Gideon and Samson lives do not become a compulsory anthology. They are existing-citation stories only.
- **Exile:** preserve Jerusalem's fall, Jeremiah's New Covenant, return and restoration. Daniel, Tobit, Judith and Esther do not create one fabricated historical sequence. Their person/Scripture records persist.
- **Christ's ministry:** retain representative teaching, forgiveness and healing, Peter's confession, the Bread of Life and Lazarus as a bridge to Jerusalem. The same John 6 feeding event occurs once in H56, not twice in H54.
- **The Passion and Easter:** the Last Supper, Gethsemane, trial, Cross, Resurrection and Ascension remain consecutive and explicit. Different Gospel trial and appearance witnesses are never silently blended.
- **The Church:** Pentecost, Saul, Cornelius, Antioch, the Jerusalem Council and Rome remain. Paul at Philippi, the Corinth stop, the Miletus farewell and the shipwreck are *not* mandatory readings.
- **Revelation:** preserve the victorious Lamb, judgment and new creation. Visionary symbols are not to be staged as literal chronological episodes in a post-Paul itinerary.
- **Genealogy:** keep Matthew 1 and Luke 3 as separate complete Gospel witnesses in the existing genealogy navigation; never combine them or label Luke's version as Mary's biological lineage.

## Continuity findings requiring editorial treatment

The consolidation contains **12 explicit continuity checks**; no issue is presumed closed simply because a source citation exists.

| Check | Boundary | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| GS-01 | HS-M03 → HS-M04 | Noah→Abraham requires the descendants through Shem and the passage of generations, not merely an abrupt scene cut. |
| GS-02 | HS-M11 → HS-M12 | The judges' recurring cycle needs an explicit hinge from tribal Israel to Samuel and the request for a king; Deborah and Samson are not essential prerequisite biographies. |
| GS-03 | HS-M12 → HS-M13 | Ruth 4 genealogy should introduce David's line without requiring full Ruth stories or inventing a chronology with Samuel. |
| GS-04 | HS-M15 → HS-M16 | Messianic prophecies in Isaiah are literary witnesses, not one event dated immediately before Samaria's fall. The two kingdoms must stay distinct. |
| GS-05 | HS-M16 → HS-M17 | Daniel, Tobit, Judith and Esther are independent witnesses of fidelity, not chronological stages in one exile. Return under Persia cannot presuppose their order. |
| GS-06 | HS-M17 → HS-M18 | The time between restoration, the Maccabees and the Incarnation must be introduced succinctly; neither invented dates nor forced overlap. |
| GS-07 | HS-M18 → HS-M19 | Matthew 1 and Luke 3 genealogies have distinct order and content: do not read them as a stitched narrative or claim Luke gives Mary's biological descent. |
| GS-08 | HS-M21 → HS-M22 | One representative miracle demonstrating authority, such as forgiveness and healing in Mark 2:1–12, prevents a Kingdom narrative consisting only of sayings; John 6 feeding remains once in HS-M22. |
| GS-09 | HS-M26 → HS-M30 | The source proposal for farewell teaching stops at John 14:14. The Holy Spirit/Paraclete promise in John 14:16–17 or John 16:7–13 is not yet carried and is a crucial bridge to Pentecost. |
| GS-10 | HS-M29 → HS-M30 | Some resurrection appearances occur at different times and in distinct Gospel witnesses; avoid compressing them into a single encounter. |
| GS-11 | HS-M33 → HS-M34 | Acts 28:23–31 is not itself Paul's arrival in Rome. Acts 28:16 explicitly describes the arrival; his mission must not appear as a teleport after Acts 15. |
| GS-12 | HS-M35 → HS-M37 | Revelation's liturgy, conflict, judgment and new creation are symbolic visions, not episodes falsely dated after Paul's imprisonment. Christ/Lamb remain central; visions not literalized. |

### Two source-verified bridge candidates

Both proposed bridges exist in the installed Catholic Public Domain Version and Douay–Rheims witness files. This is a **verse-presence check**, not theological editorial approval or collation against source printings.
