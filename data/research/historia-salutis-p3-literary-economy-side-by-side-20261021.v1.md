## Protected long readings

## Chronology correction incorporated into the main manuscript

On Easter Sunday, John's Magdalene scene now precedes Luke's Emmaus connector, which precedes John's evening Apostles' appearance; the Thomas scene remains eight days later in John 20:26. The distinct Evangelists' words are not artificially combined.
