# Historia Salutis · 93 essential Scripture units — verse-economy editorial review

**17 October 2026 · Draft research, NOT approved for the published app**

This is a proposal for using original Scripture as the direct narrator in **one continuous Salvation History**. Its source owners remain the original v18 master and the Bible witness files. It is not an extended Bible experience, a new narrative source database, or a place for AI paraphrases.

## Quantified source economy after automated verification

The installed CPDV and Douay–Rheims witnesses now pass the original/selected verse-coordinate checks for **all 93 essential units**. The historical Crampon coordinates of Jacob's wrestling and Stephen's martyrdom are handled explicitly rather than forced into the wrong Bible edition.

| Scope | Original verse units | Proposed verse units | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All 93, **nominal** counts (mixes old/source verse systems) | 1,207 | 1,069 | 138 |
| **Like-for-like comparable** units, excluding two historical numbering exceptions | 1,189 | 1,052 | **137 (11.5%)** |

A different verse count for Stephen arises from a source-edition split/merge, *not* an omitted sentence. This measure includes only A/main-narrative source excerpts; no new B context bridges, supplementary reference material or source text has been published. Red Sea H21-03 correctly became longer than its original v18 passage in order to restore the pursuit, so this is **not** optimization by a blind percentage target.

**The protected list is not permission to present 33 long passages without an editorial reason.** It reflects source-integrity safeguards: the full Ten Commandments, Beatitudes, Lord's Prayer, first covenant promises, the Annunciation and Fiat, Eucharistic institution, the Resurrection's key witness and commissioning, and the Lamb/New Creation. The remaining baseline passages require a proper continuous reading before any release approval.

## First literary batch: Creation to Red Sea (movements 1–9)

**Nine of 37 movements (24.3%) have received a first exact-wording desk examination, but have *not* passed a complete oral recitation or theological sign-off.** The following actual-source repairs correct breaks that had survived the earlier coordinate-only audit:

| Manuscript beat | Literary issue | Source-backed correction |
|---|---|---|
| H11-02 · Abraham's covenant | A smoking fire passes between “those divisions,” but the animals and their division were removed; the predicted exile and deliverance were also omitted | **Genesis 15:9–10,13–14** restored before 15:17–18 |
| H13-01 · Isaac offered | The excerpt went straight from the command to Isaac carrying the wood, omitting the journey, preparations and servants | **Genesis 22:1–19** kept as one continuous text |
| H16-03 · Joseph before Pharaoh | Pharaoh declares that Joseph's counsel is good before the reader ever hears the dreams or the counsel | **Genesis 41:25–30,33–36** reinstated in its source order |
| H21-03 · Red Sea | The Egyptians' entry into the sea was not stated before God commanded the return of its waters | **Exodus 14:23** restored after the crossing |

Eight surviving reader-facing contextual notes for this batch now explain Seth/Noah, Babel, Esau/Jacob, Moses, the plagues, cloud/fire guidance and manna in attributed, **visibly non-Scriptural** sentences. The duplicate Ark-building note H07-01 was retired because the original Genesis 6 bridge already gives that information.

This is why the like-for-like reduction is now **11.5% rather than 13.0%**. We should preserve essential causal Scripture even when it makes the text longer. The original 78-chapter workbook is unchanged, and none of the extra verses is yet publication-approved.

## Why the highest-impact cuts differ

**Red Sea — H21-03:** The earlier continuity delta correctly restored **Exodus 14:5–10** (Pharaoh's pursuit) and Moses's assurance at 14:13–14. The new short proposal keeps those before the sea crossing (14:15–16,19–23,26–31). It is intentionally *longer* than the old v18 range in order to be intelligible; the victory hymn of Exodus 15 is still accessible in Scripture, not a required extra story.

**Sinai — H22-02:** Keep Exodus 20:1–17 intact. Selective commandment sampling would replace the revealed law with an editorial summary.

**Lazarus — H58-04:** Proposed five noncontiguous ranges: **John 11:1–7; 14–17; 20–27; 32–35; 38–44**. They preserve the death/delay, Martha's Christological confession, Jesus's grief, and the resurrection sign. The decision to kill Jesus at **John 11:45–53** remains a separately source-verified bridge candidate in the continuity desk audit.

**The Bread of Life — H56-02:** Keep John's strong discourse about his flesh, blood and abiding life at **6:47–59** together. Compress only the preceding crowd's exchange, so the text cannot become a generic statement about generosity or manna.

**Cornelius — H70-01:** Maintain Peter's vision, the express universal-Gospel interpretation, Peter's witness to Christ, the Holy Ghost descending and the actual baptism of Gentiles. Only repetitive travel, introductions and retellings may be removed.

**Apostolic Council — H72-01:** Keep the contested circumcision question, Peter's reply, James's decision and the apostolic decree. The council's action cannot be reduced to a one-sentence modern paraphrase.

## Source-edition safeguards

The original v18 workbook uses historical citation coordinates in some places without enough evidence to assign an edition. Two verse offsets have now been explicitly detected rather than treated as verse deletion:

| Original v18 reference | Relevant historical witness | Proposed CPDV/Douay location | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Genesis 32:23–33 | Crampon 1923, chapter 32 | Genesis 32:22–32 | Crampon starts the Jabbok scene one verse later |
| Acts 7:54–60 | Crampon 1923, chapter 7 | Acts 7:54–59 | Crampon gives separate verse numbers to Stephen's vision/words |

Source-text equivalence and the actual v18 original printed-edition ownership still require certification. The original **Exodus 3:8a** is also a *partial-verse* instruction and must not be imported as an automatically approved complete verse.

## Implementation gates

1. The Bible witness coordinates must exist in both installed CPDV and Douay–Rheims; numbering exceptions use an explicit Crampon mapping.
2. Each discontiguous selection must display an omission marker; it must **never** become one supposedly verbatim uninterrupted Bible paragraph.
3. Cross-Gospel narratives and Old/New Testament parallels remain independently attributable to their biblical books.
4. The main story still needs its short narrative bridges from the existing 67-item B context audit. This 93-unit register does not count them as newly approved Scripture readings.
5. The final publication text requires end-to-end literary reading, theological and source-edition collation, rights verification, and acceptance of all omissions. No user-facing app changes are authorized.
