## Material source findings

**Dublin (eight).** Two directly available chaplaincy pages: [programme](https://www.latinmassdublin.ie/) explicitly says the Sunday and weekday Masses are according to **1962 books**; [times](https://www.latinmassdublin.ie/times) lists Sunday 09:00/10:30, Mon–Fri 08:00, Saturday 09:00. These exactly match eight frozen source rows. Co-located other parishes/oratory are different programmes.

**Mater Ecclesiae (eight).** [Camden diocesan decree, published November 2021](https://catholicstarherald.org/provisions-for-mass-according-to-the-1962-missal/) specifically identifies the **1962 Missal** programme at Mater Ecclesiae and says the existing Mass schedule continues. It is matched to eight dated frozen timetable rows. Direct owner homepage was unavailable in this review. Independent present-day recurrence **not approved**; no inference about unrelated parishes or celebrants.

**South St Paul (seven).** [Parish home](https://www.holytrinitysspmn.org/) explicitly contrasts Paul VI English Masses with **Tridentine Masses of 1962**. [Detailed owner schedule](https://www.holytrinitysspmn.org/schedule) matches four St Augustine Oratory celebrations (Sunday 07:00/10:00/12:00, Sat 08:00) plus three **different-building** Holy Trinity Masses (Tue/Thu 08:00, Fri noon). Do not merge venues.

**Paris (six).** [Diocesan 1962-Missal search](https://dioceseparis.fr/messes-celebrees-selon-le-missel.html) explicitly distinguishes 1962 from a separate **1970 Missal in Latin**. Search-indexed dated 18/25 October 2026 event cards give Saint-Eugène–Sainte-Cécile Sunday 08:15/11:00/19:00, Saint-Roch 09:30/20:00 and Sainte-Odile 09:30 under the **1962 Missal**. Keep source evidence **dated**; its dynamic event listing is not a perpetual schedule warranty.

**German FSSP (73 held with useful programme evidence).** [FSSP Cologne owner statement](https://koeln.petrusbruderschaft.de/wer-sind-wir/) says its community exclusively celebrates the 1962 rubrics; [German Mass-site and time directory](https://petrusbruderschaft.de/pages/wo-wir-sind/deutschland/messorte.php) provides sites/times; [2022 decree](https://petrusbruderschaft.de/pages/themen/dokumente/dekret.php) confirms permission for 1962 books. The **73 are held at exact celebration-level source/venue/time scope**: authorization and a society's general practice do not independently validate every historic member/priest/site/clock time. They remain an identifiable, unusually promising bulk exception queue.

**Park Hills (10 held).** The [diocesan bishop's dated January 2024 letter](https://covdio.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/20240119LetterToParishioners4.pdf) speaks explicitly about 1962 Masses **at temporary St Ann Mission** during a dispute over the original Park Hills building; the [parish's current timetable](https://www.ololparish.org/mass-times/) lists the ten historical times at the current parish. Do not silently treat an old diocesan witness at **another building** as exact time/site evidence for all 10 rows.

**Lyon (eight held).** [FSSP Lyon official schedule](https://www.communicantes.fr/horairescomplets.html) calls Sunday 08:30 **Lyonese rite**, and some archdiocesan feast days also Lyonese. The eight candidate rows are different times, listed without literal Roman-1962 edition. Preserve exceptions and separate school-holiday patterns. Page footer cites a September 2017 version.

**Arlington (eight held).** The [lay society's current page](https://www.latinmassarlington.org/find-tlm/parishes) reports second-Sunday Latin Novus Ordo at St Rita and one-Sunday-per-month replacements at St Anthony Mission, plus a **summer 2026** indult-renewal checkpoint. Do not infer direct diocese permission or Missal edition for all remaining slots.

The Poor Clares Joliet, Hahnville, and St Adelaide sites expressly say **traditional Latin Mass**, but their examined public pages do not directly specify which Missal edition governs all Masses. Georgetown identifies its starred times as *preconciliar Missale Romanum* and separately names specific **first-Friday 1962 events**; this does not prove an unconditional 1962 edition at every historical regular time.
