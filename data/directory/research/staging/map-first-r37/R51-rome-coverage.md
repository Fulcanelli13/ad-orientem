## Safety and source policy

Do not infer an individual priest's Canon, canonical situation or current schedule from directory affiliation. Do not merge separate churches merely because they occupy the same religious property. Do not publish an arbitrary locality-centre pin. Keep SSPX and unclassified affiliations in separate filters and preserve the original source URL for each supported claim.

The crosswalk is in `R51-rome-venue-aliases.json`; the app's Mass reader and calendar are not edited. R49 production dataset and source-only hyperlinks are touched only for existing pins.
