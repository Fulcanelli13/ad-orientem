# San Polo Stations of the Cross — photo licence and attribution

Fourteen paintings by **Giandomenico Tiepolo**, 1747–1749, oil on canvas, Oratory of the Crucifix, Church of San Polo, Venice.

**Photographs:** Didier Descouens (Wikimedia Commons user Archaeodontosaurus). Photographs are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), **not CC0**. The photographer's credit, licence link, and source-file links must accompany any deployed reproduction. The photographs and our resized/recompressed WebP adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Do not apply an NC restriction or silently remove author credit.

**Our modifications:** converted the Commons JPEG previews into WebP, with resize to a maximum 2400-pixel edge and image compression. No painted compositions were redrawn. Check the original Commons file page for the individual image's provenance. **No additional permission or licence fee is required when the CC BY-SA conditions are respected.** These files are retained from the original San Polo archive. The **later fourteen-Stations-only CC BY-SA 4.0 exception is recorded in PR #2144**. They remain absent from the live Stations reader because native-pixel, crop and 320/390px reader QA and final publication sign-off have not been completed.

## Release
The existing fourteen-Stations-only licence exception is explicit in PR #2144 and **does not extend** to other CC BY-SA photographs. Staging still does not approve replacement of previously locked individual paintings or authorize publication. Use this exact derivative set only after native-photo framing/colour checks, 320/390px mobile text-focus QA, complete bilingual attribution and share-alike disclosures, and final release approval. Rauth's copyright-claimed photographs are excluded.
